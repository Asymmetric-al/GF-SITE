20-SECOND VOICE-NOTE SCRIPTS & CAPTION TWINS: THE BRIDGE PROJECT
================================================================
For small groups, families, or Bridge Kids sending an audio greeting alongside
or instead of a written letter (always send audio plus a plain-text caption twin
so low-bandwidth recipients can read immediately if media loads slowly).

0. CLEARANCE CHECK BEFORE RECORDING (DO THIS FIRST):
- Confirm with your Bridge coordinator that voice audio and the delivery channel
  are cleared for your matched household's security tier before recording.
- If voice audio or ordinary email is not cleared for their context, use a short
  plain-text message only through the approved regional coordinator.
- Keep greetings to first names only; never mention restricted cities, workplaces,
  travel dates, or sensitive local politics.

1. SENDER VOICE-NOTE SCRIPT (~20 SECONDS):
"Hi friends, this is [First Name] and our [family / small group]. This week we
read [Scripture passage] at our table and prayed for your congregation, your
health, and your neighbors. We would love to hear one way we can pray for your
household next month whenever your schedule allows. Grace and peace in Christ."

2. RECIPIENT RESPONSE OPTIONS (ACCEPT, ADAPT, OR PAUSE: EQUAL VOICE):
- Accept and reply: "Thank you for praying with us. This month we are reading
  [Scripture passage] and would welcome prayer for [one non-identifying need].
  How can our congregation pray for your household?"
- Adapt pace, language, or format: "Thank you for writing. Short plain-text
  notes in [Language] every two months fit our family better than voice audio
  right now. Would that rhythm work for you too?"
- Slow down or pause without explanation: "Thank you for thinking of us. Our
  household is in a full season right now, so we will pause letters until
  [month / when we write again]. Please keep praying for peace and rest."

3. ROUTING (AFTER CLEARANCE):
- Already matched? Send your cleared letter or voice note plus plain-text twin to
  info@globalfellowship.org (Subject: Bridge Project: Letter or Voice Note; no
  financial gift expected).
- Need a match? Email info@globalfellowship.org (Subject: Bridge Project:
  Match Request). Either household can adapt or pause at any time.
