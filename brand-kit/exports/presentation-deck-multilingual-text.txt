Slide 2: Mission, prayerful vision, and three pillars
Our Official Mission (Official Verbatim Text): We make disciples and plant new churches by empowering local leaders and sending cross-cultural missionaries to pioneer church-planting work among unreached people.
Our Prayerful Vision (Official Verbatim Text): It is our vision to plant a Gospel witness within 30 minutes of everyone.
Our Three Pillars: Church Planting · Local Leadership · Unreached People
- 1. Church Planting: Proclaiming Christ’s death and resurrection, making disciples, baptizing believers, and gathering healthy, multiplying local churches.
- 2. Local Leadership: Empowering believers who already belong to the language, culture, and community to shepherd and multiply the church.
- 3. Unreached People: Communities with little or no access to the Gospel and no self-sustaining local church movement able to reach the wider group.*
*Unreached threshold: 2% or fewer evangelicals and 5% or fewer Christian adherents (Joshua Project, consulted Nov 2026; exact snapshot date pending verification).

Slide 3: Local church planters already belong to the neighborhood
Before founding Global Fellowship in Auburn, California in 1989, Don and Kathy Oates served in Liberia under a local pastor. When the fellowship expanded into North American cross-cultural sending in 2012, that order stayed intact across multilingual near-culture, regional, and North American teams.
In 2025, 584 workers served (reported as “workers mobilized” in our 2025 Annual Report) across 43 countries (“63 by 2030” is a prayerful aspiration).
- [PRE-1989] Liberia: Don and Kathy Oates serve under a local pastor before founding the fellowship.
- [1989] Auburn, CA: Global Fellowship begins uniting churches with indigenous church planters.
- [2012] North American Sending: Expanded into North American cross-cultural sending with local leadership intact.
- [2024] Chiang Mai Regional Hub: Southeast Asia hub opens to strengthen polycentric near-culture sending, local care, and training.
- [2025 SNAPSHOT] 43 Countries · 584 Workers: Reported across Global Fellowship's 2025 Annual Report.

Slide 4: Global coordinates, regional contextualizes, local leads
- 1. Global Coordinates: Stewards financial accountability, church links, and shared tools quietly behind the scenes. (Behind-the-scenes support)
- 2. Regional Contextualizes: Adapts church-planting training, local safety, and Health Above Results care close to the field. (Chiang Mai hub (2024) & regional leads)
- 3. Local Leads: Local planters keep their own church name, edit every story before release, and can decline any visit. (Local elders hold stop authority)

Slide 5: God knows them by name [LIVESTREAM-SAFE COLLECTIVE SPECIMEN]
Where public names or photographs bring real harm to families, local elders gather parents after market hours to read the Psalms in their mother tongue, care for a widowed neighbor, and raise new church shepherds together.
Guarding worker households on livestreamed screens is practical Christian love.
- [PRAYER 01] Quiet courage in Scripture: Ask Christ to open quiet homes for mother-tongue Bible reading and faithful local discipleship.
- [PRAYER 02] Unhurried rest for households: Pray for renewed strength for single workers, married families, and elders carrying heavy pastoral seasons.
- [PRAYER 03] Neighborly mercy: Pray for widows, elderly neighbors, and local believers showing everyday hospitality across the street.

Slide 6: Who decides whether a visit would help? The local church hosting it can say no
- [WORKED HOST STOP-AUTHORITY DECISION (ILLUSTRATIVE)] “Not this spring. Our congregation is grieving and needs space to heal.”: Proposed April visiting team postponed without penalty. The partner church prays privately at home, keeps Bridge letters unhurried, and waits for local elders' invitation. (“Not this year” is always honored without penalty.)
- [YEAR-ROUND CORRESPONDENCE] The Bridge Project: Exchange letters or short group voice notes and pray mutually across cultures (including Bridge Kids). Both households choose their own pace, and no financial gift is expected.
- [IN-PERSON TABLE GATHERING] Faith on the Frontlines: Small living-room or church coffee with a visiting planter, candid Q&A, and mutual prayer with no fundraising pitch.
