BEFORE YOU PUBLISH · FIELD SECURITY & CONSENT CARD

Check both axes first: the stricter tier always governs

Field risk varies independently by Identity and Location. Before sharing any card, slide, email, video, or social post, verify both axes below. A piece may never reveal more than the most restrictive tier on either axis.

Axis Tier 1: Open / Public Tier 2: Partial / Pseudonym Tier 3: Restricted / Withheld
Identity Cleared full public name First names, initials, or approved alias Collective description (for example, Church Planters)
Location Cleared city and/or country Broad region only (for example, Central Asia) No country, city, or dialect clues (use tier-neutral WEEKLY PRAYER · 2026 on card fronts)
Imagery Consented eye-level photo, vetted contextual street photo, OR photo-free Metadata-stripped detail photo OR photo-free Photo-free layout ONLY
Channels Print, email, web, consented social Vetted print, private email, Tier 2 web Encrypted or vetted private prayer & print only (NEVER social or livestream)

A. Consent & Voice Clearance

  • Written consent logged on the standalone 2-page Revocable Consent Form for exact wording, imagery, tier, and each channel (private email vs. encrypted chat selected separately).
  • Local leader reviewed the final headline, caption, and English translation with full veto authority.
  • Recorded vs. livestreamed check passed separately: never project Tier 2/3 personal names, cities, or photos onto a broadcast; public livestreams allow Tier 1 explicit opt-in or Tier 1 anonymous collective prayer only.
  • Calm privacy request printed on Tier 2 and Tier 3 pieces asking supporters not to share online.

B. Mosaic & Hidden-Data Hygiene

  • No hidden file leaks in saved filenames (never include real names or countries), camera GPS data, PDF properties, or image descriptions.
  • No regional or seasonal clues (omit region-coded colors, maps, flags, regional textiles, weather, or hemisphere seasons on restricted cards).
  • No stigmatizing graphics (never use silhouettes, blurred faces, black censor bars, padlocks, or redaction stamps).
  • No military framing (remove adversarial terms such as target, penetrate, conquer, crusade, or underground operatives).

60-Second Emergency Security Step-Down

STEP 01

Step Down or Go Silent

Switch immediately to the Tier 3 photo-free master (collective description, tier-neutral WEEKLY PRAYER header, zero images or cities), or honor a household’s No-Send Option with zero public update.

STEP 02

Retire Direct & Partner Copies

Remove GF-controlled web pages, caches, and email archives immediately; use the private distribution log to ask authorized churches and prayer partners to stop sharing forwarded or printed copies.

STEP 03

Calm Partner Notice

Write calmly to authorized churches and prayer partners asking them to discard earlier versions. Default to silence on full withdrawal; share a stepped-down replacement only if approved.
