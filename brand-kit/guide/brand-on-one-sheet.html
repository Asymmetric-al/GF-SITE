Step 2 · Four checks before anything leaves your desk

  • God acts first: Never claim a financial gift saves or transforms anyone; local believers lead their church.
  • Local stop authority: Local elders edit every story, keep their church name, and can decline any visit.
  • Real or None & dated totals: Zero stock portraits or AI faces; date every figure beside its scope.
  • Health Above Results: A one-sentence rest update or skipping a heavy month is always honored.
