## Practical Licensing, Bundling & Email Fallback Boundary

1. **Deterministic Local Bundling (`brand-kit/tokens/fonts/`):** All SIL OFL 1.1 WOFF2 font files used by HTML/PDF templates are bundled locally alongside `OFL-1.1.txt` and `font-checksums.json`, eliminating unversioned runtime CDN drift when regenerating print or digital assets. Standalone Level 4 Zero-Attribution masters `GF-TPL-16` (English), `GF-TPL-19` (1-page 100% Arabic-only RTL handout), and separate internal reference `GF-TPL-19B` (upright Latin `Noto Sans`) embed neutral SIL OFL 1.1 `Noto` fonts inline as base64 `@font-face` rules so they stay 100% self-contained with zero Global Fellowship brand fonts and zero proprietary operating-system fonts.
2. **Zero-Exception Automated PDF Embedding Gate (`G6`):** `scripts/verify-brand.ts` decompresses every exported PDF stream (all 30 exported PDFs including Level 4 `GF-TPL-16`, `GF-TPL-19`, separate `GF-TPL-19B`, standalone confidential `GF-TPL-18`, and organizational kits `GF-TPL-21` through `GF-TPL-25`), extracts every embedded `/FontName` and `/BaseFont`, and verifies with zero exemptions that **100% of embedded PDF fonts** map to an explicit SIL OFL 1.1 family in the table above with zero proprietary fonts embedded.
3. **HTML Email Client-Resident System Fallbacks (Never Bundled or Embedded):** Because Outlook desktop and several web email clients ignore `@font-face` and `<link>` web fonts, `GF-TPL-08` and `GF-TPL-09` load `Source Serif 4` and `Plus Jakarta Sans` first for supporting clients (Apple Mail, iOS Mail, Hey) and declare client-resident operating-system fallbacks (`Georgia, serif` and `"Helvetica Neue", Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif` at `font-weight: 700`) strictly as CSS fallback names. Global Fellowship never bundles, redistributes, or embeds any proprietary OS font binary.
4. **Google Docs, Google Slides & Office Starters:** `Source Serif 4`, `Plus Jakarta Sans`, and `Noto Sans` are available natively in Google Workspace via `More fonts` with zero local installation, and are pre-wired into all six editable starters in `brand-kit/office/`: `worker-prayer-letter-public-starter.docx`, zero-logo `worker-prayer-letter-restricted-starter.docx`, Finance manual fill-in `donor-receipt-and-redesignation-starter.docx` (all with named `word/styles.xml` and `Georgia`/`Calibri` `altName` fallbacks), zero-attribution English `local-only-meeting-sheet-starter.docx` and native RTL Arabic `local-fellowship-brief-arabic-rtl-starter.docx` (neutral `Noto` fonts with zero Global Fellowship identifiers), and `global-fellowship-deck-starter.pptx` (with slide-master theme fonts, per-script `<a:r>` font runs on Slide 4, embedded Slide 1 street photo `GF-PHO-01`, and native `mailto:` hyperlinks synced verbatim with `scripts/brand-deck-data.ts`).
5. **Self-Hosting & Subsetting (Future Website Phase):** Under SIL OFL 1.1 Section 3, ship unmodified binaries (or rename modified subsets per upstream `OFL-1.1.txt` when Reserved Font Names apply).
6. **100% Original Global Fellowship Vector, Icon & Editorial Assets (`G6` Zero Third-Party Redistribution):**
   - **Original 1.65px Line Icon Set (`scripts/brand-icons.ts`):** All 12 functional UI, channel, and guide icons (`mail`, `bookOpen`, `shield`, `userGroup`, `checkCircle`, `alert`, `info`, `globe`, `fileLedger`, `videoCamera`, `radioBroadcast`, `megaphoneShare`) are **100% original SVG artwork created for Global Fellowship** on a 24x24 grid with rounded caps and joins (zero third-party MIT vector redistribution inside `brand-kit/` exports, while sharing stroke harmony with future Next.js web components).
   - **Original Marks, Editorial & Scrim Plates & Motion:** All SVG marks (`brand-kit/logos/svg/`), Living Aperture arcs, cross-free `architectural-frame-dark.svg`, gallery-grade pure-vector architectural print plates (`brand-kit/patterns/editorial-aperture-script-plate.svg`, `brand-kit/patterns/editorial-borrowed-table-plate.svg`, and `brand-kit/patterns/photo-scrim-stress-proof.svg`), Office starters (`brand-kit/office/`), kinetic Local Edit motion proof (`brand-kit/exports/video/`), and HTML/PDF templates are original Global Fellowship works with zero stock photography or AI-generated people.
