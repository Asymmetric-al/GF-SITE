PUBLIC STATEMENT OF FAITH (8 ARTICLES) & CHURCH SAFETY (2 OF 2)

What Global Fellowship Confesses

Our verbatim 8-article public Statement of Faith (normalized reading copy: wording matches our public archive word for word, with a single typographical double-period cross.. at the end of Article 5 normalized to cross. without altering meaning). Missionaries and staff also affirm a 10-point internal statement (request the full internal text anytime at info@globalfellowship.org).

1. The Scriptures We believe that the writings of both Old and New Testaments were breathed out by God through the Holy Spirit, perfectly and without error, and are the final authority in all matters of faith and practice.
2. The Triune God We believe in one true and living God the maker of heaven and earth, who exists eternally as three distinct persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.
3. Creation and the Fall We believe man was created in the image of God, to glorify God, and enjoy Him forever. But, because of Adam's sin, man lost his innocence and incurred the penalty of spiritual and physical death, so that all human beings are now born with a sinful nature, unable to please God, unable to satisfy God's just requirements, and are therefore separated from God, lost, and unable to save themselves.
4. Jesus Christ We believe Jesus Christ is the only begotten Son of God, who was born of the virgin Mary, lived a life of perfect obedience, suffered under Pontius Pilate, was crucified, died, and was buried; and that on the third day He arose bodily from the tomb, ascended into heaven, and now sits at the right hand of the throne of God.
5. Salvation by Grace Through Faith We believe salvation is in no way a work of man, but a gracious gift of God, received by faith, and made possible only by the substitutionary death of Jesus Christ on the cross.
6. The Church and Ordinances We believe in one church, which is the body of Christ, and includes all those who have been born again by the Holy Spirit. We affirm that believers in Jesus Christ should be baptized, and should participate together in the Lord's Supper to remember His death until He comes.
7. The Return of Christ We believe Jesus Christ is coming again in glory to receive His church to Himself, and to judge the world in righteousness.
8. Resurrection and Eternity We believe in the resurrection of the dead, the eternal punishment of the wicked, and the eternal blessedness of the redeemed.
