How to Write Your First Letter

  1. Introduce your household simply: Share first names, ordinary rhythms from your week, and one joyful moment around your table.
  2. Share one Scripture passage: Name a verse encouraging your home lately and ask specifically how you can pray for their family and local congregation.
  3. Invite mutual prayer: Share one honest prayer request of your own so they can pray for you too. Mutual prayer honors equal dignity in Christ.
  4. Keep translation easy: Short, warm sentences translate far more clearly across languages than idioms or slang.

Healthy Rhythms & Freedom to Pause

  • No rigid reply quota: Ministry seasons, harvest months, illness, and travel affect response times. Slow replies never mean your letter was unwelcome.
  • No fundraising ask: Never send cash, unrequested gifts, or private wire transfers directly to a planter. Keep Bridge strictly relational.
  • Equal freedom to pause: Either your household or the local church planter may pause or close correspondence at any time with full gratitude and no guilt.
  • Questions anytime: Write your Bridge Coordinator at info@globalfellowship.org or call 1 866 984 5622.
Four Everyday Privacy Habits That Guard Your Friend: (1) Keep letters, names, return addresses, and voice notes strictly inside your household or small group. (2) Never post letters, snapshots, or country details on social media or church livestreams. (3) Avoid asking for exact street addresses, local political commentary, or names of new believers. (4) Route all correspondence through your assigned coordinator channel rather than searching for your friend on social platforms.

Writing With Children (Bridge Kids Safety)

Children are warmly welcome to draw a favorite meal, game, or Bible story on the Bridge Kids sheet. A parent or guardian always reviews every drawing, uses first names only, omits home addresses and school names, and sends scans strictly through the assigned coordinator channel.

Rooted in Our Prayerful Vision

“It is our vision to plant a Gospel witness within 30 minutes of everyone.” Every prayer spoken around your dinner table stands beside local believers living out our Three Pillars: Church Planting, Local Leadership, and Unreached People.
