SUPPORT STAFF BRIEF · HOME OFFICE & REGIONAL SERVICE

Support-raised and Home Office roles built to serve local church planters

Local church planters and cross-cultural missionaries cannot focus on discipleship and local leadership if finance, donor care, security, and operations falter behind them. Support staff serve inside our three-level model (Global coordinates. Regional contextualizes. Local leads.) so designated gifts reach local teams accurately and on time.

OFFICIAL VERBATIM TEXT · Our Mission & Three Pillars

“We make disciples and plant new churches by empowering local leaders and sending cross-cultural missionaries to pioneer church-planting work among unreached people.”

Three Pillars: Church Planting · Local Leadership · Unreached People

How Support Staff Serve Behind the Scenes

  • Real coaching, plain timelines: Before you commit, leadership walks honestly through budget goals, training, and realistic timing.
  • Same whole-life care: Support staff receive the exact same Health Above Results care as field workers (singles, couples, and families).
  • Open financial books: Inspect our 2025 Statements of Activity freely at finance@globalfellowship.org before joining.

Where Support Staff Serve Every Week

  • Finance & stewardship: Accurate disbursements, open financial statements, and prompt human donor answers.
  • Church & Bridge engagement: Pairing churches with church planters through The Bridge Project and guiding listening-first visits.
  • Communications & operations: Guarding consent records, regional translations, and dependable low-bandwidth tools.
  • Example support week (illustrative): Mon donor receipting & inquiries · Tue regional transfer review · Thu Bridge letter pairing & security tier check.
Three Illustrative Funding Pathways (Clarified Plainly on Your First Call · Not an Active Job Posting)
1. Home Office & Regional Hub Roles Supported by operations funding (8% admin fee, gifts, rent, WMN; entity/fund scope pending Finance review) or regional hub agreements (including Chiang Mai).
Your next decision: Confirm open budget slots and Meadow Vista, regional hub, or remote hours.
2. Shared Funding Role Combines partial Home Office or regional hub operational funding with a smaller personal ministry-partner team.
Your next decision: Review exact split percentages and transition runway with leadership.
3. Fully Support-Raised Role 100% funded through a personal team of churches and friends who pray and give monthly.
Your next decision: Confirm coaching support, target monthly budget, and church endorsement.
How to Start · Email info@globalfellowship.org (Subject: Operations & Support Roles)

Your first call is a calm fit conversation (no application required) covering four honest questions before you decide whether to apply:

Funding, pay & benefits: Funded, shared, regional-hub, or support-raised terms?
Realistic launch timeline & support gaps: Coaching milestones, monthly budget goals, and what happens if support raising takes longer than planned (exact runway terms confirmed with Mobilization).
Local church role: How your elders stand with you through discernment.
Care & rhythm: Member-care check-ins, rest policies, and Meadow Vista/hub/remote hours.
