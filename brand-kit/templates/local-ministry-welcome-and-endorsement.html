1. You lead ministry in your context

You decide how to proclaim Jesus Christ, disciple believers, plant churches, and serve neighbors in your own language and culture. Global staff coordinate; regional leaders contextualize care and training; local leaders lead.

2. Your editorial voice stays yours

No story, photograph, translation, or prayer request about your ministry is shared without your review. You hold full authority to edit wording, decline photography, postpone a visiting team, or withdraw consent at any time.

3. Whole-life care without quota pressure

Regional leaders stand beside you in prayer, peer friendship, training, and member care for singles, couples, children, and teenagers, honoring Health Above Results rather than demanding monthly victory reports.

4. Mutual accountability in Christ

We serve together on a shared historic evangelical Statement of Faith, honest two-way communication, open financial stewardship, and respect for every church’s local maturity.
