F. Bridge Project Voice-Note Rhythm (20-Second Greeting)

Before recording, confirm your household’s cleared format on your Bridge match card. Keep voice greetings around 20 seconds (matching our companion voice-note script), use first names only, ask one neighborly question, and close with a short spoken prayer.

Recipient pacing: The receiving household holds equal freedom to reply by voice, switch to a short text note, or pause for a season with no guilt or financial expectation.

G. Low-Bandwidth Delivery & File Size Standard

Every standalone 3x Retina mobile card stays well under 150 KB for metered 3G/4G connections, paired 1-to-1 with an unwrapped UTF-8 plain-text twin for Signal, WhatsApp, or LINE when recipients turn off image downloads.
