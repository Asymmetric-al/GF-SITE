When a cross-cultural missionary arrives, the local leader still leads

Global Fellowship unites local church planters and cross-cultural missionaries to proclaim Jesus Christ’s death and resurrection, make disciples, and gather self-governing churches in their mother tongue. Since 1989 we have held one conviction: local believers lead their own church while teammates serve alongside them. In 2025, 584 workers served across 43 countries (2025 Annual Report).
