Listening-first Exposure Trip Agreement

Completed jointly by local hosting leaders and visiting church elders before any trip is announced to a congregation, funds are raised, or airfare is purchased.

Unconditional Local-Host Veto: Local church elders and regional hosts hold full authority to say “not this year,” shorten dates, reduce team size, or postpone a visit at any stage if family capacity, congregation health, or regional safety shifts. Saying no carries no relational penalty and never weakens long-term fellowship or support.

Side A · Local Host & Regional Authority

  • Season & timing: Hosts confirm privately whether welcoming guests strengthens or strains everyday household and congregation life during the proposed window.
  • Team size & daily pace: Hosts set the exact headcount ceiling, lodging expectations, dress and cultural norms, and a sustainable daily rhythm.
  • Teaching & ministry leadership: Local elders lead Scripture teaching, worship, and neighborhood hospitality; guests speak only where explicitly invited.
  • Media & privacy boundaries: Hosts set binding rules before arrival on smartphones, photos, audio recording, names, and post-trip church reports.

Side B · Visiting Church Commitments

  • Arrive as guests and learners: Leave uninvited building projects, outside programs, and short-term savior scripts at home. Listen first and pray faithfully.
  • Self-funded & no host financial burden: Visiting churches cover all airfare, lodging, local transport, and host hospitality expenses so local households carry no financial strain.
  • Refundable travel & graceful pause: Team books refundable or insured tickets and absorbs any host-called postponement calmly and without pressure.
  • Multi-year friendship: Commit to ongoing prayer, Bridge Project correspondence, and durable regional partnership long after returning home.

Step 1 · Elder Examination

Visiting elders examine Global Fellowship’s Sending Church and Elders’ Vetting Packet and speak with Global Engagement before mentioning dates publicly.

Step 2 · Private Host Check

Regional Director consults local elders privately without pressure so “not this year” remains genuinely easy to say.

Step 3 · Written Sign-Off

Both sides complete Page 2 security boundaries and signatures before any team recruitment or flight booking begins.
