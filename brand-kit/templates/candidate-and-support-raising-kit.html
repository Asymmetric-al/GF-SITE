ETHICAL SUPPORT-RAISING & HOME ASSIGNMENT · PAGE 2 OF 2

Build lasting prayer and stewardship friendships without pressure

Support raising is an invitation into long-term fellowship around Christ’s church, never a high-pressure fundraising campaign. Lead with local elders’ work, honest budgets, and respect for every friend’s freedom to say no.

What Ethical Support Raising Looks Like

  • Local leaders stay the active subject: Speak of local elders and congregations with dignity in their own language, never as helpless recipients waiting for rescue.
  • Open numbers before any ask: Share your realistic monthly budget, Global Fellowship’s 501(c)(3) accountability (Candid ID 8043461), and the dated 2025 financial summary upfront.
  • Ask clearly and receive “no” warmly: Invite prayer first and financial partnership second with no guilt, urgency countdowns, or implied obligation.
  • Honor transitions openly: If your assignment changes or ends, coordinate promptly with Finance so recurring partners are notified and invited to choose their next step.

Security Boundaries During Support Raising

  • Protect your tier from Day 1: If preparing for a sensitive or restricted region, never post destination cities, visa pathways, host names, or fundraising gauges on public social media.
  • Use vetted rooms and private cards: Hold small living-room conversations and use approved printed cards or private direct email instead of public crowdfunding pages.
  • Keep giving links matched to your tier: Never use third-party link-shortening services or post restricted worker links in YouTube livestream descriptions.
  • Protect rest and family health: Under Health Above Results, singles and families alike keep Sabbath rest and honest boundaries during pre-field preparation.

Furlough & Home-Assignment Church Visit Checklist

1. Guard household rest first: Block family rest and medical appointments before filling Sunday calendars. One calm shared meal with elders often strengthens partnership far more than ten rushed stage appearances.

2. Confirm livestream rules before Sunday: Ask pastors in advance whether services stream to YouTube or Facebook. For restricted or alias-tier workers, request cameras off during testimonies and keep real surnames, cities, and host ministries off projection slides and printed bulletins.

Sample Home-Assignment Note to a Sending Pastor “Dear Pastor [Name], [I / our family] will be back in [City/State] from [Month] to [Month] for rest, medical care, and visiting the churches who pray with [me / us]. [I / We] would love to sit down with you and the elders over coffee to thank you in person, share what God has been teaching our local fellowship, and hear how [I / we] can pray for your church. Because of our regional privacy guidelines, we keep updates offline and off livestreams, so an in-person meal or small-group evening works especially well.”
