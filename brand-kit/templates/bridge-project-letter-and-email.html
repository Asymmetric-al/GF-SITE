THE BRIDGE PROJECT · PRAYER & LETTERS

Exchange letters and pray with a local church planter

Welcome to The Bridge Project and Bridge Kids. Pair with a local church planter for two-way letters, drawings, or cleared voice notes in Jesus Christ; no financial gift is ever expected. Both households set their own pace and hold equal freedom to pause whenever a season shifts.

Need a match? Write with your name and whether you join individually, as a family, or as a small group (Subject: Bridge Project match request); our coordinator checks regional readiness first and replies personally. If pairing takes extra time while a host church is in a busy or sensitive season, your coordinator will let you know what you can pray for meanwhile.

Already matched? Check your approved format first, then send: Confirm whether text, a child’s drawing photo, or a 20-second voice note is cleared for your match; for restricted households, use private encrypted chat or text-only routing. Send cleared messages to info@globalfellowship.org (Subject: Bridge Project approved format check), or mail physical letters to Global Fellowship, Attn: The Bridge Project, P.O. Box 1, Meadow Vista, CA 95722 (regional staff coordinate translation and privacy care).

Three prompts for your first letter

  1. Introduce yourself or your group: Share first names and an ordinary snapshot from your week (a meal, a study group, or a child’s drawing).
  2. Share Scripture & invite prayer: Name one verse strengthening you lately and ask how you can pray for them this month.
  3. Ask a neighborly question: Ask about a favorite meal, a psalm they return to, or how their congregation gathers.
Writing with kids? Bridge Kids table prompts:
1. Draw your dinner table or favorite outdoor game and snap a quick phone photo.
2. Write one question for a friend in another country.
3. Pray one short sentence together for their household before sending.
Two safety habits before you send:
1. Keep letters private: Never post letters, names, photos, or return addresses on social media or church livestreams.
2. Avoid sensitive questions: Do not ask for street addresses, local politics, or names of new believers in restricted areas.
