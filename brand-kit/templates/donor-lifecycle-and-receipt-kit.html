1. Annual 501(c)(3) Tax-Receipt Cover Letter (January Mailing)

(EIN 68-0214543), a registered 501(c)(3) religious nonprofit organization. Thank you for standing alongside local church planters and cross-cultural coworkers throughout [Year]. God builds his church through faithful local believers, and your open-handed generosity supports practical ministry costs so workers can remain present in their neighborhoods without financial distraction.

IRS Substantiation Notice: Global Fellowship, Inc. confirms that no goods or services were provided in whole or in part in exchange for any contribution listed on this statement, other than intangible religious benefits. Contributions are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by United States law. Please retain this statement for your [Year] tax records.

Open books before and after you give: Review our annual Statements of Activity and public Candid profile (ID 8043461) anytime. If you notice any discrepancy, need a separate designation breakdown, or have stewardship questions, write directly to finance@globalfellowship.org or call 1 866 984 5622.

2. First-Gift & Recurring Confirmation

Send within 48 hours of processed receipt. List exact gift date, amount, cleared public designation label (never restricted cities or family names for Tier 2/3 workers), and 501(c)(3) tax status.

Donor freedom & list privacy: Remind recurring givers they may pause, change amounts, or cancel anytime with no guilt, and confirm Global Fellowship never sells, rents, or trades donor contact lists.

Optional fellowship door: Mention The Bridge Project (letters and mutual prayer with no additional financial ask).

3. Non-Cash Gifts (Stock, DAF & IRA QCD)

Securities transfer: Acknowledge exact share count, company name, and receipt date with no goods or services; donor consults their tax advisor on deductible fair market value.

Donor-Advised Fund (DAF) grant: Issue a warm acknowledgment of receipt rather than a duplicate tax-deduction receipt, because tax deductibility occurred at contribution to the sponsoring DAF institution.

IRA Qualified Charitable Distribution (QCD): Confirm direct custodian transfer, EIN 68-0214543, and IRC 408(d)(8) public-charity qualification with no goods or services.

4. PCI-Safe Failed or Expired Card Care Notice: Never invite or permit a donor to email a credit card number, expiration date, CVV, or bank routing/account number. Write calmly: “Cards expire and banks reissue numbers all the time, so please feel no urgency or guilt. Update your payment method safely by contacting Finance at finance@globalfellowship.org or calling our Home Office line at 1 866 984 5622 during Pacific business hours (confirmed online portal link pending). If your household season has changed, simply reply ‘Please pause my recurring gift’ and we will update it promptly with sincere thanks.”

5. Worker Transition, Fund Closure & Gift Re-Designation Tree

Global Fellowship exercises IRS-required variance power with early written notice, strict household privacy protection, and explicit donor choice. Never silently transfer a recurring card charge onto a different fund without notifying the supporter first.

Situation Recurring Schedule Rule Plain-English Donor Communication
Worker transitions off field support Notify supporters at least 30 days ahead when feasible; set an automatic recurring stop date unless the donor explicitly opts into a new designation. Share only household-cleared details (no private medical or pastoral specifics); explain how any remaining balance covers final transition care or General Field Fund; invite clear choice.
Project fund completes or closes Pause recurring project charges on the closure date and send written notice to every active giver. Report completion honestly, state IRS 501(c)(3) variance power plainly, and offer three equal options: another active project, General Field Fund, or ending recurring giving.
Security step-down (Tier 1 to Tier 2/3) Switch receipt and portal labels immediately to the approved protective designation code; strip cities, surnames, and threat details. Send a calm administrative note via cleared channel confirming giving continues smoothly under the updated protective label.
