Paper Stock & Ink

Letterhead: 100gsm uncoated warm white. Business cards: 350gsm matte uncoated, zero spot UV or metallic foil (plain CMYK or 1-color black).

Envelopes (#10 & DL)

Public mailings place the horizontal lockup (120px / 32mm wide, 24px tall) at top-left. Restricted correspondence uses plain unbranded envelopes with personal return name only.

Office Fallbacks

When SIL OFL webfonts are unavailable on local machines, use Georgia (10.5pt body) and Calibri (Bold labels) without changing margins.

Travel Safety Reminder: Workers crossing borders into Tier 2 or Tier 3 jurisdictions carry the Restricted Travel Card only (zero Global Fellowship name, cross mark, or California postal address). Confidential pastoral check-ins ship in a strictly separate file so print vendors never receive care records.
