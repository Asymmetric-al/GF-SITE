Health Above Results: A Seasonal Check-In

Designed for calm tea-table conversations with single workers, married couples, parents, children, teenagers, and regional caregivers alike. You never need to reach crisis before asking for a slower month, pastoral counsel, or a confidential referral.

1. Soul & Walk With Christ

Where have you sensed God’s nearness lately, and where has prayer or Scripture felt dry, rushed, or heavy?

2. Rest, Body & Emotional Load

How are you sleeping, resting, and carrying grief, transition, or isolation? What would one honest week of relief look like right now?

3. Household, Friendships & Belonging

For singles: who knows how you really are each week? For couples and parents: how are your marriage, children, and teenagers holding up?

4. Ministry Rhythm & Local Team Health

Are expectations clear and sustainable, or is unspoken pressure pushing visible results ahead of whole-life health?

5. Financial & Practical Stability

Are there administrative, support-team, visa, or legal pressures that our regional or Home Office team can help lift?

Planned & Extended Rest

Use the one-sentence Rest Option in the Worker Prayer-Letter Kit with your regional lead’s blessing. If rest extends beyond one month, your Regional Director coordinates local handoffs and supporter cover so rest never stalls care.

Confidential Counseling Referral

Regional care leads connect workers to vetted counselors in their working language without sharing clinical notes with fundraising or public communications staff.

Singles, Kids & Teens

Schedule dedicated check-ins for single workers and for missionary children and teenagers navigating school, grief, or cross-cultural transitions.

Privacy, Safeguarding & Outside Escalation: Member-care conversations stay strictly private inside regional care files and never appear in donor newsletters or public reports. To raise a concern or request a counseling referral outside your direct supervisor’s line, contact the International Member Care Facilitator directly through your private regional care directory.
