Draw a picture and pray for a friend across the world

Families and children around the world follow Jesus together. Draw something from your week below, write one friendly question, and pray together around your dinner table. After we send your drawing, our friend’s family may write back later, switch to a short text note, or pause whenever they need to. No financial gift is ever expected.
