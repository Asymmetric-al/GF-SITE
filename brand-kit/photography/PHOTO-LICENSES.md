Zero identifiable faces, zero national flags, zero religious or folk amulets/shrines, zero commercial storefront signs or readable vehicle plates, **100% sRGB ICC color profile (`0xE2`)** with one unified warm-neutral documentary house grade (`91%` chroma restraint, `1.03` micro-contrast, subtle warm paper balance), and **zero EXIF/APP1/IPTC/COM metadata** (`0` metadata segments verified automatically by `scripts/verify-brand.ts` in `G3`/`G6`).

## 2. Three-Path Photography Sourcing & Tier Boundary Rule

1. **Path A: Public Contextual Street Photography (Tier 1 Public Channels Only):** Use only vetted non-identifying frames from `brand-kit/photography/` (`GF-PHO-01` through `GF-PHO-06`) that contain zero recognizable faces, zero national flags, zero religious or folk symbols, zero commercial signage or vehicle plates, sRGB color normalization, and zero EXIF metadata. Always pair with visible-only alt text, photographer attribution, license URL, and the honest contextual disclaimer (`"contextual street photography, not a Global Fellowship congregation"`).
2. **Path B: Commissioned Global Fellowship Field Photography (Tier 1 Public & Tier 2 Vetted Channels):** Any photograph depicting real Global Fellowship workers, local elders, or partner congregations requires a signed, revocable, channel-scoped `GF-TPL-15` Story & Photography Consent Record in the private consent archive, EXIF/GPS stripping prior to upload, and zero mosaic clues (`G3`).
3. **Path C: Photo-Free Print, Restricted, and Zero-Attribution Fallback (Tier 2/3 & Level 4):** Restricted prayer cards (`GF-TPL-01`), Tier 3 mobile notes & plain-text twins (`GF-TPL-14`: `exports/mobile-prayer-note-twin.txt`, `-thai-twin.txt`, `-arabic-twin.txt`), Tier 1 anonymous collective livestream prayer slides (`GF-TPL-10` Slide 5; zero Tier 2/3 personal details, each broadcast requires service clearance), Level 4 zero-attribution local handouts (`GF-TPL-16`, `GF-TPL-19`), and office copier bulletins (`GF-TPL-17`) remain **100% photo-free** and rely on clean typography or cross-free architectural framing (`patterns/`).
