# Faith on the Frontlines Gatherings

**Faith on the Frontlines** gathers small groups of friends, neighbors, and church members inside a host's living room or church fellowship hall to meet a visiting church planter face to face[^source-synthesis].

## What Happens at a Gathering

- **Warm hospitality:** A simple meal or coffee hosted by a local household or congregation.
- **Firsthand witness:** A visiting church planter shares honest stories of God's work, ordinary daily ministry, and real prayer burdens in their own words.
- **Reciprocal conversation:** Open Q&A and mutual prayer (including inviting the visiting planter to share a question or encouragement for the host church's own neighborhood when the planter wishes).
- **Clear, pressure-free invitation:** Guests receive a simple prayer card and clear, non-coercive ways to stay connected through prayer, The Bridge Project, or financial partnership (`"relational warmth, sound judgment, and persistence without pressure"`)[^source-synthesis].
