# Church Planting and Local Leadership Model

Global Fellowship's unchanged official mission guides every church-planting partnership: _"We make disciples and plant new churches by empowering local leaders and sending cross-cultural missionaries to pioneer church-planting work among unreached people."_[^foundations]

## How the Three Connected Levels Work Together

> `"Global coordinates. Regional contextualizes. Local leads."`[^source-synthesis]

- **Local church planters lead in context:** They identify community needs, speak the heart language, choose culturally faithful methods, shepherd new believers, and retain ownership of their ministry identity (`integration without assimilation`).
- **Regional teams contextualize and care:** Regional directors and care facilitators (such as the Southeast Asia hub in Chiang Mai opened in 2024) know local customs, walk personally beside church planters, connect peer leaders across borders, and remain accountable to Global Fellowship's Executive Directors and Board.
- **Global staff coordinate and remove barriers:** Home Office and international teams provide donor care, financial stewardship, equipping links, storytelling help, and church-partnership coordination so frontline leaders stay free for ministry.

## Near-Culture and Cross-Cultural Missionaries in One Family

- **Who counts as a missionary:** Anyone sent to establish a Christ-centered church where no self-sustaining Gospel community exists, regardless of passport. Most Global Fellowship workers come from the regions where they serve[^source-synthesis].
- **Cross-cultural posture:** Cross-cultural workers enter a new language and culture with humility, serving alongside and under local leaders rather than importing headquarters control.
- **From proclamation to mature, sending churches:** The intended spiritual movement runs from Gospel proclamation and patient discipleship to baptism, gathered local churches, mature indigenous elders, local pastoral support, and new churches sending new planters. Businesses, youth centers, resource centers, and mercy projects serve strictly as tools connected to discipleship and church formation.
- **Honest timelines:** Church maturation follows God's timing rather than an arbitrary funding cutoff; continuing missionary partnership and growing local church ownership can coexist faithfully across seasons[^source-synthesis].
