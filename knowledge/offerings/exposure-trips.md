# Listening-First Exposure Trips

Global Fellowship **Exposure Trips** exist to cultivate lasting relationships between sending churches and local church planters, never as short-term project tourism or outside construction spectacles[^source-synthesis].

## Local Hosts Lead Every Trip Decision

Under Global Fellowship's Global Engagement model, **local church planters and regional leaders own the invitation**[^source-synthesis]:

- **Local hosts decide** whether a visit serves their current ministry season, what the purpose is, how many guests can be welcomed without strain, what visiting teams do, and what photography or social-media boundaries apply.
- **"Later" is always a respected answer.** A local leader declining or postponing a trip to protect congregation health or field safety is treated as wise leadership, never a disappointment.

## What Visitors Actually Come to Do

1. **Listen and learn** from local believers about their neighborhood, faith, and perseverance.
2. **Pray and encourage** church planters and families personally, bearing witness that they are remembered by the wider body of Christ.
3. **Serve practically as invited** by the local host, leaving outside agendas at home.
4. **Return committed for the long term** to pray, advocate, correspond, and walk with the regional fellowship for years after the flight home.
