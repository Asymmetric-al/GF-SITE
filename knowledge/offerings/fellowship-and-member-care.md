# Global Fellowship Community and Member Care

Pioneer church planting carries deep relational, emotional, spiritual, and physical cost. Global Fellowship holds to **Health Above Results**: protecting the whole person whose life carries the ministry rather than burning workers out for short-term reports[^source-synthesis].

## How Member Care Operates

- **Regionally led, culturally native care:** Care is led closest to the worker by regional teams who understand their language, daily pressures, and local culture. International staff equip caregivers, resource specialist referrals, and make sure caregivers themselves receive care[^source-synthesis].
- **Whole-life scope:** Attends to spiritual, emotional, mental, physical, financial, family, and relational health.
- **Explicitly inclusive of every household shape:** Care serves **single workers** alongside married couples, children, and teenagers, never treating family care as exclusively for married parents.
- **Privacy, referrals, and safe escalation:** Member care upholds personal privacy, connects workers with qualified outside counselors or specialists when needed, maintains clear safeguarding responsibilities, and provides a trusted route to raise concerns outside a worker's direct leader whenever necessary[^source-synthesis].

## Care and Personal Agency Work Together

Global Fellowship's orientation distinguishes clearly between organizational care and personal stewardship: GF listens, prays, stays in regular contact, and coordinates resources, while workers take honest initiative for language study, daily rest rhythms, financial counsel, and asking for help before crisis hits. _(Boundary note: Public brand templates supply only the calm tonal shell for member care; actual counseling and care practice belongs to trained regional and international care leads.)_
