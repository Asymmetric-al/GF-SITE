## 2. Financial Partnership & Giving Designations

Global Fellowship, Inc. is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit (`EIN 68-0214543`) supporting church planting across three clear giving pathways[^source-synthesis]:

1. **Missionary Support (Near-Culture & Cross-Cultural):** Monthly or one-time support designated for specific church planters or support-raised staff, freeing them to devote full time to Gospel ministry where no established congregation yet exists to support a pastor.
2. **Field Projects:** Designated gifts for practical ministry tools (such as Bibles, pastoral training, church-plant gathering spaces, humanitarian care, or crisis medical relief), **always explained in direct connection to the local leaders and churches they serve**.
3. **Where Most Needed & Home Office Operations:** Unrestricted or Home Office gifts (plus optional donor fee-coverage gifts) that sustain administration, donor care, payment processing, and urgent regional needs.
