# The Bridge Project and Bridge Kids

**The Bridge Project** is one of Global Fellowship's clearest visible proofs that fellowship is reciprocal rather than transactional[^source-synthesis].

## What It Is

- A structured friendship pairing individuals, households, small groups, or church classes with a local church planter for ongoing two-way correspondence, mutual prayer, and encouragement.
- **Bridge Kids:** A parent-led family extension helping children learn to pray for, draw pictures for, and correspond warmly with a church planter's household.

## The Unmistakable Rule: Zero Financial Expectation

- **Financial support is explicitly neither expected nor required.** Participants are never placed into a disguised fundraising funnel; the friendship and mutual prayer are the entire point[^source-synthesis].
- **Local opt-out and pause sovereignty:** Local church planters and households hold the exact same right to accept, pause, or step back from correspondence whenever their family or ministry season shifts, with zero effect on fellowship, pastoral care, or standing.
- Both households give and receive: supporters pray for and encourage a church planter, and the church planter prays for, teaches, and encourages the participating household in return.

## Practical Safety & Correspondence Discipline

Before writing a first letter, every Bridge Project participant receives a concise safety guide aligned with [Field Security Policy](../policies/field-security-and-disclosure.md)[^security]:

- Follow the worker's approved security tier on names, cities, and channel rules.
- Never post letters, return addresses, photos, or worker names on social media or public church livestreams.
- Ask warm human questions (family life, favorite foods, how to pray this month) rather than sensitive political or operational questions.
