## Block `COPY-CHURCH-01`: Pastor & Missions Committee Introduction Letter

**Subject:** `Introduction from Global Fellowship (local-led church planting)`  
**Body:**  
Dear Pastor [Name],

Many churches carry a real longing to see healthy local churches planted among unreached people, yet feel weary of short-term programs that leave local leaders on the sidelines.

Global Fellowship is an interdenominational evangelical fellowship founded in 1989 after Don and Kathy Oates served under a local church leader in Liberia. Centered on the good news of Jesus Christ's death and resurrection, we gather believers into locally led congregations built around Scripture, baptism, and the Lord's Supper. Our mission is unchanged: _"We make disciples and plant new churches by empowering local leaders and sending cross-cultural missionaries to pioneer church-planting work among unreached people."_

We stand on a clear historic evangelical Statement of Faith, publish our annual financial statements openly (including a plain explanation of why we are not currently an ECFA member), and partner with churches around multi-year relationships: praying faithfully, connecting small groups with church planters through The Bridge Project (with no financial ask), hosting a visiting church planter, or planning a listening-first Exposure Trip shaped by local hosts.

If it would serve your elders or missions committee, I would be glad to send our two-page Church Partnership Brief or share twenty minutes over coffee or a call.

In Christ,  
[Name], Global Fellowship

## Block `COPY-CHURCH-02`: 2-Minute Sunday Pulpit Announcement Script (Livestream-Safe)

> "Good morning, church. Before we pray together, picture a small living room this morning where neighbors are opening the Gospel of Luke in their own mother tongue for the first time, led by a pastor from their own community. Through Global Fellowship, our church gets to stand alongside brothers and sisters like them. Founded in 1989 and sending cross-cultural missionaries since 2012, Global Fellowship brings indigenous church planters and cross-cultural teammates together in one family in Jesus Christ, where local believers lead the work in their own neighborhoods and missionaries serve alongside and under them.
>
> You will find a prayer insert in your bulletin today. Because some of our brothers and sisters serve in places where public names could bring real harm, we do not put every name or city on our livestream. God knows them by name, and we can pray faithfully at home. One simple way your household or small group can begin this week is The Bridge Project: you exchange letters and pray with a local church planter, with no financial gift expected. Let us pray together right now."

_(Optional elder Q&A speaker footnote: Roughly 3.6 billion people live in unreached people groups, where 2% or fewer are evangelical Christians and 5% or fewer are Christian adherents; Joshua Project, consulted November 2026; exact live snapshot date pending `OVR-19`.)_

## Block `COPY-CHURCH-03`: Listening-First Exposure Trips (Two-Sided Host & Church Framing)

**Headline:** **Who decides whether a visit would help? The local church hosting it can say no.**  
**Body:**  
An Exposure Trip with Global Fellowship is built for long-term church friendship, never short-term tourism[^exposure-trips]. Before any plane ticket is purchased, the local church planter and regional team decide:

- Whether a visit strengthens or strains the local church in this season (`"not this year"` is always a respected answer).
- What the clear purpose of the visit will be, how many guests can come, and what photography or sharing boundaries protect local families.

Your team arrives to listen, learn from local believers, share meals, pray together, and return home ready to walk alongside that regional church-planting family for years.

## Block `COPY-CHURCH-04`: Faith on the Frontlines Living-Room Invite & No-Pressure Giving Script

**Invitation Card Copy (Greater Sacramento / Inquiry-Based; schedule `OVR-08`, program title review `OVR-10`):**  
**Headline:** **An evening at the table with a visiting church planter.**  
**Body:**  
Join neighbors on [Date] at [Time] in [Host Home / Church Fellowship Hall] for coffee, dessert, and candid conversation with a visiting church planter serving alongside Global Fellowship. Hear firsthand how God is gathering local churches in Jesus Christ, ask real questions, and pray together. No financial gift is expected. _(Please confirm phone, photo, and recording boundaries with the host before beginning so we protect our guest's family and congregation.)_

**Host Spoken Transition (No-Pressure Giving & Next-Step Script):**

> "Thank you for welcoming our friend tonight. If you want to stay connected, three simple options sit on the table: take a prayer card home, sign up for The Bridge Project to exchange letters with no financial ask, or read our open financial statements to partner financially. Whatever you choose, thank you for praying with us."
