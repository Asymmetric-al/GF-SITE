Many churches carry a real longing to see healthy local churches planted among unreached people, yet feel weary of short-term programs that leave local leaders on the sidelines.

Global Fellowship is an interdenominational evangelical fellowship founded in 1989 after Don and Kathy Oates served under a local church leader in Liberia. Centered on the good news of Jesus Christ's death and resurrection, we gather believers into locally led congregations built around Scripture, baptism, and the Lord's Supper. If it would serve your elders or missions committee, I would be glad to send our two-page Church Partnership Brief or share twenty minutes over coffee or a call.

In Christ,  
[Name], Global Fellowship

## 2. Sending Church & Elders' Vetting Brief (`COPY-CHURCH-VET-01`)

Give a pastor or missions committee every hard doctrinal, governance, financial, and field-security answer in one calm 10-minute packet (`GF-TPL-22`)[^proof-2025]:

1. **What do you believe?** Historic evangelical Christian faith centered on Scripture, the Triune God, salvation by grace through faith in Jesus Christ, and the calling of the local church. The full 8-article public Statement of Faith is included in `GF-TPL-22`, and leadership walks elders through our internal 10-point staff affirmation during vetting conversations.
2. **Who leads the work on the ground?** Local believers lead their own churches (`Global coordinates. Regional contextualizes. Local leads.`). Cross-cultural missionaries arrive as learners and coworkers alongside and under local elders, never as outside directors imposing Western church forms.
3. **Who oversees Global Fellowship legally and financially?** Global Fellowship, Inc. is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit founded in Auburn, California in 1989 (`EIN 68-0214543`, mailing address Meadow Vista, CA), led by Founder & President Don Oates, Co-Executive Directors Jeremy Beaumont and Conrad O'Brien, and a governing board chaired by Eric Bergen.
4. **Can we review your financial statements?** Yes. Our 2025 Statement of Activity (`$5.82M` consolidated received / `$5.73M` distributed, including `$2.08M` received / `$2.13M` distributed directly through Global Fellowship, Inc.), 2025 Statement of Financial Position, and Candid profile (`8043461`) are published on our website. We maintain Board oversight and designated-fund tracking, and our Finance team welcomes direct questions from pastors and treasurers at `finance@globalfellowship.org`[^proof-2025].
5. **How do you care for missionaries we send?** Member care is led regionally (including our Southeast Asia hub opened in Chiang Mai in 2024) covering spiritual, emotional, physical, financial, and family health for both singles and families. We hold **Health Above Results**: rest seasons and honest hardship never disqualify a worker.
6. **How do you protect sensitive workers during Sunday services and livestreams?** Two independent permissions govern church services: offline recordings vs. public YouTube/Facebook livestreams. Tier 3 workers and households are **never** recorded (even offline) or livestreamed; Tier 2 speakers appear in offline church recordings only with separate prior written opt-in, and Tier 2/3 worker names, cities, and photos are **never** shown on public livestreams[^security].

Before we pray together, picture a small living room this morning where neighbors are opening the Gospel of Luke in their own mother tongue for the first time, led by a pastor from their own community. Through Global Fellowship, our church gets to stand alongside brothers and sisters like them. ## 4. Listening-First Exposure Trips & Two-Sided Host Agreement (`COPY-CHURCH-03` & `COPY-TRIP-AGREE-01`)

### Public Overview (`COPY-CHURCH-03`)

**Headline:** **Who decides whether a visit would help? The local church hosting it can say no.**  
An Exposure Trip with Global Fellowship is built for long-term church friendship, never short-term tourism[^exposure-trips]. Before any plane ticket is purchased, the local church planter and regional team decide whether a visit strengthens or strains the local church (`"not this year"` is always respected), what purpose it serves, how many guests can come, and what photography or sharing boundaries protect local families. Your team arrives to listen, share meals, pray together, and return home ready to walk alongside that regional fellowship for years.

### Two-Sided Host Agreement (`COPY-TRIP-AGREE-01`, Paired With `GF-TPL-23`)

No church announces a trip, raises trip funds, or books airfare until both Side A (Local Host & Regional Director) and Side B (Visiting Church Leadership) sign `GF-TPL-23`:

- **Side A (Local Host & Regional Leadership Authority):**
  1. **Unconditional right to say "not this year":** Local elders and regional hosts may decline, shorten, postpone, or cancel any visit at any stage if family strain, congregation health, or security conditions shift, with zero relational penalty.
  2. **Schedule, group size, and focus:** Local hosts set maximum team size, visit length, daily pace, cultural norms, and any speaking expectations.
  3. **Media and camera boundaries:** Local hosts set clear written rules before arrival on smartphones, photos, audio recording, names, and post-trip sharing.
- **Side B (Visiting Church Leadership Commitments):**
  1. **Arrive as guests and learners:** Leave uninvited building projects, outside curriculum, and short-term savior narratives at home.
  2. **Self-funded & zero burden on local hospitality:** Visiting teams cover their own travel, lodging, meals, and host-related hospitality costs.
  3. **Strict post-trip security discipline:** Honor all photo, name, city, and livestream boundaries on site and at home, with zero social tagging or geo-located photos in sensitive areas[^security].
  4. **Cancellation & travel-insurance readiness:** Purchase refundable or insurable travel and absorb cancellation costs gracefully if local hosts pause a visit.
  5. **Long-term friendship beyond the flight home:** Commit to ongoing prayer, Bridge Project correspondence, or multi-year church partnership.

**Headline:** **An evening at the table with a visiting church planter.**

Hear firsthand how God is gathering local churches in Jesus Christ, ask real questions, and pray together. No financial gift is expected. _(Please confirm phone, photo, and recording boundaries with the host before beginning so we protect our guest's family and congregation.)_

**Host Spoken Transition (No-Pressure Next Steps):**

> "Thank you for welcoming our friend tonight. If you want to stay connected, three simple options sit on the table: take a prayer card home, sign up for The Bridge Project to exchange letters with no financial ask, or read our open financial statements to partner financially. Whatever you choose, thank you for praying with us."

### Live-Room & Q&A Security Run-of-Show (`COPY-FOTF-RUN-01`)

- **48-hour pre-check:** Confirm the speaker's cleared disclosure tier with the Global Engagement Coordinator. If ANY speaker or story in the room is Tier 2 or Tier 3, default to **turning off all livestreams, video cameras, voice recorders, and public photography** unless a Regional Director confirms an explicit offline-only Tier 2 exception in advance (public livestreams are never permitted for Tier 2/3 personal details). Use first names only on name tags when sensitive workers attend.
- **60-75 minute calm room flow (aligned with `GF-TPL-07`):**
  1. **Min 0-5 (Welcome & Safety Cue):** Welcome guests and give a warm 15-second device reminder (_"Because some friends we pray for serve where public names could bring risk, let us keep phones tucked away tonight"_).
  2. **Min 5-25 (Firsthand Story & Scripture):** Visiting planter shares in their own voice.
  3. **Min 25-45 (Open Q&A with Host Safety Guard):** Redirect sensitive city or travel questions gracefully:
     > _"That is a thoughtful question. Because neighbors and house churches there still face real scrutiny, we keep exact cities and travel logistics offline, but I can gladly share what everyday discipleship looks like around the kitchen table."_
  4. **Min 45-55 (Guided Prayer Together):** Pray specifically for local elders, endurance, households, and mother-tongue Scripture.
  5. **Min 55-60 (Two Equal Doors):** Offer The Bridge Project (zero financial ask) beside optional giving/partnership info on a quiet side table.
