# Usable Copy Library: Donor Lifecycle, Tax Receipts, and Gift Re-Designation

This playbook equips Global Fellowship's Finance and Donor Care staff with complete, plain-spoken, legally careful copy across the entire giving lifecycle. Every template honors **God-and-local-church agency discipline** (God builds the church, local believers lead, gifts provide practical support rather than purchasing spiritual outcomes) and respects open Finance confirmation items (`OVR-01`, `OVR-02`, `OVR-03`, `OVR-16`) without overclaiming[^proof-2025][^open-verification][^editorial].

**Home-Office-to-Supporter Tier 2/3 Receipt Rule:** Unlike field-worker prayer letters (which strip GF logos on Tier 2/3), official **Home Office IRS 501(c)(3) receipts and Finance re-designation emails** retain Global Fellowship, Inc.'s legal name, `EIN 68-0214543`, and Meadow Vista address as required for U.S. tax substantiation, while the **worker reference** strictly uses the cleared Tier 2/3 alias or approved ministry code (zero surnames, cities, countries, or confidential details) AND includes a calm _"please keep this update private; do not post online or on church livestreams"_ reminder.

## 1. First-Gift & Single-Contribution (`$250+`) Thank-You Receipt (`COPY-DONOR-FIRST-01`)

**When to send:** Within 48 hours of a donor's first processed gift (one-time or recurring), AND for any single contribution of **`$250` or more** (including an existing recurring donor's extra one-time gift, satisfying IRS IRC Section 170(f)(8) contemporaneous written acknowledgment; use Section 5B/5C instead if the gift arrived via DAF or IRA QCD).  
**Security rule:** For Tier 2 or Tier 3 worker designations, print only the cleared public designation name or approved ministry code; never include restricted country names, cities, or family details in email receipts, and add the calm Tier 2/3 privacy reminder.

## 5. Non-Cash Gift Acknowledgments: Stock, DAF, IRA QCD, and Check (`COPY-DONOR-NONCASH-01`)

**IRS Accuracy Rule:** Donor-Advised Fund (DAF) distributions and Qualified Charitable Distributions (IRA QCD gifts) carry distinct tax-receipt wording. Never issue a standard individual tax-deduction receipt for a DAF grant (the donor received their tax deduction when contributing to the sponsoring DAF institution).

### 5A. Stock or Securities Transfer Acknowledgment

> Thank you for your contribution of `[Number]` shares of `[Company Name / Ticker]` received by Global Fellowship, Inc. (`EIN 68-0214543`) on `[Transfer Receipt Date]`. No goods or services were provided in exchange for this gift. Per IRS rules for non-cash securities gifts, Global Fellowship acknowledges the description and receipt date of the shares transferred; please consult your tax advisor regarding your deductible fair market value.

### 5B. Donor-Advised Fund (DAF) Grant Acknowledgment

> Thank you for recommending a grant of `$[Amount]` through `[Sponsoring DAF Institution]`, received on `[Date]` and designated for `[Cleared Fund or Worker Name]`. Because tax deductibility was established at the time of your contribution to `[Sponsoring DAF Institution]`, this note serves as an acknowledgment of receipt and our gratitude rather than a separate tax receipt. No goods or services were provided in return for this grant.

### 5C. IRA Qualified Charitable Distribution (QCD) Acknowledgment

> Global Fellowship, Inc. (`EIN 68-0214543`) confirms receipt of your Qualified Charitable Distribution of `$[Amount]` transferred directly from `[IRA Custodian]` on `[Date]`. Global Fellowship, Inc. is a public charity qualified to receive IRA QCD transfers under IRC Section 408(d)(8), and no goods or services were provided in exchange for this transfer. Please keep this letter with your tax records and share it with your tax preparer.

## 6. Worker Transition, Fund Closure, and Gift Re-Designation Notice (`COPY-DONOR-REDESIG-01`)

**Governance Principle:** Global Fellowship exercises IRS-required variance power transparently and pastorally. When a worker concludes field service, shifts roles, enters an extended rest season, or a project fund closes, **notify recurring and recent donors promptly, protect worker privacy, and offer an explicit choice rather than silently moving recurring charges**.

### Decision Tree for Finance & Donor Care

| Scenario | Immediate Action on Recurring Gifts | Donor Notice & Options |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Worker concludes field service or transitions off support** | Notify donors at least 30 days ahead when possible; set automatic stop date for recurring charges unless donor opts into a new designation. | Explain transition respectfully (zero confidential medical/pastoral details), confirm how any remaining balance is applied (final transition care or General Field Fund), and invite donor to choose a new worker/fund or conclude giving cleanly. |
| **Specific project goal is fully funded or closed** | Pause recurring project charges at closure date and notify active givers. | Report clearly that the project need has closed, explain IRS variance power plainly, and let donors redirect remaining recurring gifts or stop them immediately. |
| **Worker enters security step-down (Tier 1 -> Tier 2/3)** | **Do not expose location or threat details.** Update receipt label immediately to approved Tier 2/3 code or collective label. | Send calm administrative notice via cleared channel confirming gifts continue without interruption under the updated protective designation label. |

### Re-Designation Notice Template

**Subject:** `An update regarding your giving to [Cleared Worker / Project Name]`  
**Preview text:** `Thank you for your faithful support, and please choose how you would like to direct future gifts.`

**Body:**

Dear `[Donor First Name]`,

Thank you for your faithful prayer and generosity toward `[Cleared Worker / Project Name]`. We are writing to share a practical update regarding your giving designation.

As of `[Effective Date]`, `[Cleared Worker / Project Name]` is `[concluding this season of field service / completing this project fund / transitioning roles]`. Out of respect for household privacy, we share only the details they have cleared for distribution, and we are deeply grateful for the years of partnership you have shared.

**How existing and future gifts will be handled**

- **Remaining designated balance:** In keeping with IRS 501(c)(3) stewardship standards and board oversight, any remaining designated funds after final `[transition / project]` expenses are applied to `[related regional church-planting fund / Global Fellowship General Field Fund]`.
- **Your recurring gift (`$[Amount]` / `[Cadence]`):** We will **not** assume you want future recurring charges moved automatically without your consent. Your recurring schedule for this fund will conclude automatically after `[Final Charge Date]` unless you choose one of the options below:
  1. **Direct future gifts to the Global Fellowship General Field Fund** (designated for field church-planting ministry and regional teams where needs are urgent; distinct from Where Most Needed and Home Office Operations).
  2. **Choose another cleared worker, field project, or Where Most Needed & Home Office Operations** (reply to this email or call `1 866 984 5622` and we will gladly share current options).
  3. **Allow this recurring schedule to close cleanly** after `[Final Charge Date]`, with our sincere gratitude.

_(For Tier 2 or Tier 3 worker transitions, add: "Out of care for this household and the local believers they serve alongside, please keep this update private; pray faithfully at home and in small groups, and do not post worker names, aliases, codes, or details on social media or church livestreams.")_

Simply reply with your preference or reach us at `finance@globalfellowship.org` (`1 866 984 5622`).

With gratitude,

## 8. Year-End Stewardship Appeal Framework (God-and-Local-Church Agency, Zero Manufactured Urgency)

When writing a November or December stewardship letter or email:

1. **Lead with concrete witness, never a ticking countdown clock:** Open with what God and local churches did faithfully across the year (dated to the actual reporting period) rather than `"midnight deadline"` panic or `"double your impact"` gimmicks unless a real verified matching grant exists.
2. **Keep God and local believers as the subject of spiritual fruit:** Never write `"your year-end gift rescues"` or `"saves souls"`. Write `"God gathers churches; local believers shepherd their neighbors; your year-end giving helps sustain households and regional care so they can stay present."`
3. **Separate prayer and giving cleanly:** Always invite prayer alongside any financial invitation, and keep candid financial scope notes (2025 consolidated vs. GF entity figures, Candid ID `8043461`, candid ECFA note) accessible before asking[^proof-2025].
4. **Provide practical year-end dates honestly:** Note standard United States postmark rules (checks postmarked by December 31) and broker lead times for stock or DAF gifts (recommend initiating securities transfers by mid-December so brokerage settlement clears before December 31)[^source-synthesis].
