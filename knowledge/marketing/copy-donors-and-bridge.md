## 1. Bridge Project Welcome Email (`COPY-BRIDGE-01`)

**Subject:** `The Bridge Project: How to write your first letter (no gift expected)`  
**Preview Text:** `Three simple prompts, two safety habits, and how to send your first letter with no financial gift expected.`

**Body:**  
Welcome to The Bridge Project (and Bridge Kids, if children are drawing or writing alongside you).

Through this fellowship, your household or small group exchanges letters and prays mutually with a local church planter in Jesus Christ. You will never be asked for money through The Bridge Project[^bridge], and the local household holds the exact same right to accept, pause, or step back from letters whenever their season shifts.

**Three prompts for your first letter:**

1. **Introduce your home or group:** Share first names and a snapshot from your week (a shared meal or a smartphone photo of a child's drawing).
2. **Share Scripture and invite prayer:** Name one verse strengthening you lately and ask how you can pray for them this month.
3. **Ask a neighborly question:** Ask about a favorite meal, a psalm they return to, or how their congregation gathers on Sundays.

**Writing with kids? Bridge Kids table prompts:** Draw your dinner table or favorite outdoor game and snap a quick phone photo; write one question for a friend in another country; pray one short sentence aloud together before sending.

**How to send your first letter (or voice note):** Email your letter, a phone photo of a family drawing, or a 20-second voice note (confirm clearance with your Bridge coordinator first before recording) to `info@globalfellowship.org` with `Subject: Bridge Project: Letter or Voice Note` (or `Subject: Bridge Project: Match Request` if requesting a new pairing), or mail it to `Global Fellowship, Attn: The Bridge Project, P.O. Box 1, Meadow Vista, CA 95722` (or call `1 866 984 5622` during Pacific business hours; web page planned). Our coordinator replies personally, checks safety boundaries, and coordinates translation as needed; replies follow local rhythms rather than a fixed monthly schedule, and both households share equal freedom to pause or step back at any time.

**Two safety habits before you send:**

- **Keep letters private:** Never post letters, worker names, return addresses, or photos on social media or church livestreams[^security].
- **Avoid sensitive questions:** Do not ask for street addresses, local politics, or names of new believers in sensitive regions.

Reply directly to this email if you ever feel stuck on what to write. We are glad you are here.

## 2. Bridge Coordinator & Bridge Kids Guardian-Safety Guide (`COPY-BRIDGE-SAFE-01`)

To protect children, families, and field households across The Bridge Project and Bridge Kids:

1. **First names only for minors:** Children writing or drawing on `GF-TPL-20` sign with a **first name and optional age only** (never last names, school names, sports jerseys, or home street addresses).
2. **Parent/guardian approval & channel check first:** Before sending any child's photo, voice note, or drawing, a parent or guardian reviews it and confirms the cleared delivery channel (`info@globalfellowship.org`, Subject: `Bridge Project`) with the Bridge Coordinator.
3. **Zero public posting of received letters or drawings:** Neither sending families nor Global Fellowship may post received Bridge Project letters, return drawings, or field-worker replies on social media, church newsletters, or public websites without fresh written Revocable Consent (`GF-TPL-15`) signed by all parties (and parent/guardian co-signature for any identifiable minor).
4. **Equal right to pause:** Remind participating households at onboarding that local church planters hold the exact same freedom to pause, delay replies during busy or sensitive seasons, or step back cleanly with zero guilt.

_(Requires Regional Director + Security Lead + Worker/Family sign-off before release. Never publish restricted names or locations on social media. Default initial hard-news alert carries **zero fundraising ask** so grief and safety are never mixed with an appeal.)_

**Subject (Lock-Screen Safe):** `Praying this week with our friends in Christ`  
**Preview Text:** `Private prayer note from our fellowship. Please do not forward.`

**Body:**  
Dear friends,

[Two to three plain, factual sentences approved directly by the household and Regional Director describing the medical need, sudden relocation, or loss, without names, cities, graphic detail, or speculation.]

**How you can stand with them right now:**

1. **Pray specifically:** [1 to 3 concrete requests approved by the worker or family, or a single sentence asking for prayer when silence honors grief best.]
2. **Protect their privacy:** Please pray faithfully at home and in small groups, and do not forward, screenshot, or post this note on social media or church livestreams.
3. **Practical help (never combined into an initial hard-news alert):** If the household and Regional Director later approve a verified practical need, Home Office sends a separate, clearly scoped stewardship note naming the exact project code and pre-approved variance policy.

Our regional member-care team is standing closely with our friends. We will share another update only when they give permission.
