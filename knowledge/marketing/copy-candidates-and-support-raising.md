## 1. Cross-Cultural Missionary Candidate Brief: Six Questions Before Applying (`COPY-CAND-01`)

**Headline:** **Six questions people ask before applying.** Global Fellowship sends cross-cultural missionaries (from North America and Majority-World churches alike, including bi-vocational and professional-skills teammates) to pioneer church planting among unreached people alongside and under local leaders[^candidates]:

1. **Who leads when I arrive?** The local leader still leads. You come to learn language deeply and serve alongside and under indigenous believers rather than directing outside projects.
2. **How is service funded (including bi-vocational paths)?** Workers build a personal prayer and financial partnership team, or combine local employment or bi-vocational work where visas and host context fit; lean, support-raised Home Office staff handle receipts and disbursements, and leadership reviews realistic budget norms before any departure date is set.
3. **What does discernment to departure look like?** Start with an exploratory first call (no application wall; email `info@globalfellowship.org` with `Subject: Exploring service`, your first name, and your working language, omitting restricted cities or names, or ask for a regional-hub encrypted handoff), followed by formal church-elder discernment with your sending congregation: doctrinal and calling review, regional team fit, cross-cultural and language-learning prep, and partnership building.
4. **Who cares for me or my household?** Under _Health Above Results_, regional directors stand beside single workers, couples, children, and teenagers alike, including confidential counseling referrals[^member-care].
5. **What happens when conflict arises?** Regional directors mediate early, and every worker has direct access to our International Member Care Facilitator outside their direct reporting line whenever an independent advocate is needed.
6. **What happens after my first message?** A mobilization lead replies directly to schedule an exploratory first call before any formal application; when you are ready to pursue commissioning, you bring your sending pastor or elders into review of calling, language readiness, and our historic evangelical Statement of Faith (8 public articles on `globalfellowship.org`, plus the full doctrinal questionnaire reviewed candidly before candidacy).

## 2. Prospective Support Staff Brief (`COPY-STAFF-01`)

**Headline:** **Support-raised and Home Office roles built to serve local church planters.**  
Local church planters and cross-cultural missionaries cannot focus on discipleship and local leadership if finance, donor care, communications, technology, and security systems falter behind them. Home Office and regional support staff serve inside Global Fellowship's operating order (`Global coordinates. Regional contextualizes. Local leads.`) so designated gifts reach local teams accurately[^support-staff].

- **How funding works:** Global Fellowship's 2025 Annual Report states that 100% of gifts designated for Global Fellowship missionaries and field projects go directly to the field. On our 2025 cash-basis consolidated Statement of Activity, Home Office operating expenses totaled `$251,610.11`, alongside an 8% administrative fee on applicable funds (`$192,504.66`), Home Office gifts, optional gifts toward card fees, rental income, and support-raised staff. Leadership confirms upfront whether a specific Home Office or regional hub role (including Chiang Mai) is support-raised, shared, or operations-funded, alongside compensation, benefits, coaching, and realistic timelines.
- **Whole-life care included:** Support staff share the same _Health Above Results_ care standard as field workers (for singles, couples, and families alike).
- **Begin a direct conversation:** Email `info@globalfellowship.org` (Subject: `Operations & Support Roles`) or call `1 866 984 5622` (Pacific business hours) for an unhurried first fit conversation covering current team needs (`GF-TPL-13` provides illustrative funding pathways and a 4-question pre-call checklist without implying an active vacancy).

## 5. Ethical Support-Raising Communication Guide (`COPY-SUPP-GUIDE-01`)

Global Fellowship invites workers to build **long-term prayer and stewardship friendships** rather than using guilt, urgency countdowns, or pity framing[^candidates]:

- **Lead with God's work and local believers:** Present local elders and congregations as active subjects in their own language, never as passive recipients waiting for Western rescue.
- **Ask clearly without pressure:** Invite friends and churches plainly to pray first and consider partnering financially if God leads. State your monthly budget honestly, explain Global Fellowship's 501(c)(3) accountability (`EIN 68-0214543`, Candid ID `8043461`), and share the 2025 financial summary upfront before asking for a gift.
- **Protect your field tier from Day 1:** If preparing for a Tier 2 or Tier 3 region, **never** publish your destination city, visa strategy, host names, or fundraising target on public social media or open fundraising pages[^security]. Hold living-room conversations and use vetted direct email or printed cards.
- **Honor "no" and rest seasons:** Receive every "no" or "not right now" with warmth and zero guilt. When you transition out of a field role, coordinate through Finance so recurring gifts are never continued quietly without consent.
