### Comment & Direct Message Moderation Protocol

- **Rule 1 (Immediate Hide / Delete for Field Safety):** If any public commenter posts a restricted worker's real surname, exact city, house-church location, or travel dates, **hide or delete the comment immediately** (without scolding publicly), notify the Regional Director, and send a polite private message explaining why sensitive details stay offline.
- **Rule 2 (Financial or Skeptical Questions):** Never delete honest questions about finances, ECFA membership, or governance. Reply calmly in public with factual 2025 summary links and invite direct correspondence with `finance@globalfellowship.org`.
- **Rule 3 (Prayer Requests & Pastoral Care):** Acknowledge prayer notes warmly and route private follow-ups to `prayer@globalfellowship.org` or `info@globalfellowship.org`.
- **Rule 4 (Spam, Abuse, or Impersonation):** Block and report scam accounts or abusive comments; never debate hostility in comment threads.
