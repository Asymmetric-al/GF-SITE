# Organization-Wide Channel Playbooks

Adapt Global Fellowship's single character intelligently to each medium rather than forcing identical layouts onto every channel. For exact file paths and exports for every Asset ID below, see [Asset Index](../references/asset-index.md)[^asset-index].

## 1. Churches, Bulletins & Presentations

- **Primary assets:** `GF-TPL-06` (Church & Sunday Kit), `GF-TPL-17` (pure-black half-letter bulletin), `GF-TPL-22` (Elders' Vetting Packet), `GF-TPL-23` (Exposure Trip Host Agreement), `GF-TPL-10` & `office/global-fellowship-deck-starter.pptx` (Tier 1 8-slide deck; no restricted `.pptx` exists by design).
- **Sunday copier rule:** Handouts and inserts must print cleanly in pure `#000000` black on white at 300dpi without dark-ink waste (`OVR-18`).
- **Livestream rule:** Never project Tier 2/3 personal names, cities, or photos on a church livestream; only Tier 1 explicit opt-ins or separately cleared Tier 1 anonymous collective prayer (`GF-TPL-10` Slide 5) belong on a public stream[^security]. Offline recording and public livestream permissions are cleared independently on `GF-TPL-15`.

## 2. Responsive Email & Field Newsletters

- **Primary assets:** `GF-TPL-08` (Bridge Project email), `GF-TPL-09` (Stewardship & Hard-News emails), `GF-TPL-21` & `office/donor-receipt-and-redesignation-starter.docx` (Finance receipts), `GF-TPL-04` & `office/worker-prayer-letter-{public,restricted}-starter.docx`.
- **Layout & images-off craft:** Single-column fluid table (`600px` max width, clean at `320px` and Harbor Night dark mode), Warm Paper ground, `16px` serif body, underlined Harbor Teal links, and **zero-`<img>` live-text mastheads** so images-off clients show crisp flush-left typography with no broken image frames[^visual].
- **Header & subject rules:** Stewardship emails use daylight Warm Linen headers; any Candle Apricot hospitality band requires the Charcoal Ink monochrome lockup (`9.8:1` AAA). Pastoral hard-news emails use a neutral lock-screen-safe subject (`Praying this week with our friends in Christ`) with **zero initial fundraising ask**.
- **Cadence:** Mix prayer, Bridge, and thank-you notes alongside stewardship updates so supporters never hear from GF solely when money is requested[^verbal].

## 3. Mobile Prayer Notes & Short-Form Vertical Video

- **Primary assets:** `GF-TPL-14` (Page 1 & Page 3: Tier 2/3 EN/TH/AR `< 150 KB` phone cards + 1-to-1 plain-text twins `exports/mobile-prayer-note-*-twin.txt`; Page 2: Tier 1 Public video storyboard & 30s audio-first spec) and `GF-VID-01` (`living-nearness-prayer-vertical.mp4` + 10-cue WebVTT + transcript).
- **Low-bandwidth parity:** Always send the plain-text twin alongside any phone graphic, strip metadata/identifying filenames, and keep video captions inside the 4:5 platform safe zone.
- **Strict video tier boundary:** `GF-VID-01` embeds a street photograph (`GF-PHO-01`) and the GF mark, so it is **Tier 1 Public Social & Lobby Display ONLY** (never Tier 3 or photo-free). Tier 3 uses photo-free `GF-TPL-01` print and `GF-TPL-14` EN/TH/AR plain-text twins strictly in vetted private groups (never recorded or livestreamed; public livestreams carry only separately cleared anonymous collective prayer modeled on `GF-TPL-10` Slide 5).

## 4. Social Media & OpenGraph Previews (Tier 1 Only)

- **Primary assets:** `GF-TPL-11` (8 PNGs: Square, Open Books Portrait, Photo Story, Story, OG card, 3 Q&A Carousels) paired with `GF-SCR-05` (`exports/social-photo-credits.txt`).
- **Hard security rule:** **NEVER** post Tier 2/3 personal worker details, photos, cities, regional cues, or Bridge Project correspondence on social media[^security]. Public prayer posts use cleared anonymous collective prompts on Harbor Night and remind followers not to post names or locations.
- **Attribution & alt text:** Include CC attribution + license URL + non-congregation disclaimer (`GF-SCR-05`) whenever using vault street photos, and write factual alt text without leaking redacted place names.

## 5. Print, Prayer Cards & Annual Reports

- **Primary assets:** `GF-TPL-01`/`02` (4x6 duplex bleed prayer cards), `GF-TPL-05` (2025 Annual Report spread), `GF-TPL-07`, `GF-TPL-20` (Bridge Kids pure-black sheet), `GF-TPL-25` (Bridge Welcome Guide).
- **Print craft:** `10pt+` body copy, Warm Paper + hairline tables over heavy boxes, unified tier-neutral fronts/backs across Tier 1 and Tier 3 prayer cards, and press-profiled CMYK proofs before bulk printing (`OVR-18`)[^visual].

## 6. Internal Communication, Confidential Member Care & Level 4 Local Print

- **Primary assets:** `GF-TPL-18` (standalone Confidential Member Care Journal), `GF-TPL-12` (public stationery & zero-brand Restricted Travel Card), `GF-TPL-24B` (internal Day-1 Onboarding & Signatures), `GF-TPL-15` (Revocable Consent Form), `GF-TPL-16` & `GF-TPL-19` (Level 4 English and pure-Arabic zero-attribution masters + `.docx` starters), `GF-GDE-02`, and `GF-GDE-03`.
- **Hard isolation rules:** Never bundle confidential pastoral care (`GF-TPL-18`) into public stationery or donor mailings. Carry only the unbranded Restricted Travel Card into Tier 2/3 regions. Ministries requiring zero foreign association print exclusively from Level 4 masters (`GF-TPL-16`, `GF-TPL-19`, or Level 4 `.docx` starters) with zero GF mark, name, EIN, or brand font fingerprint.
