# Organization-Wide Channel Playbooks

Consistent branding means **one recognizable character adapted intelligently to each medium**, never stamping an identical poster across every screen and page.

## 1. Church Relationships, Bulletins & Live Presentations

- **Primary tools:** `church-partnership-and-sunday-kit.html` (`GF-TPL-06`), Sending Church & Elders' Vetting Packet `church-elders-vetting-packet.html` (`GF-TPL-22`), Two-Sided Exposure Trip Host Agreement `exposure-trip-host-agreement.html` (`GF-TPL-23`), standalone half-letter 1-color black copier master `sunday-bulletin-insert-half-letter.html` (`GF-TPL-17`), pure-black family table `bridge-kids-drawing-and-prayer-sheet.html` (`GF-TPL-20`), Bridge Matched-Household Welcome Guide `bridge-matched-household-welcome-guide.html` (`GF-TPL-25`), 16:9 deck `presentation-deck.html` (`GF-TPL-10`, body `>= 20px` back-row legibility, logical Arabic `/ActualText`), and editable 8-slide PowerPoint/Slides starter `brand-kit/office/global-fellowship-deck-starter.pptx` (with embedded Slide 1 street photo `GF-PHO-01`, per-script `<a:r>` font runs on Slide 4, and clickable `mailto:` links).
- **Sunday survival rule:** Every church bulletin insert and handout must reproduce cleanly on a **pure `#000000` black-and-white 300dpi church copier** with white/light grounds and sharp luminance contrast (`OVR-18` tracks physical print verification).
- **Livestream safety rule:** Most churches stream Sunday services publicly to YouTube or Facebook. Never project Tier 2 or Tier 3 personal names, cities, or photographs on a church livestream; only Tier 1 explicit opt-ins or Tier 1 anonymous collective congregation-wide prayer (as modeled on Slide 5 of `GF-TPL-10`) belong on a public broadcast[^security]. Recorded offline church services and public livestreams are cleared independently on the Revocable Consent Form (`GF-TPL-15`).

## 2. Responsive Email & Field Newsletters

- **Primary tools:** `bridge-project-letter-and-email.html` (`GF-TPL-08`), `donor-stewardship-and-hard-news-email.html` (`GF-TPL-09`), `donor-lifecycle-and-receipt-kit.html` (`GF-TPL-21`), Finance manual fill-in `brand-kit/office/donor-receipt-and-redesignation-starter.docx`, `worker-prayer-letter-kit.html` (`GF-TPL-04`), and dual editable Word/Docs starters `brand-kit/office/worker-prayer-letter-public-starter.docx` (Tier 1) and `brand-kit/office/worker-prayer-letter-restricted-starter.docx` (zero-logo Tier 2/3 safe default).
- **Layout & typography craft:** Fluid 100% single-column table (`600px` max width, MSO Outlook wrapper, verified zero overflow at `320px` with stacked ledger rows and `375px` plus unified Harbor Night `#0B272E` `@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark)` support), Warm Paper (`#FAF7F2`) ground, `16px` `Source Serif 4` / `Georgia` body, `Plus Jakarta Sans` progressive enhancement with clean client-resident `"Helvetica Neue", Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif` weight `700` fallback, underlined Harbor Teal (`#0C525C`) links, and **pure live-text zero-`<img>` `Global Fellowship` mastheads** (zero `<img>` tags inside `.email-shell`) so images-off inbox clients render crisp flush-left typography with zero broken-image placeholders (`bridge-project-email-images-off.png` and `donor-stewardship-email-images-off.png`).
- **Warm Open Books Header & Candle Apricot Rule:** On Open Books stewardship emails (`GF-TPL-09` Template A), use Warm Linen (`#F1ECE3`) with Kiln Clay (`#7E3119`) and Charcoal Ink (`#16191C`) text. Whenever Candle Apricot (`#F2B488`) is used as a hospitality surface, pair it strictly with the Charcoal Ink monochrome lockup (`gf-lockup-horizontal-mono-2x.png`, `9.80:1` AAA)[^visual].
- **Subject lines & lock-screen safety:** Keep routine subjects plain and specific (`The Bridge Project: How to write your first letter (no gift expected)`). For sensitive pastoral hard-news emails (`GF-TPL-09` Template B), use the neutral no-details collective default (`Praying this week with our friends in Christ`) with **zero fundraising ask** so private medical or crisis details never flash on a public phone screen.
- **Cadence discipline:** Send prayer, Bridge, and thank-you notes alongside financial updates so supporters never hear from Global Fellowship solely when money is requested.
