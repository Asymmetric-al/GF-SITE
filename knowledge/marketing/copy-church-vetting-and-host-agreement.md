## 1. Sending Church & Elders' Vetting Brief (`COPY-CHURCH-VET-01`)

**Purpose:** Give a pastor or missions committee every hard doctrinal, governance, financial, and field-security answer in one calm 10-minute packet before a first phone call[^foundations][^proof-2025].

### Six Questions Elders Ask First (Plain Answers)

1. **What do you believe?** Global Fellowship holds historic evangelical Christian faith centered on Scripture, the Triune God, salvation by grace through faith in Jesus Christ, and the calling of the local church. The full 8-article public Statement of Faith is included verbatim in `GF-TPL-22` (internal 10-point staff reconciliation tracked under `OVR-15`).
2. **Who leads the work on the ground?** Local believers lead their own churches (`Global coordinates. Regional contextualizes. Local leads.`). Cross-cultural missionaries arrive as learners and coworkers alongside and under local elders, never as outside directors imposing Western church forms.
3. **Who oversees Global Fellowship legally and financially?** Global Fellowship, Inc. is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit founded in 1989 (`EIN 68-0214543`, Meadow Vista, CA), led by Founder & President Don Oates, Co-Executive Directors Jeremy Beaumont and Conrad O'Brien, and a governing board chaired by Eric Bergen.
4. **Can we inspect your books, and why are you not ECFA-accredited?** Yes. Our 2025 Consolidated Statement of Activity (`$5.82M` received / `$5.73M` disbursed across Global Fellowship `$2.08M/$2.13M` and affiliated ministries MissionStream `$3.08M/$2.92M` and BOLK `$660k/$683k`) and Candid profile (`8043461`) are open to review before you partner. We are not currently an ECFA member due to annual fees and small Home Office staff capacity; we welcome direct questions at `finance@globalfellowship.org`[^proof-2025].
5. **How do you care for missionaries we send?** Member care is led regionally (including our Southeast Asia hub opened in Chiang Mai in 2024) rather than only from California, covering spiritual, emotional, physical, financial, and family health for both singles and families. We hold **Health Above Results**: rest seasons and honest hardship never disqualify a worker.
6. **How do you protect sensitive workers during Sunday services and livestreams?** Two independent permissions govern church services: offline recordings vs. public YouTube/Facebook livestreams. Tier 2 and Tier 3 worker names, cities, and photos are **never** shown on public livestreams[^security].

## 2. Exposure Trip Two-Sided Host Agreement (`COPY-TRIP-AGREE-01`)

**Core commitment:** Every Exposure Trip begins with **mutual discernment and an unconditional local-host veto**. No church announces a trip, raises trip funds, or books airfare until both Side A (Local Host & Regional Director) and Side B (Visiting Church Leadership) sign this plain-language agreement (`GF-TPL-23`)[^exposure-trips].

### Side A: Local Host & Regional Leadership Authority

1. **Unconditional right to say "not this year":** Local elders and regional hosts may decline, shorten, postpone, or cancel any visit at any stage if family strain, congregation health, or security conditions shift, with zero relational penalty or loss of fellowship.
2. **Schedule, group size, and ministry focus:** Local hosts set maximum team size, visit length, daily pace, dress/cultural norms, and whether any visiting teaching or speaking is appropriate.
3. **Media, camera, and social boundaries:** Local hosts set clear written rules before arrival on smartphones, photographs, audio recording, names, and post-trip sharing.

### Side B: Visiting Church Leadership Commitments

1. **Arrive as guests and learners:** Leave uninvited building projects, outside curriculum, and short-term savior narratives at home. Listen first, pray faithfully, and honor local decisions.
2. **Self-funded & zero burden on local hospitality:** Visiting teams cover their own travel, lodging, meals, and host-related hospitality costs so local households are never financially strained.
3. **Strict post-trip security discipline:** Every team member follows agreed photo, name, city, and livestream boundaries both on site and after returning home, including zero social media tagging or geo-located photos in sensitive areas[^security].
4. **Honest cancellation & travel-insurance readiness:** Visiting churches purchase refundable or insurable travel arrangements and absorb cancellation costs gracefully if local hosts pause a visit for safety or congregation health.
5. **Long-term friendship beyond the flight home:** Commit to ongoing prayer, Bridge Project correspondence, or multi-year church partnership rather than one-off mission tourism.

## 3. Faith on the Frontlines: Live-Room & Q&A Security Run-of-Show (`COPY-FOTF-RUN-01`)

Faith on the Frontlines brings visiting church planters into homes and local congregations for unhurried conversation, prayer, and honest Q&A[^gathering-offering].

### Pre-Gathering Check (48 Hours Before)

- **Confirm the speaker's cleared security tier** with the Global Engagement Coordinator (Tier 1 Open, Tier 2 Partial/Alias, or Tier 3 Private/Offline Only).
- **Device & livestream rule:** If ANY speaker or story in the room is Tier 2 or Tier 3, **turn off all livestreams, video cameras, voice recorders, and public photography**. Announce this calmly at the start so guests feel welcomed rather than policed.
- **Name tag rule:** Use first names only when sensitive workers are present; never print sensitive regions or surnames on lobby signage or social-media event promos.

### 60–75 Minute Calm Room Flow (Aligned with `GF-TPL-07`)

1. **Minutes 0–5 (Welcome & Room Safety Cue):** Host welcomes guests, explains the relaxed format, and gives a warm 15-second device reminder (_"Because some friends we pray for serve where public names could bring risk, let us keep phones tucked away tonight and enjoy being present together"_).
2. **Minutes 5–25 (Firsthand Story & Scripture):** Visiting planter or regional leader shares in their own voice (grounded in everyday discipleship, local church life, and honest challenges).
3. **Minutes 25–45 (Open Q&A with Host Safety Guard):** Host moderates calmly. If a well-meaning guest asks a sensitive location or personal question (_"Which exact city?"_ or _"How did you enter the country?"_), the host or speaker redirects gracefully without awkwardness:
   > _"That is a thoughtful question. Because neighbors and house churches there still face real scrutiny, we keep exact cities and travel logistics offline, but I can gladly share what everyday discipleship looks like around the kitchen table."_
4. **Minutes 45–55 (Guided Prayer Together):** Pray specifically for local elders, endurance, households, and open doors for Scripture in the community's mother tongue.
5. **Minutes 55–60 (Clear, Low-Pressure Next Steps):** Offer two equal doors: (a) **The Bridge Project** (prayer and letter exchange with zero financial ask) and (b) **Optional giving/church partnership info** on a quiet side table (no passing a plate with pressure).

## 4. Bridge Coordinator & Bridge Kids Guardian-Safety Guide (`COPY-BRIDGE-SAFE-01`)

To protect children, families, and field households across The Bridge Project and Bridge Kids:

1. **First names only for minors:** Children writing or drawing on `GF-TPL-20` (`bridge-kids-drawing-and-prayer-sheet.pdf`) sign with a **first name and optional age only** (never last names, school names, sports jerseys, or home street addresses).
2. **Parent/guardian approval & channel check first:** Before sending any child's photo, voice note, or drawing, a parent or guardian reviews it and confirms the cleared delivery channel (`info@globalfellowship.org`, Subject: `Bridge Project`) with the Bridge Coordinator.
3. **Zero public posting of received letters or drawings:** Neither sending families nor Global Fellowship may post received Bridge Project letters, return drawings, or field-worker replies on social media, church newsletters, or public websites without fresh written Revocable Consent (`GF-TPL-15`) signed by all parties (and parent/guardian co-signature for any identifiable minor).
4. **Equal right to pause:** Remind participating households at onboarding that local church planters hold the exact same freedom to pause, delay replies during busy or sensitive seasons, or step back cleanly with zero guilt.
