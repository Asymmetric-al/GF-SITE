Warmly in Christ, [Name]`

## 3. Ethical Support-Raising Communication Guide (`COPY-SUPP-GUIDE-01`)

Global Fellowship invites workers to build **long-term prayer and stewardship friendships** rather than using guilt, urgency countdowns, or pity framing[^candidates].

- **Lead with God's work and local believers:** Present local elders and congregations as active subjects in their own language, never as passive recipients waiting for Western rescue.
- **Ask clearly without pressure:** Invite friends and churches plainly to pray first and consider partnering financially if God leads. State your monthly budget honestly, explain Global Fellowship's 501(c)(3) accountability (`EIN 68-0214543`, Candid ID `8043461`), and share the 2025 financial summary upfront before asking for a gift.
- **Protect your field tier from Day 1:** If you are preparing for a Tier 2 or Tier 3 region, **never** publish your destination city, visa strategy, host names, or fundraising target on public social media or open fundraising pages[^security]. Hold small-group living-room conversations and use vetted direct email or printed cards.
- **Honor "no" and rest seasons:** Receive every "no" or "not right now" with warmth and zero guilt. When a supporter steps back or you transition out of a field role, thank them genuinely and coordinate through Finance so recurring gifts are never continued quietly without consent.

### Tier 2 & Tier 3 (Partial-Alias & Restricted-Region Field Workers)

```text

[Neutral Professional or Personal Sign-Off - ZERO Global Fellowship Name, Logo, EIN, or Mission Keywords]
```

**Hard Tier 2/3 Rules:**

- **Never** send routine local or in-country email from a `@globalfellowship.org` address or append Global Fellowship's California address, EIN, or giving link.
- **Never** include a Global Fellowship logo image or banner in a Tier 2/3 signature.
- Keep supporter updates strictly on approved vetted channels (`GF-TPL-14` private text twins or `worker-prayer-letter-restricted-starter.docx`).

## 5. Day-1 New Worker & Staff Communications Onboarding Checklist (`COPY-ONB-01`)

Complete all seven steps during onboarding week before a new worker or staff member sends any external update[^security][^open-verification]:

1. **Confirm assigned disclosure tier (Tier 1, Tier 2, Tier 3, or Level 4) in writing** with the Regional Director and Operations Lead (`OVR-14`).
2. **Set up the matching email signature** (Tier 1 plain-text GF signature OR Tier 2/3 zero-GF signature - never mix accounts).
3. **Read the 2-minute Brand on a Page and Field-Safe Checklist (`GF-GDE-02`, `GF-GDE-03`)** so Mission, Vision, Three Pillars, and retired claims are crystal clear.
4. **Download the exact matching Office starter** (`worker-prayer-letter-public-starter.docx` for Tier 1 ONLY; `worker-prayer-letter-restricted-starter.docx` for Tier 2/3; `local-only-meeting-sheet-starter.docx` / Arabic RTL starter for Level 4).
5. **Confirm giving-link and QR code safety rules** (never post Tier 2/3 giving links on social media or public church livestreams; never use redirect link-shortening services).
6. **Review `GF-TPL-15` Revocable Consent and EXIF stripping** before sharing any photograph, quote, or child drawing.
7. **Save the emergency & member-care contact sequence** (Regional Director primary; Co-Executive Directors `executivedirectors@globalfellowship.org` and `1 866 984 5622` escalation; zero invented off-hours hotlines per `OVR-17` and `OVR-20`).

## 6. Furlough & Home-Assignment Church Visit Starter (`COPY-FURLOUGH-01`)

When a field household returns for rest and church connection (under Health Above Results):

- **Before accepting a speaking invitation:** Confirm with your Regional Director whether the church's Sunday service is livestreamed or archived online. If Tier 2 or Tier 3, request cameras off during your testimony and keep names, cities, and host ministries off slides and printed bulletins.
- **Protect household rest first:** Schedule genuine family rest weeks before filling Sundays. A single quiet meal with elders or a small living-room gathering often bears deeper fruit than ten rushed pulpit appearances.
- **Invitation note to a sending pastor:**

> **Subject:** `Visiting home this [Season] and hoping to see your elders`  
> **Body:** `Dear Pastor [Name], [I / our family] will be back in [City/State] from [Month] to [Month] for rest, medical checkups, and connecting with the churches who pray for [me / us]. [I / We] would love to sit down with you and the elders over coffee to thank you in person, share what God has been teaching our local fellowship, and hear how [I / we] can pray for your congregation. Because of our regional privacy guidelines, we keep our updates offline (off livestreams and social media), so an in-person meal or small-group evening works especially well. Let [me / us] know if a morning or evening in [Month] works for you.`
