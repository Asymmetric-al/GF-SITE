## Mandatory Trend Caution

Between 2024 and 2025, reported source labels and definitions shifted across multiple categories: 2024 reported `36 countries where churches were planted` and `525 missionaries`, whereas 2025 reported `43 countries served` and `584 workers mobilized`; meanwhile reported Bibles (`9,223` vs `8,000+`), baptisms (`1,700` vs `1,000+`), and churches planted (`1,254` vs `230`) changed substantially, including a known scope shift on church planting[^source-synthesis]. Never treat non-identical year labels (such as `36` vs `43`) as apples-to-apples growth, never cherry-pick rising lines while hiding lower lines, and never chart multi-year field trends until Operations reconciles definitions across years (`OVR-04`, `OVR-05`).
