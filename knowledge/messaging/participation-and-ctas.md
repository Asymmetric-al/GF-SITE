# Participation Pathways and Call-to-Action System

Global Fellowship invites participation with **"relational warmth, sound judgment, and persistence without pressure"**[^source-synthesis]. Every invitation makes two things unmistakable: **what this step actually involves** and **what happens immediately after you say yes**.

## Seven Concrete Participation Pathways

| Pathway | Who It Fits Best | Honest Terms & Expectations | Direct / Transitional CTA | What Happens Next |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Pray With a Church Planter (Print & Mobile Note)** | Anyone; families, small groups, congregations, WhatsApp/Signal prayer groups | Free, ongoing, respectful of field-security rules (never post restricted card or Note details online). | `Request a prayer card` / `Join a monthly prayer Note` | Enroll via Global Engagement (`info@globalfellowship.org`) or an authorized regional encrypted group coordinator to receive a cleared 4x6 print/PDF prayer card or a lightweight `<150 KB` phone graphic paired with an exact plain-text twin (`GF-TPL-14`). |
| **2. Join The Bridge Project (& Bridge Kids)** | Households, small groups, parents with children, newcomers | Two-way letters and prayer with a local church planter; **explicitly no financial expectation** (and the local household holds the equal right to pause or step back). | `Request a Bridge Project match` (`info@globalfellowship.org`; planned `globalfellowship.org/bridge-project`, `OVR-21`) | Receive your match guide, first-letter and Bridge Kids prompts, and safe-correspondence rules before writing. |
| **3. Partner Financially** | Individuals, families, churches | Designate support for a missionary, field project, or Home Office operations; read open Statements of Activity first. | `Explore giving designations` (`finance@globalfellowship.org`; planned `globalfellowship.org/give`, `OVR-21`) / `Review our financial statements` (`finance@globalfellowship.org`; planned `globalfellowship.org/financials`, `OVR-21`) | Choose a worker or project, receive clear 501(c)(3) tax receipts, and receive honest seasonal updates. |
| **4. Build a Church Partnership** | Pastors, elders, missions committees | Multi-year prayer, mutual learning, sending, hosting visiting planters, and optional listening-first Exposure Trips. | `Start a church conversation` / `Share the Church Brief with elders` | Schedule a peer conversation with Global Engagement to review our Statement of Faith, open books, and regional fit without pressure. |
| **5. Host a Faith on the Frontlines Gathering** | Homes and churches (currently documented in Greater Sacramento; confirm regional availability `OVR-08`) | Informal meal or living-room evening where a visiting church planter shares firsthand stories, invites prayer, and offers a pressure-free side-table option. | `Inquire about hosting` | Connect with the coordinator to confirm visiting planter availability, security tier, and host run-of-show. |
| **6. Take a Listening-First Exposure Trip** | Church leaders and committed church members | Local hosts define purpose, group size, timing, and photo boundaries (`"not this year"` is always a respected host answer). | `Read the Exposure Trip Host Agreement` | Begin discerning with a regional team whether a visit serves the local church's current season. |
| **7. Explore Serving (Cross-Cultural, Near-Culture, or Support Staff)** | Missionary candidates, indigenous leaders, operations/care professionals | Shared Statement of Faith, exploratory first call separate from formal church-elder discernment, serving under local leadership (for cross-cultural workers), support-raised or bi-vocational pathways clarified upfront, and regional member care. | `Read Candidate Expectations` / `Explore open support roles` (`info@globalfellowship.org`) | Walk through direct human conversations and mutual discernment rather than an automated funnel. |

## Mandatory CTA Rules

1. **Retire single-track boilerplate:** Never stamp `"I want to be sent! / Send Me / Missions Coach"` onto financial, prayer, or story pages.
2. **Pair direct and transitional invitations:** Every giving or serving page also offers a no-cost transitional step (praying, joining The Bridge Project via `info@globalfellowship.org` / planned `globalfellowship.org/bridge-project` (`OVR-21`), or reading the Annual Report).
3. **Honor local host consent on every trip or event CTA:** Never promise instant booking dates for Exposure Trips or Faith on the Frontlines without regional confirmation.
4. **Never attach manufactured urgency:** Avoid countdown clocks, `"last chance to save a village"`, or guilt-inducing microcopy.
