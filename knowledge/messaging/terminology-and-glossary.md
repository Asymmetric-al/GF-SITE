# Global Fellowship Terminology, Plain-Language Glossary, and Vocabulary Guide

Global Fellowship speaks with theological fidelity **and** plain-language hospitality. Whenever a Christian or missiological term appears on a public page, define it naturally in context so a newcomer or small-group member never feels shut out by insider jargon.

## 1. Plain-Language Glossary (Explain Naturally for Newcomers)

| Term | Plain-Language Explanation for General Readers | Missiological / GF Precision Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The Gospel** | The Good News that God loves the world and brings sinful, separated people back to himself through the life, sacrificial death, and bodily resurrection of Jesus Christ, received by faith. | Always central; never reduced to humanitarian aid or social improvement[^foundations]. |
| **Disciple / Making Disciples** | Following Jesus Christ in daily life and walking patiently with new believers as they learn Scripture, pray, obey Christ, and share him with others. | Long-term relational formation, not a one-time decision count. |
| **Church Planting** | Proclaiming Christ's death and resurrection, making disciples, baptizing believers, and gathering healthy, multiplying local churches. _(Canonical Pillar 1 gloss from `foundations.md:62`.)_ | Focuses on living congregations and mature leadership, not physical buildings. |
| **Local Leadership** | Empowering believers who already belong to the language, culture, and community to shepherd and multiply the church. _(Canonical Pillar 2 gloss from `foundations.md:63`.)_ | Core operating principle since 1989: when a cross-cultural missionary arrives, the local leader still leads. |
| **Unreached People (UPG)** | Communities with little or no access to the Gospel and no self-sustaining local church movement able to reach the wider group. _(Canonical Pillar 3 gloss from `foundations.md:64`.)_ | Formal Joshua Project threshold: `<= 2%` evangelical Christian AND `<= 5%` Christian adherents overall[^missions-context]. |
| **Unengaged, Unreached People Group (UUPG)** | An unreached community marked by the absence of known active efforts to establish self-sustaining churches. | Standard Joshua Project UUPG definition; distinguish clearly from UPG and never blend counts[^missions-context]. |
| **Near-Culture (Local / Indigenous) Missionary** | A believer sent to plant a church among people who share a similar language, history, or regional culture where no Gospel community yet exists. | Global Fellowship defines a missionary by frontier church-planting calling, not by passport. |
| **Cross-Cultural Missionary** | A believer who crosses significant language, cultural, or national boundaries to learn a community deeply and serve alongside and under local leaders in church planting. | Serves in humility under local leadership since GF's 2012 sending expansion. |
| **Integration Without Assimilation** | _(Internal term)_ Local ministries belong fully to Global Fellowship's relational family and accountability while keeping their own name, leadership, cultural wisdom, and methods. | Translate into plain speech publicly: _"Local ministries keep their own identity, leadership, and local wisdom while sharing care and accountability in one family."_ |
| **Fellowship (_Koinonia_)** | Shared life and mutual belonging in Christ where every participant both gives and receives prayer, wisdom, encouragement, hospitality, and practical help. | Never a polite synonym for "donor list." |

## 2. Preferred vs. Avoided Vocabulary

| Use This | Instead of This | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Local church planter / near-culture missionary** | `"Native missionary"` / `"national worker"` | Honors equal missionary dignity without colonial or patronizing overtones. |
| **Local leader / partner pastor / host** | `"Beneficiary"` / `"recipient"` / `"field asset"` | Preserves full human agency and peer leadership. |
| **Partner / supporter / friend / sending church** | `"Funder"` / `"donor base"` / `"prospect"` | Reflects reciprocal fellowship rather than transactional extraction. |
| **Serve alongside and under local leaders** | `"Lead indigenous teams"` / `"deploy staff"` | Accurate to Global Fellowship's actual operating order since 1989. |
| **Unreached communities / neighbors without a local church** | `"Darkness"` / `"unreached targets"` / `"the masses"` | Sees real human neighbors made in God's image; avoids dehumanizing or military framing. |
| **Workers serving across 43 countries (2025 Annual Report)** | `"584 troops mobilized"` / `"expanding footprint"` | Plain human language safe under adversarial-reader review[^security]. |
| **Health Above Results** | `"Work-life balance perk"` | Protects the theological seriousness of sustainable endurance for singles and families. |

## 3. Adversarial-Reader Vocabulary Discipline

Officials and hostile actors in restricted countries read public missions websites and exported PDFs[^security]. In all public copy and especially on Tier 2 and Tier 3 worker materials:

- **Never use** military or conquest metaphors: `target`, `penetrate`, `conquer`, `crusade`, `army`, `infiltrate`, `covert`, `underground operatives`.
- **Use with care:** Preserve `"pioneer"` inside the verbatim Mission Statement (locked), while avoiding `"frontier conquest"` imagery elsewhere. Retain `"Faith on the Frontlines"` solely as the documented existing North American gathering name pending leadership review (`OVR-10`).
