# Global Fellowship Core Narrative Architecture

Global Fellowship never casts a Western agency or donor as the hero rescuing distant strangers. It tells a truer Christian story where **God builds his church in Jesus Christ**, **local believers lead in their own communities**, **cross-cultural teammates serve alongside and under them**, and **every believer joins one worldwide household**[^strategy][^creative-record].

## 1. Master Organizational Narrative (Five Movements)

1. **The Reality (A Neighbor Without a Local Church):** Picture an ordinary street or apartment where neighbors share meals and carry grief, yet no local church gathers nearby and the Gospel of Christ's death and resurrection is rarely heard in their mother tongue. Every unreached place is somebody's home. _(Research context pending `OVR-19`: Joshua Project estimates roughly 3.6 billion people across 7,200+ unreached people groups[^missions-context].)_
2. **The Conviction (Who Leads When a Missionary Arrives):** Local believers already speak the language and proclaim Christ in everyday neighborhood life. Before founding Global Fellowship in Auburn, California in 1989, Don and Kathy Oates served in Liberia under a local church leader. When cross-cultural sending began in 2012, that order held firm: **when a cross-cultural missionary arrives, the local leader still leads.**
3. **The Fellowship (How We Work Together):** Global Fellowship unites indigenous near-culture church planters and cross-cultural missionaries (Majority World and North American alike) in one worldwide family. `"Global coordinates. Regional contextualizes. Local leads."` Local ministries keep their identity (`integration without assimilation`), regional teams guard worker whole-life health (`Health Above Results`), and global staff remove administrative barriers.
4. **The Invitation (Reciprocal Participation):** Believers join as peers through faithful prayer, two-way Bridge Project letters (no financial ask), listening-first Exposure Trips hosted by local leaders, hosting visiting planters at Faith on the Frontlines, open-books giving, or exploring cross-cultural and support service.
5. **The Prayerful Longing (Locked Vision):** Every step points toward one unchanging prayerful vision (never a coverage metric)[^foundations]:
   > "It is our vision to plant a Gospel witness within 30 minutes of everyone."

## 2. Four-Lens Ethical Narrative Posture

Using StoryBrand's decision clarity without donor saviorism, every audience enters a distinct posture while **God stays the Author of salvation** and **local leaders remain full peers**[^creative-record]. For concrete CTAs and proof points per audience, see [Audience Messaging Matrix](audience-messaging-matrix.md) and [Participation Pathways](participation-and-ctas.md).
