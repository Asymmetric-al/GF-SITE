# Global Fellowship Message Hierarchy and Altitude Ladder

Whether someone has five seconds on a Sunday bulletin or twenty minutes in an elders' meeting, Global Fellowship's message stays anchored in **Jesus Christ**, **local leadership**, **church planting among unreached people**, and **one worldwide family**. Supporting copy never replaces [Protected Foundations](../brand/foundations.md)[^foundations].

## Altitude 1: The 5-Second Lead (Cold First Impression)

**Purpose:** Let a first-time reader understand immediately who leads the work, why Jesus Christ is central, and what distinguishes Global Fellowship.

- **Flagship Headline (Primary Across Web, Deck, Social & Print):** `When a cross-cultural missionary arrives, the local leader still leads.`
- **Short Travel Descriptor (15 words, for social bios, OG previews, and verbal intros):** `Christian fellowship planting churches in Jesus Christ among unreached people, led locally with cross-cultural teammates.`
- **Full Subhead:** `Global Fellowship is a worldwide Christian fellowship planting churches in Jesus Christ among unreached people, where local believers lead and cross-cultural missionaries serve alongside and under them.`

**5-Second Slot Routing:** Use the **Flagship Headline** on cold external surfaces (web hero, deck cover, OG cards, candidate briefs) where "who leads when an outsider arrives" is the immediate distinguishing question; whenever the Flagship Headline travels alone outside a known church context, require the **15-word Short Travel Descriptor** immediately beside it so Christian church planting and Jesus Christ stay explicit. Use the **Supporting Headline** below on church welcome sheets, prayer cards, and Bridge Project notes where shared belonging in Christ leads:

- **Supporting Headline:** `Brought near in Christ. Led by local churches.`
- **Supporting Subhead:** `Local church planters already speak the language. Since 1989, we have served together as one family under local leadership.`

## Altitude 2: The 30-Second Spoken or Written Introduction (58 Words)

> When a cross-cultural missionary arrives, the local leader still leads. Global Fellowship unites indigenous church planters and cross-cultural teammates under local leadership in Jesus Christ to plant local churches among unreached people. Start by praying or joining The Bridge Project with no financial ask. _"It is our vision to plant a Gospel witness within 30 minutes of everyone."_

## Altitude 3: The 2-Minute Spoken Explanation (~190 Words: Pastors, Candidates, and Thoughtful Readers)

> Picture a neighborhood where no local church gathers yet and neighbors rarely or never hear the Good News of Jesus Christ in their mother tongue. Many churches long to stand with unreached communities in ways that build lasting local leadership rather than short-term outside dependence.
>
> Before founding Global Fellowship in Auburn, California in 1989, Don and Kathy Oates served in Liberia under a local church leader. When cross-cultural sending began in 2012, uniting near-culture, Majority-World, and North American workers, that order stayed unchanged: when a cross-cultural missionary arrives, the local leader still leads.
>
> Our official mission states it plainly: _"We make disciples and plant new churches by empowering local leaders and sending cross-cultural missionaries to pioneer church-planting work among unreached people."_ That mission rests on three pillars: **Church Planting**, **Local Leadership**, and **Unreached People**.
>
> **Global coordinates. Regional contextualizes. Local leads.** Local ministries keep their own identity, regional teams guard worker health for singles and families under _Health Above Results_, and global staff keep financial books open before anyone gives.
>
> Join through prayer, two-way Bridge Project letters with no financial ask, listening-first visits, open-books giving, or exploring service.

**Speaker Verification Footnote (Print / Q&A Reference, Never Spoken Aloud as Parenthetical Clutter):** UPG scale per Joshua Project live dashboard (consulted November 2026; exact snapshot date pending `OVR-19`): roughly `3.6 billion` people across more than `7,200` unreached people groups (`<= 2%` evangelical Christian and `<= 5%` Christian adherents). 2025 figures: `584` workers served (reported as `"workers mobilized"`) across `43` countries per the 2025 Annual Report; `$5.73M` disbursed (`$5.82M` received) across Global Fellowship (`$2.08M` received / `$2.13M` disbursed) and affiliated entities MissionStream and BOLK per the 2025 Consolidated Statement of Activity (`$5.9M` field giving summarized in the 2025 Annual Report; entity scope and `$192.5k` 8% admin fee tracked in `OVR-01`, `OVR-03`, `OVR-16`)[^proof-2025].
