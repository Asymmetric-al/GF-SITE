# Global Fellowship Message Hierarchy and Altitude Ladder

Whether a reader has five seconds on a Sunday bulletin or twenty minutes in an elders' meeting, Global Fellowship's message stays anchored in **Jesus Christ**, **local leadership**, **church planting among unreached people**, and **one worldwide family**. Supporting copy never replaces [Protected Foundations](../brand/foundations.md)[^foundations].

## 1. Altitude 1: 5-Second Lead

- **Flagship Headline (Cold External Primary):** `When a cross-cultural missionary arrives, the local leader still leads.`
- **Short Travel Descriptor (15 words, bios/OG/intros):** `Christian fellowship planting churches in Jesus Christ among unreached people, led locally with cross-cultural teammates.`
- **Full Subhead:** `Global Fellowship is a worldwide Christian fellowship planting churches in Jesus Christ among unreached people, where local believers lead and cross-cultural missionaries serve alongside and under them.`
- **Supporting Headline (Warm Church / Bridge Surfaces):** `Brought near in Christ. Led by local churches.`
- **Supporting Subhead:** `Local church planters already speak the language. Since 1989, we have served together as one family under local leadership.`

_Slot rule:_ Pair the Flagship Headline with the 15-word Short Travel Descriptor whenever it travels outside a known church setting so Jesus Christ and church planting stay explicit. Reserve the Supporting Headline for warm church/Bridge surfaces paired with its subhead.

## 2. Altitude 2: 30-Second Introduction (58 Words)

> When a cross-cultural missionary arrives, the local leader still leads. Global Fellowship unites indigenous church planters and cross-cultural teammates under local leadership in Jesus Christ to plant local churches among unreached people. Start by praying or joining The Bridge Project with no financial ask. _"It is our vision to plant a Gospel witness within 30 minutes of everyone."_

## 3. Altitude 3: 2-Minute Spoken Explanation (~190 Words)

> Picture a neighborhood where no local church gathers yet and neighbors rarely or never hear the Good News of Jesus Christ in their mother tongue. Many churches long to stand with unreached communities in ways that build lasting local leadership rather than short-term outside dependence.
>
> Before founding Global Fellowship in Auburn, California in 1989, Don and Kathy Oates served in Liberia under a local church leader. When cross-cultural sending began in 2012, uniting near-culture, Majority-World, and North American workers, that order stayed unchanged: when a cross-cultural missionary arrives, the local leader still leads.
>
> Our official mission states it plainly: _"We make disciples and plant new churches by empowering local leaders and sending cross-cultural missionaries to pioneer church-planting work among unreached people."_ That mission rests on three pillars: **Church Planting**, **Local Leadership**, and **Unreached People**.
>
> **Global coordinates. Regional contextualizes. Local leads.** Local ministries keep their own identity, regional teams guard worker health for singles and families under _Health Above Results_, and our Home Office publishes annual financial statements and answers questions before anyone gives.
>
> Join through prayer, two-way Bridge Project letters with no financial ask, listening-first visits, giving, or exploring service.

_Speaker Q&A footnote (do not speak as parenthetical clutter):_ UPG research context (Joshua Project, consulted November 2026, pending `OVR-19`): ~3.6B people across 7,200+ UPGs (`<= 2%` evangelical and `<= 5%` Christian adherents). 2025 figures: `584` workers served (`"workers mobilized"`) across `43` countries (`2025 Annual Report`); `$5.73M` distributed (`$5.82M` received) on our 2025 consolidated Statement of Activity (`$2.08M` received / `$2.13M` distributed directly through Global Fellowship, Inc.; `$5.9M` field giving in the Annual Report; `OVR-01`, `OVR-03`, `OVR-16`)[^proof-2025].
