# Audience Messaging Matrix

Use this matrix before drafting any page, email, presentation, or handout. Every row links to its full audience profile in `knowledge/audiences/`[^source-synthesis][^narrative].

| Audience | Core Hesitation | Lead Message | Primary Proof | Respectful Next Step |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Local church planters & indigenous leaders** ([profile](../audiences/local-church-planters.md)) | _"Will an international fellowship take over our name, dictate methods, or expose our people?"_ | **Keep your voice, your name, and your leadership.** GF stands beside you with regional care and practical support without absorbing your ministry. | 1989 Liberia founding posture; Level 3 Endorsement & Level 4 Zero-Attribution (`GF-TPL-16`, `GF-TPL-19`); Chiang Mai hub (2024); revocable consent & editorial veto. | Personal conversation with a regional director before any formal agreement. |
| **2. Cross-cultural missionary candidates** ([profile](../audiences/cross-cultural-candidates.md)) | _"Where do I fit if local leaders lead? Will I or my family burn out chasing numbers?"_ | **Come to learn, serve under local leaders, and endure in health.** No solo heroics and no inflated quarterly quotas. | 2012 sending order; _Health Above Results_ (singles & families); regional care + independent facilitator; clear funding expectations (`GF-TPL-07`, `GF-TPL-24A`). | Read the Candidate Brief and schedule an exploratory first call (`GF-SCR-01`). |
| **3. Prospective support staff** ([profile](../audiences/support-staff-candidates.md)) | _"Is operations, finance, IT, or care real mission work, and how does support-raising work?"_ | **Removing barriers so church planters thrive is shared mission.** Support staff belong to the same fellowship and Statement of Faith. | Support-raised Home Office & regional staff model; transparent role briefs (`GF-TPL-13`, `GF-TPL-24A`) stating funding status upfront. | Review role expectations and speak with Operations (`info@globalfellowship.org`). |
| **4. Sending churches & pastors** ([profile](../audiences/sending-churches-and-pastors.md)) | _"Are they doctrinally sound, financially honest, and respectful of local churches?"_ | **Historic Christian faith, local church multiplication, and multi-year friendship** where local hosts can say no to visits. | Verbatim 8-article Statement of Faith (`GF-TPL-22`; internal 10-point `OVR-15B`); 2025 statements & Candid `8043461`; two-sided Host Agreement (`GF-TPL-23`) & B&W insert (`GF-TPL-17`)[^proof-2025]. | Share the Elders' Vetting Packet (`GF-TPL-22`) or schedule an elders' call ([church copy](../marketing/copy-churches-and-events.md)). |
| **5. Individual financial & prayer partners** ([profile](../audiences/individual-givers-and-prayer-partners.md)) | _"Where does my money go, why aren't they ECFA-accredited, and am I connecting with real people?"_ | **Transparent stewardship inside real relationships.** Read open books, ask hard questions, and pray through honest seasonal updates. | Published 2024/2025 Statements of Activity; candid ECFA rationale; scoped 2025 reporting (`OVR-01`, `OVR-16` open); Donor Lifecycle Kit (`GF-TPL-21`); candid worker letters[^proof-2025]. | Request 2025 statements (`finance@globalfellowship.org`), request a prayer card, or designate a gift. |
| **6. Families, small groups & newcomers** ([profile](../audiences/families-small-groups-and-newcomers.md)) | _"I don't know missions jargon and I'm not ready for a big commitment."_ | **Start with friendship and prayer, with zero financial ask.** Exchange letters with a church planter through The Bridge Project. | Bridge Project & Bridge Kids zero-money policy (`GF-TPL-08`, `GF-TPL-20`, `GF-TPL-25`); plain-language glossary; `<60`-word mobile Notes (`GF-TPL-14`). | Request a Bridge Project match (`info@globalfellowship.org`) or bring a prayer card to dinner. |
| **7. Existing workers & longtime supporters** ([profile](../audiences/existing-workers-and-supporters.md)) | _"Can I find usable templates, private care, or direct human gift support fast?"_ | **You are known here, and you never have to perform perfection.** Access zero-Figma Office starters, confidential care, and real humans in Home Office. | Six Office starters (`brand-kit/office/`); standalone confidential Member Care (`GF-TPL-18`); Day-1 onboarding (`GF-TPL-24B`); direct Finance contact (`finance@`, `1 866 984 5622`, `GF-TPL-13`). | Open your tier-matched Office starter, email Finance directly, or reach your regional care lead. |
