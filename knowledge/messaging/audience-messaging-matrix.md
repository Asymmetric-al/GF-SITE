# Audience Messaging Matrix

Use this matrix before drafting any page, email, presentation, or handout. Every row links to a fuller audience profile in `knowledge/audiences/`[^source-synthesis].

| Audience | Core Decision / Hesitation | Lead Message (Tailored) | Primary Credible Proof | Respectful Next Step |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Local church planters & indigenous ministry leaders** ([profile](../audiences/local-church-planters.md)) | _"Will joining an international fellowship take over our ministry, dictate our methods, or put our people at risk?"_ | **Keep your voice, your name, and your leadership.** Global Fellowship exists to stand beside you with regional pastoral care, prayer, and practical help without absorbing your ministry. | 1989 Liberia founding posture; local-first endorsement and zero-attribution co-branding options; regional care hubs (Chiang Mai opened 2024); revocable consent & editorial veto. | Start a personal conversation with a regional director before any formal agreement. |
| **2. Cross-cultural missionary candidates** ([profile](../audiences/cross-cultural-candidates.md)) | _"Where do I fit if local leaders lead? Will an agency burn me or my family out chasing numbers?"_ | **Come to learn, serve under local leaders, and endure in health.** You will not carry pioneer work alone or be measured by inflated quarterly counts. | 2012 cross-cultural sending order; _Health Above Results_ (singles and families); regional care teams; clear distinction between GF support and personal responsibility. | Read the Candidate Expectations Brief and schedule an exploratory first call (starter note in `brand-kit/exports/candidate-first-call-message.txt`). |
| **3. Prospective support staff** ([profile](../audiences/support-staff-candidates.md)) | _"Is administration, finance, IT, or member care real mission work, and what does support-raising expect of me?"_ | **Removing barriers so church planters thrive is shared mission.** Support staff belong to the same fellowship, hold the same Statement of Faith, and serve regional and local teams directly. | Support-raised Home Office & international staff model; clear role descriptions across Member Care, Global Engagement, Operations, and Finance. | Review open role expectations and speak with Operations or Co-Executive Directors. |
| **4. Sending churches & pastors** ([profile](../audiences/sending-churches-and-pastors.md)) | _"Are they theologically sound, financially honest, and respectful of local churches rather than running short-term tourism?"_ | **Historic Christian faith, local church multiplication, and long-term partnership.** Your congregation can pray, send, host visiting planters, and take listening-first trips where the local host can say no. | Verbatim 8-article public Statement of Faith (`P008`, `GF-TPL-22`; 10-point internal reconciliation `OVR-15`); 2025 Annual Report & published Statements of Activity; Candid `8043461`; two-sided Exposure Trip host agreement (`GF-TPL-23`) & half-letter B&W bulletin (`GF-TPL-17`). | Share the Elders' Vetting Packet (`GF-TPL-22`) with your missions team or schedule an elders' conversation ([vetting copy](../marketing/copy-church-vetting-and-host-agreement.md)). |
| **5. Individual financial & prayer partners** ([profile](../audiences/individual-givers-and-prayer-partners.md)) | _"How is my gift actually used, why aren't they ECFA members, and am I joining real people or a machine?"_ | **Transparent stewardship inside real relationships.** Read our open financial statements, ask hard questions freely, and stand with workers through prayer and honest updates. | Open 2024/2025 Statements of Activity; candid ECFA explanation; scoped 2025 field-giving reporting (`OVR-01`, `OVR-16` logged transparently); Donor Lifecycle & Receipt Kit (`GF-TPL-21`); real worker letters including quiet seasons. | Request 2025 statements (`finance@globalfellowship.org`), request a prayer card, or choose a worker/project designation. |
| **6. Families, small groups & newcomers to missions** ([profile](../audiences/families-small-groups-and-newcomers.md)) | _"I don't know missions jargon and I'm not ready for a big commitment. How can our household begin?"_ | **Start with friendship and prayer, with zero financial ask.** Through The Bridge Project, your family or small group exchanges letters with a church planter and learns their world. | Bridge Project & Bridge Kids explicit zero-money policy (`GF-TPL-20`, `GF-TPL-25`); plain-language glossary explaining Christian & missions terms; simple first-letter guide (`GF-TPL-08`) and `<60`-word mobile prayer Notes (`GF-TPL-14`). | Sign up for The Bridge Project (`info@globalfellowship.org`) or bring a printed prayer card to your dinner table. |
| **7. Existing workers & longtime supporters** ([profile](../audiences/existing-workers-and-supporters.md)) | _"Can I find clear care, editable templates, recurring-gift support, or a real human fast when I need help?"_ | **You are known here, and you never have to perform perfection.** Access ready Word/PowerPoint starters (public, safe-by-default zero-logo restricted, Level 4 local-only EN & Arabic RTL, and Finance manual fill-in donor receipts), mobile Notes, separate Day-1 onboarding (`GF-TPL-24B`), standalone confidential member care (`GF-TPL-18`), and direct Home Office handoffs without bureaucracy. | Direct contacts (`finance@`, `info@`, `1 866 984 5622`; planned `globalfellowship.org/give`, `OVR-21`); six OOXML starters (`brand-kit/office/`); Donor Lifecycle & Receipt Kit (`GF-TPL-21`); Supporter Quick Reference (`GF-TPL-13`); standalone 2-page Consent Form (`GF-TPL-15`); standalone Member Care Journal (`GF-TPL-18`). | Open the Office starter or Supporter Quick Reference, email Finance directly, or reach your regional care lead. |
