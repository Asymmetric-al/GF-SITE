# Audience Profile: Sending Churches and Pastors

Pastors, elders, and missions committees guard where their congregation invests prayer, people, and tithes. They spot theological fuzziness, short-term mission tourism, and financial spin immediately.

## Real Questions & Hesitations They Bring

1. _"Is Global Fellowship doctrinally orthodox and committed to real local churches (Word, baptism, Lord's Supper, elders), or is it vague humanitarian relief?"_
2. _"How are our church's gifts stewarded, and why is Global Fellowship not currently ECFA-accredited?"_
3. _"Will an Exposure Trip or partnership strengthen local leaders without creating outside dependence?"_
4. _"Can our congregation hear firsthand from a church planter without a high-pressure fundraising pitch?"_

## What Must Be Clear Immediately

- **Unmistakable historic Christian ecclesiology:** Present the canonical 8-article public Statement of Faith reading copy (`foundations.md:76-83`; internal 10-point alignment tracked in `OVR-15B`) upfront alongside how discipleship grows into locally led churches[^source-synthesis].
- **Radical financial candor:** Share published 2025 Statements of Activity and Financial Position, Candid profile `8043461`, and plain-spoken ECFA explanation ([2025 Proof Ledger](../messaging/proof-ledger-2025.md))[^proof-2025].
- **Listening-first church trips:** Exposure Trips require a two-sided Host Agreement (`GF-TPL-23`) giving local hosts unconditional authority over purpose, size, media rules, and timing (`"not this year"` is always respected).
- **Ready Sunday tools:** Supply a 2-minute livestream-safe pulpit script, a pure-black half-letter bulletin insert (`GF-TPL-17`), and a livestream-safe collective prayer slide (`GF-TPL-10` Slide 5).
