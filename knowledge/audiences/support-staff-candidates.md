# Audience Profile: Prospective Support Staff

Global Fellowship's global and regional support teams remove administrative, financial, communication, and care barriers so church planters can stay focused on making disciples and planting churches[^source-synthesis].

## Who They Are & What They Carry

- Skilled Christian professionals in member care, finance, administration, donor care, global engagement, hospitality, project development, communications, and IT who want their vocation to serve unreached church planting directly.
- Looking for authenticity: they want to know whether support roles are treated as second-class "back office" jobs or valued members of the fellowship, and they need plain honesty about support-raised compensation.

## Real Questions & Hesitations They Bring

1. _"How does an operations, finance, or member-care role genuinely connect to planting churches among unreached people?"_
2. _"Are staff expected to raise personal support, and how does Global Fellowship help me build a prayer and financial team?"_
3. _"What is the actual working culture of a lean Home Office and international team?"_

## What Must Be Clear Immediately

- **Same fellowship, same calling:** Support staff affirm the same Statement of Faith, share the same seven values, and directly sustain local and regional church-planting teams.
- **Honest funding & regional hub expectations:** State plainly where Home Office (Meadow Vista, remote) or regional hub roles (including Chiang Mai) require personal prayer and financial support development, shared funding, or operational funding rather than burying that detail late in the interview process[^source-synthesis].
- **Worked Q&A: "What happens if personal support takes longer than planned?":** Answer candidly on the first call without shaming or vague reassurance: confirm target monthly budget, coaching check-ins, realistic ramp milestones, and whether a phased start, bi-vocational bridge, or pause is available if full funding is not reached by the initial target date (exact transition-runway and minimum-funding policies remain pending Mobilization and Operations confirmation under `OVR-23`).
- **Caregivers receive care:** Member-care, regional hub, and Home Office staff are explicitly included in _Health Above Results_ and mutual accountability.
- **Role-status honesty & intake routing:** Never imply an archived job description PDF guarantees an active current vacancy; route exploratory inquiries directly to `info@globalfellowship.org` (`Subject: Operations & Support Roles`) or `1 866 984 5622` for an unhurried first fit conversation (`GF-TPL-13`).
