# Audience Profile: Prospective Support Staff

Global Fellowship's global and regional support teams remove administrative, financial, communication, and care barriers so church planters stay focused on making disciples and planting churches[^source-synthesis].

## Who They Are & What They Carry

- Skilled Christian professionals in member care, finance, administration, donor care, global engagement, hospitality, project development, communications, and IT.
- Want to know whether support roles are valued inside the fellowship and need plain honesty upfront about support-raised compensation.

## Real Questions & Hesitations They Bring

1. _"How does an operations, finance, or member-care role genuinely connect to planting churches among unreached people?"_
2. _"Are staff expected to raise personal support, and what happens if support takes longer than planned?"_
3. _"What is the working culture of a lean Home Office and international team?"_

## What Must Be Clear Immediately

- **Same fellowship, same calling:** Support staff affirm the same Statement of Faith, share the same seven values, and receive the same _Health Above Results_ care standard.
- **Honest funding & hub clarity:** State upfront whether a Home Office (Meadow Vista / remote) or regional hub role (including Chiang Mai) is support-raised, shared, or operations-funded[^source-synthesis].
- **Candid support-gap Q&A:** On the first call, review target monthly budget, coaching check-ins, realistic ramp milestones, and whether a phased start, bi-vocational bridge, or pause is possible (exact runway norms pending Mobilization/Operations confirmation under `OVR-23`).
- **Role-status honesty & routing:** Never imply an archived job description guarantees a live vacancy; route exploratory inquiries to `info@globalfellowship.org` (`Subject: Operations & Support Roles`) or `1 866 984 5622` (Pacific business hours).
