# Audience Profile: Existing Workers and Longtime Supporters

An organization's brand lives or dies with the people already inside it. Existing workers (`584` workers served across `43` countries in the 2025 Annual Report; source label `"workers mobilized"`) and recurring supporters need clarity, respect, and tools that work in real life with minimal friction[^source-synthesis].

## Real Needs & Pain Points

- **Existing workers:** Need fast, simple self-service templates for quarterly prayer letters and prayer cards that work in Google Docs or Word without a graphic designer; need honest permission to write a quiet or rest-season update without performing victory; need clear, private member-care contacts (inclusive of single workers) and simple security step-down rules when local conditions tighten.
- **Existing supporters:** Need direct, frustration-free ways to manage recurring gifts, update payment details, confirm tax receipts, read clear updates, and reach a real human (`finance@globalfellowship.org`, `support@globalfellowship.org`, `1 866 984 5622`, `P.O. Box 1, Meadow Vista, CA 95722`).

## What Must Be Clear Immediately

- **Zero performance pressure:** _Health Above Results_ shows up visually and verbally through an approved quiet-month update template (including a valid 1-sentence rest update), a standalone confidential Member Care check-in journal (`GF-TPL-18`, separated cleanly from public stationery `GF-TPL-12`), and `<60`-word mobile prayer Notes (`GF-TPL-14`).
- **Zero-Figma everyday usability:** Six genuine editable OOXML Office starters (`brand-kit/office/worker-prayer-letter-public-starter.docx`, safe-by-default zero-logo `worker-prayer-letter-restricted-starter.docx`, Level 4 zero-attribution `local-only-meeting-sheet-starter.docx`, native RTL Arabic `local-fellowship-brief-arabic-rtl-starter.docx`, Finance manual fill-in `donor-receipt-and-redesignation-starter.docx`, and `global-fellowship-deck-starter.pptx` with embedded Slide 1 street photo) plus SIL OFL Google Fonts (`Plus Jakarta Sans` + `Source Serif 4`, with `Calibri`/`Georgia` offline Office fallbacks) and transparent PNG lockups work directly in Word, PowerPoint, LibreOffice, and Google Workspace.
- **Direct human access & recurring-gift handoffs:** Recurring supporters update payment methods, designations, or receipts directly via `finance@globalfellowship.org` or `1 866 984 5622` (online giving portal destination at `globalfellowship.org/give` remains pending confirmation under `OVR-21`), inspect open books without automated bots, and reference the Donor Lifecycle & Receipt Kit (`GF-TPL-21`) and Supporter Quick Reference (`GF-TPL-13`, Page 2).
