# Audience Profile: Existing Workers and Longtime Supporters

Global Fellowship's brand lives or dies with the people already inside it (`584` workers served across `43` countries in the 2025 Annual Report, source label `"workers mobilized"`, plus recurring supporters)[^source-synthesis].

## Real Needs & Pain Points

- **Existing workers:** Need zero-Figma templates for quarterly letters and prayer cards in Word or Google Docs; explicit permission for quiet/rest-month updates without performing victory; confidential member care inclusive of singles; and simple step-down rules when local conditions tighten.
- **Recurring supporters:** Need direct human help to update recurring gifts, confirm tax receipts, read candid updates, and reach Home Office without bots (`finance@globalfellowship.org`, `info@globalfellowship.org`, `1 866 984 5622` during Pacific business hours, `P.O. Box 1, Meadow Vista, CA 95722`; see [Inbound Triage](../policies/inbound-triage-and-content-ops.md) for monitored legacy aliases).

## What Must Be Clear Immediately

- **Zero performance pressure:** _Health Above Results_ shows up through the rest-month / 1-sentence update option (`GF-TPL-04`), standalone confidential Member Care Journal (`GF-TPL-18`, strictly separated from public stationery `GF-TPL-12`), and `<60`-word mobile Notes (`GF-TPL-14`).
- **Zero-Figma everyday usability:** Six editable OOXML Office starters in `brand-kit/office/` (Tier 1 public letter, Tier 2/3 zero-logo restricted letter, Level 4 English and Arabic RTL sheets, Finance donor receipts, and Tier 1 `.pptx` deck) work directly in Word, PowerPoint, LibreOffice, and Google Workspace.
- **Direct human gift care:** Recurring supporters update payment methods (PCI-safe), designations, or receipts via `finance@globalfellowship.org` or `1 866 984 5622` (online portal destination pending `OVR-21`).

## Key Deliverables

- `brand-kit/office/` (all 6 editable starters), `GF-TPL-04` (Worker Letters), `GF-TPL-14` (Mobile Notes), `GF-TPL-15` (Consent Form), `GF-TPL-18` (Confidential Care Journal), `GF-TPL-12` (Stationery & Travel Cards), `GF-TPL-13` (Supporter Quick Reference), `GF-TPL-21` (Donor Lifecycle & Receipts), `GF-TPL-24B` (Day-1 Onboarding & Signatures), `GF-GDE-02` & `GF-GDE-03`.
