# Audience Profile: Families, Small Groups, and Newcomers to Missions

Many Christians want their children, dinner tables, and small groups to care about the global Church, yet feel intimidated by unfamiliar missions jargon (`UPG`, `10/40 window`, `support-raising`) or pressured by immediate financial appeals.

## Real Questions & Hesitations They Bring

1. _"What does 'unreached' actually mean, and why is church planting different from charity?"_
2. _"Is there a real way our family or small group can get to know a church planter and pray together without getting put on an aggressive donation list?"_
3. _"What should we write in a first letter, and what questions might accidentally put a worker in danger?"_

## What Must Be Clear Immediately

- **Zero-jargon hospitality:** Explain Christian and missions terms warmly on the page ([Terminology and Glossary](../messaging/terminology-and-glossary.md)).
- **The Bridge Project (& Bridge Kids):** Real two-way correspondence and prayer with a local church planter, with **explicitly zero financial expectation**[^source-synthesis]. Give families concrete first-letter prompts and simple, calm safety tips so writing feels joyful and easy.
- **Tangible table habits:** A 4x6 prayer card built to sit beside dinner plates or on a refrigerator door without looking like a charity flyer.
