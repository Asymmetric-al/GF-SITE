# Audiences

Dedicated, source-grounded audience profiles capturing real decisions, hesitations, proof needs, and deliverables across all seven Global Fellowship participant groups (`type: Audience`).

- [Local Church Planters and Indigenous Ministry Leaders](local-church-planters.md) - Indigenous and near-culture church planters discerning partnership, autonomy, editorial voice, and security.
- [Cross-Cultural Missionary Candidates](cross-cultural-candidates.md) - Singles, couples, and families discerning service alongside and under local leaders.
- [Prospective Support Staff](support-staff-candidates.md) - Operations, member-care, finance, IT, and engagement professionals considering support-raised service.
- [Sending Churches and Pastors](sending-churches-and-pastors.md) - Pastors, elders, and missions committees evaluating doctrine, stewardship, trips, and long-term partnership.
- [Individual Financial and Prayer Partners](individual-givers-and-prayer-partners.md) - Thoughtful individuals seeking transparent stewardship and genuine relational connection.
- [Families, Small Groups, and Newcomers to Missions](families-small-groups-and-newcomers.md) - Households and groups seeking plain-language understanding and zero-money entry via The Bridge Project.
- [Existing Workers and Longtime Supporters](existing-workers-and-supporters.md) - Current workers needing self-service templates and private care, plus recurring supporters needing direct service.
