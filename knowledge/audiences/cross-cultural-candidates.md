# Audience Profile: Cross-Cultural Missionary Candidates

Since 2012, Global Fellowship has sent cross-cultural missionaries (North American and Majority-World senders alike) to pioneer church planting alongside and under local leadership[^source-synthesis].

## Who They Are & What They Carry

- Singles, couples, and families sensing a call toward unreached communities, alongside their sending pastors and elders evaluating agencies carefully.
- Younger disciples (Gen Z and Millennial) who often navigate student debt or bi-vocational work, ask candid questions about colonial baggage and mental-health care, and want a human conversation before a long online application.
- Wary of rigid bureaucratic agencies on one side and isolated independent sending without pastoral care on the other.

## Real Questions & Hesitations They Bring

1. _"What does serving under local leadership look like day to day? Who sets priorities and resolves conflict?"_
2. _"Is there real, lasting care for single workers and missionary kids, or will we be pressured to chase big numbers?"_
3. _"What does Global Fellowship handle, and what personal responsibilities (language learning, support development, daily rhythms) belong to me?"_
4. _"How does theological unity work across denominational backgrounds?"_

## What Must Be Clear Immediately

- **Humble posture from day one:** Cross-cultural workers (including bi-vocational and South-to-South teammates) come to learn language deeply and serve alongside and under local leaders, honoring the 1989 Liberia founding posture.
- **Health Above Results is concrete:** Regionally led care addresses spiritual, emotional, physical, financial, and family health for singles, couples, children, and teenagers, with confidential referral routes outside a direct supervisor's line[^source-synthesis].
- **Honest personal agency & funding clarity:** GF handles administration, donor care, equipping, and regional care; workers take active responsibility for personal support development (or bi-vocational pathways), language study, and healthy rhythms[^source-synthesis].
- **Historic faith, peace on nonessentials:** Clear affirmation of the historic evangelical Statement of Faith (`8` public articles; full internal `10`-point doctrinal questionnaire reviewed candidly during candidacy, `OVR-15B`) with unity across denominational backgrounds.
