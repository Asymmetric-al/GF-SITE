# Audience Profile: Cross-Cultural Missionary Candidates

Since 2012, Global Fellowship has sent cross-cultural missionaries (both North American and polycentric senders such as Latin American teammates) to pioneer church planting alongside and under local leadership[^source-synthesis].

## Who They Are & What They Carry

- Singles, couples, and families sensing a call toward unreached communities, alongside their sending pastors and elders who are evaluating agencies carefully.
- Younger (Gen Z and Millennial) disciples often discover missions first through short-form mobile video or trusted friends, navigate student debt or remote/bi-vocational work realities, ask direct questions about colonial baggage and mental-health care, and expect community-first discernment before filling out a long online application.
- Often wary of two extremes: rigid bureaucratic agencies that treat workers like cogs, or unstructured independent sending that leaves families isolated without pastoral care.

## Real Questions & Hesitations They Bring

1. _"What does serving under local leadership actually look like day to day? Who sets priorities and resolves conflict?"_
2. _"Is there real, lasting care for single workers and missionary kids, or will we be pressured to chase big numbers?"_
3. _"What does Global Fellowship handle, and what personal responsibilities (language learning, support development, daily rhythms, long-term planning) belong to me?"_
4. _"How does theological unity work across denominational backgrounds?"_

## What Must Be Clear Immediately

- **Humble posture from day one:** Cross-cultural workers (whether North American or South-to-South Majority-World senders, including bi-vocational and professional-skills teammates serving church-planting goals) come to learn language deeply and serve alongside and under local leaders, following Don and Kathy Oates's Liberia posture prior to founding Global Fellowship in 1989.
- **Health Above Results is concrete:** Regional teams provide culturally attuned care for spiritual, emotional, physical, financial, and family health, explicitly inclusive of single workers, couples, children, and teenagers, with confidential referral routes outside a direct supervisor's line[^source-synthesis].
- **Honest personal agency & funding clarity:** Global Fellowship provides administration, donor care, leadership, equipping, and care, while workers take active responsibility for personal support development, language study, clear communication, and healthy rhythms[^source-synthesis].
- **Shared historic faith, gracious on nonessentials:** Clear affirmation of Global Fellowship's historic evangelical Statement of Faith (`8` public articles on `globalfellowship.org`; full internal `10`-point doctrinal questionnaire reviewed candidly during candidacy, `OVR-15`) paired with peace across denominational differences.
