# Audience Profile: Individual Financial and Prayer Partners

Individual supporters give and pray out of love for Christ and desire to stand beside faithful church planters. Many feel fatigued by manipulative fundraising mailers, fabricated urgency, and impersonal donor portals.

## Real Questions & Hesitations They Bring

1. _"Where does my money actually go, and how are Home Office staff and card fees paid?"_
2. _"Why are some missionaries listed only by initials without photos? Can I trust them and know how to pray specifically?"_
3. _"How is giving to a building or project connected to real local churches?"_
4. _"Will I only hear from Global Fellowship when they want money?"_

## What Must Be Clear Immediately

- **Support belongs inside fellowship:** Giving frees a church planter (indigenous or cross-cultural) to devote full attention to evangelism, discipleship, and forming a new church where no established congregation yet exists to support a pastor[^source-synthesis].
- **Open Books honesty without spin:** Explain exact scoped field giving alongside separately reported 2025 funding lines beside Home Office operations (`$251.6k`: `$192.5k` 8% admin fee with entity/fund scope pending `OVR-01`, `$40.2k` Home Office & fee-coverage gifts, `$33k` rent, and `$14.5k` WMN Inc., never claiming `"0% overhead"` or unqualified `"100%"` headlines while `OVR-01` remains open)[^proof-2025].
- **Equal dignity for restricted workers:** Explain warmly why names and photos are withheld for safety and give concrete, meaningful prayer prompts so supporters feel deeply connected even without a photo.
- **Projects serve people and churches:** Always explain any project designation in relation to the local church-planting team and ministry purpose it serves.
