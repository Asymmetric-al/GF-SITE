# Ethical Storytelling, Dignity, and Revocable Consent Policy

Every story Global Fellowship shares belongs first to the people who lived it. Public stories must honor their dignity, protect their safety, preserve their voice, and remain completely separate from private pastoral care[^source-synthesis].

## 1. Five Non-Negotiable Storytelling Standards

1. **Local agency and voice first:** Tell stories in church planters' own words whenever safe and consented. Depict local leaders teaching, hosting, praying, and deciding, never as helpless recipients.
2. **No poverty or persecution spectacle:** Never use downward camera angles, desaturated "sadness grading," weeping close-ups of children, or sensationalized persecution headlines to drive giving.
3. **Honest space for hardship and silence:** Never pressure workers for monthly miracle reports. A slow month of language study, grief, waiting, or rest (even a single-sentence rest update or No-Send Option) is faithful ministry.
4. **Never fabricate or pass off composites as real people:** Zero invented quotes, uncredited stock portraits representing field workers, or AI-generated human faces. Any composite layout specimen awaiting fresh consent (`GF-TPL-05`, `OVR-06`) must be labeled unmistakably as a composite specimen inside standalone PDFs.
5. **Local editorial veto:** The contributing church planter or regional leader holds final review over headline, wording, photo selection, caption, translation, and channel scope, with unconditional authority to revise or decline without penalty.

## 2. Plain-Language Revocable Consent (`GF-TPL-15`)

Use the standalone 2-page accessible **Revocable Consent Form** (`brand-kit/templates/revocable-consent-form.html`, `GF-TPL-15`). Store signed forms or logged verbal consents **strictly outside Git** in Global Fellowship's private authorized archive (`OVR-14`; final legal retention wording pending `OVR-22`). Every consent conversation must be plain, translatable, and confirm five points:

- **Exact scope:** Exact text, Identity tier (full name, alias, initials, or collective description), Location tier, and visual permission (photo-free, non-identifying detail photo, or eye-level portrait/video).
- **Independent channel opt-ins (never bundled):** Check each channel separately: printed prayer card / **authorized encrypted chat or password-gated portal (all tiers; required for Tier 3 electronic updates)** / **vetted private email (Tier 1-2 only; NEVER Tier 3)** / Annual Report PDF (Tier 1-2 only; **NEVER Tier 3**) / public website (Tier 1-2 only; **NEVER Tier 3**) / **recorded offline church service (Tier 1-2 only; NEVER Tier 3)** / **livestreamed public church service (Tier 1 personal opt-in or Tier 1 anonymous collective prayer ONLY; NEVER Tier 2/3 personal details)** / public social media or short-form video (Tier 1 only; **NEVER Tier 2 or Tier 3**)[^security].
- **24-month expiry & minors:** Permission expires automatically for fresh review every **24 months** (or sooner if local safety shifts). Identifiable minors require a parent or guardian co-signature.
- **Zero coercion:** Declining, choosing photo-free, or withdrawing consent never affects fellowship standing, pastoral care, or support.
- **Withdrawal contacts (printed directly on `GF-TPL-15`):** Regional Director (Page 1 encrypted contact), `info@globalfellowship.org`, `executivedirectors@globalfellowship.org` (urgent escalations), or `1 866 984 5622` (Pacific business hours; off-hours/weekend urgent withdrawals go via Regional Director encrypted channel + `executivedirectors@` pending `OVR-17`).

## 3. Executing a Consent Withdrawal

When anyone withdraws or steps down permission for a story, photo, name, or video, execute the single canonical 4-stage procedure (including `COPY-WITHDRAW-01` and `COPY-RETIRE-01` scripts and 24-hour Tier 2/3 non-acknowledgment escalation) in [On-Call Consent Withdrawal Runbook](consent-withdrawal-runbook.md)[^withdrawal].
