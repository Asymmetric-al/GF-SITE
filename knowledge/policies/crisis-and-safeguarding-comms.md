# Crisis, Security Incident, and Safeguarding Communications Playbook

Global Fellowship communicates in crisis with **speed, truthfulness, zero speculation, and primary allegiance to household safety and human dignity**[^security].

**Hard Escalation Boundary (`OVR-17`):** Global Fellowship does **not** publish an invented `security@` address or unmonitored 24/7 hotline[^open-verification]. Until Operations activates and tests a dedicated off-hours security route, urgent escalations move strictly through:

1. **Field-side primary:** The worker's **Regional Director** (private encrypted internal directory stored outside Git, `OVR-14` / `OVR-20`).
2. **Home Office escalation:** **Co-Executive Directors** (`executivedirectors@globalfellowship.org`), backed by `info@globalfellowship.org` and `1 866 984 5622` (Pacific business hours).

---

## 1. Detention, Interrogation, or Emergency Evacuation (Security Silence Protocol)

**Governing rule:** **Zero public posting. Zero mass email. Zero fundraising.** Speculation on social media, prayer chains, or newsletters can endanger detained believers and local hosts[^security].

1. **Freeze external channels within 30 minutes:** Suspend scheduled newsletters, social posts, and worker updates tied to the region. If a public webpage exists on `globalfellowship.org`, execute Stage 2 of [On-Call Consent Withdrawal Runbook](consent-withdrawal-runbook.md) immediately and confirm live removal in a private browser window[^withdrawal].
2. **Single spokesperson:** Only the Co-Executive Directors (with the Regional Director and security counsel) authorize every written word.
3. **Notify vetted inner circle privately (encrypted/phone only):** Inform immediate family and sending-church elders first with explicit instructions to pray privately at home and keep names, cities, and circumstances off livestreams, bulletins, and social media.
4. **Lock-screen-safe private prayer message (only if cleared by family & Regional Director):**

> **Subject:** `Quiet prayer request for our fellowship`  
> **Body:** `Friends, we are asking a small circle to pray steadily today for wisdom, protection, and calm endurance for members of our fellowship navigating a sudden disruption. Out of care for everyone involved, we are keeping details private and offline. Please pray in your home, do not forward this note, and do not share names or regions online or in public services. We will write again once it is safe to share more.`

## 2. Sudden Medical Emergency or Bereavement

Model written updates on `GF-TPL-09` Template B (`COPY-CRISIS-01` in [Donors & Bridge Copy](../marketing/copy-donors-and-bridge.md)):

- **Lock-screen-safe subject:** Never put a diagnosis, death notice, or hospital location in the subject or preview text (`Praying this week with our friends in Christ`).
- **Family consent first:** Never share medical details, bereavement news, or financial-need asks without explicit consent from the household (or next of kin) and the Regional Director.
- **Zero initial appeal:** The initial pastoral note carries no donate button or fundraising ask. If the family later approves a practical care fund, send a separate calm follow-up only after clearance.

---

## 3. Consent Withdrawal & Emergency Content Removal

For any urgent or routine request to withdraw a story, name, quote, audio clip, or photo (including `COPY-WITHDRAW-01` and `COPY-RETIRE-01` scripts and 24-hour Tier 2/3 follow-up), execute the single canonical 4-stage procedure in [On-Call Consent Withdrawal Runbook](consent-withdrawal-runbook.md)[^withdrawal].

---

## 4. Data, Email, or Security Breach Notification

1. **Contain first:** Revoke compromised credentials, preserve logs, and determine whether Tier 2/3 field identifiers or donor data were exposed (note: `GF-SITE` never stores raw payment card numbers; processing stays with the external PCI-compliant processor).
2. **Check legal notice duties with counsel (`OVR-22`):** Confirm applicable state/federal notification timelines (including California residency rules given GF's Meadow Vista registration).
3. **Notify affected people plainly:** State what happened, what data was and was not exposed, concrete steps already taken, protective actions needed (including immediate security step-down if any Tier 2/3 link was exposed), and direct contact (`executivedirectors@globalfellowship.org` / `1 866 984 5622`).

---

## 5. Safeguarding, Abuse, Harassment, or Financial Integrity Concerns

- **How concerns are reported publicly (without inventing an unmonitored hotline):** Contact the **Co-Executive Directors** (`executivedirectors@globalfellowship.org` or `1 866 984 5622` Pacific business hours), or if the concern involves executive leadership, write directly to the **Board Chair** (Eric Bergen) at `P.O. Box 1, Meadow Vista, CA 95722` (Attn: Board Chair, Confidential)[^source-synthesis].
- **Immediate safety & civil duty:** Where imminent danger or child harm is suspected, prioritize emergency services and mandatory civil reporting alongside pastoral care; never handle abuse allegations informally or ask a vulnerable reporter to mediate with an alleged perpetrator.
- **Zero retaliation & confidentiality:** Acknowledge receipt promptly, protect reporter confidentiality consistent with safety and legal duty, and keep investigation records strictly outside public repositories and general channels.

## 6. Public & Media Non-Comment Holding Statements

Staff, regional teams, and partner churches must **never** speak to journalists on the record about field incidents, sensitive regions, or personnel crises without Co-Executive Director approval.

### Standard Media Holding Statement (`COPY-MEDIA-HOLD-01`)

> _"Global Fellowship cares deeply about the safety and well-being of the communities and coworkers we serve alongside. Out of respect for the privacy and security of individuals and local congregations, we do not comment publicly on specific personnel, regional locations, or security matters. Media inquiries may be directed to our Meadow Vista office at `executivedirectors@globalfellowship.org` or `1 866 984 5622`."_

### Public Question on Sensitive Regions (Church / Social Script)

> _"Thank you for asking and caring about local believers in that region. Because public details can create real risk for households serving there, we do not share specific cities, worker names, or local security details publicly. If you would like to pray faithfully offline, write to `info@globalfellowship.org` and our team will gladly share how our prayer fellowship works."_
