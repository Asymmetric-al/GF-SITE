# Contemporary Missions Context and Sector Landscape

Credible primary missiological definitions and fair peer-sector context informing Global Fellowship's positioning and statistical hygiene.

## 1. Global Gospel Access Definitions & Statistical Hygiene

Always cite source, reporting date, and statistical definition whenever using global missions figures. Never blend definitions across Joshua Project, Gordon-Conwell, and Lausanne.

- **Unreached People Group (UPG, Joshua Project):** A people group with **2% or fewer** evangelical Christians **and** **5% or fewer** Christian adherents overall[^joshua-project]. Live tracker (consulted Nov 2026; exact publish snapshot date must be pinned before external print/web use under `OVR-19`): roughly `17,300` tracked groups, of which roughly `7,200` (~42%), representing ~`3.6 billion` people (~43.5% of world population), are classified as unreached.
- **Unengaged, Unreached People Group (UUPG, Joshua Project):** A narrower, continuously updated subset of UPGs with **no known active efforts to establish self-sustaining evangelical churches** (do not quote a fixed count without pinning the live snapshot date under `OVR-19`)[^joshua-project].
- **Global South demographic center (Gordon-Conwell CSGC):** Multi-decade research documents that the clear majority of global Christians live in the Global South (Africa, Asia, Latin America, Oceania). Describe this shift qualitatively in public copy; keep approximate series figures (~`2.67B` Christians of ~`8.2B` people, ~32%) as internal research context until exact annual edition/table pinning under `OVR-19`[^gordon-conwell].
- **Urbanization, migration & polycentric sending (Lausanne _State of the Great Commission_):** Absolute unreached headcount has grown due to population concentration, urbanization, and migration, while Majority World churches lead as primary senders ("from everywhere to everyone")[^lausanne-sotgc]. Represent **urban apartment fellowships and diaspora church planting** alongside rural contexts.
- **Retired heuristic ban:** Never repeat unqualified `` `3% of missionaries / 1% of Christian giving` `` slogans; primary critiques show they routinely undercount indigenous, near-culture, and bi-vocational Majority World planters. See [Retired Claims](../messaging/retired-claims.md).

## 3. Category Clichés Retired & GF's Truthful Distinction

Leave behind interchangeable category wallpaper (`"reaching the unreached"`, `"transforming nations"`), donor-as-savior framing (`"$X/month native missionary"`), heroic Western-only missionary myths, unverified stats, and worn visual clichés (globes with crosses, pity portraits).

Global Fellowship's credible distinction (never claiming exclusivity over faithful peer ministries) rests on the documented combination rooted in Don and Kathy Oates's pre-1989 Liberia posture[^source-synthesis]: **one shared family where local leaders still lead when cross-cultural workers arrive**, **integration without assimilation**, **Health Above Results** (inclusive of singles), **reciprocal zero-ask fellowship** (The Bridge Project & local-host veto Exposure Trips), and **lean, open-books stewardship** (with all open Finance gates tracked in [Open Verification Register](open-verification-register.md)).
