# Global Fellowship Brand Character and Values in Action

Brand Integrity (_The Brand IDEA_) requires that what people encounter externally matches how people are treated internally. Global Fellowship's brand personality is drawn directly from its seven documented values and everyday operating culture, never invented from marketing adjectives[^source-synthesis].

## The Five Enduring Character Traits

1. **Convicted and Humble:** Direct about Jesus Christ, Scripture, repentance, baptism, and local church formation, yet quick to pray first, listen across cultures, and hold ministry methods with open hands[^foundations][^source-synthesis].
2. **Warm Through Practical Attention:** Warmth shows up in remembering names, keeping promises, replying to letters, hosting meals, checking on a single worker during a quiet season, and giving clear answers rather than cheerful slogans[^source-synthesis].
3. **Purposeful Yet Patient:** Urgent about the roughly 3.6 billion people living in unreached people groups where 2% or fewer are evangelical Christians and 5% or fewer are Christian adherents (Joshua Project, consulted November 2026; pending `OVR-19`), yet patient with the years required to learn a language, disciple believers, and grow elders who sustain healthy local churches[^source-synthesis].
4. **Candid About Cost and Health:** Honest about loneliness, spiritual opposition, grief, financial boundaries, and organizational limits. Celebrates faithful rest as readily as visible fruit.
5. **Inviting Without Pressure:** Guided by Global Fellowship's own documented standard of _"relational warmth, sound judgment, and persistence without pressure"_[^source-synthesis].

## Seven Core Values Translated Into Communication Behavior

Each of Global Fellowship's seven core values carries a specific behavioral standard for how the brand speaks, designs, and treats people[^source-synthesis]:

| Core Value | What It Requires in Communication & Design | What It Explicitly Forbids |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Gospel first, infrastructure follows** | Explain buildings, businesses, training, and projects strictly in relation to discipleship and local churches. Keep branding practical and affordable so infrastructure serves mission. | Turning a construction project, app, or brand refresh into the main hero of the story. |
| **2. Relationships are a priority** | Make space for genuine two-way connection (Bridge Project letters, listening-first Exposure Trips, personal follow-through). Honor spouses, children, and single workers equally when speaking of "family." | Treating supporters as transaction records or picturing "family" as exclusively married couples with children. |
| **3. Local leadership** | Give local leaders the opening voice, editorial approval, right to decline visits or photos, and top-level billing on co-branded partner pieces. | Headquarters publishing a story about a local ministry without the leader's review or speaking as if Western staff direct field methods. |
| **4. Humility** | Acknowledge dependence on God for every spiritual result. Credit local hosts, peer churches, and regional caregivers plainly. Hold discretionary methods lightly. | Triumphalist statistics, "only we do this" claims, or demeaning other Christian ministries. |
| **5. Health above results** | Include honest templates for quiet or restful seasons (plus a valid No-Send Option). Never pressure workers to manufacture dramatic stories or monthly wins to keep support active. Protect privacy in member care. | Turning a worker's personal struggle into fundraising drama or equating rest with lowered commitment. |
| **6. Biblical principle over policy** | Measure every method, story, and decision by Scripture before strategy or speed, backed by clear safeguarding, privacy, consent, and financial policies that protect people consistently. | Repeating the retired legacy claim (`no written policies`) that contradicts current safeguarding standards. |
| **7. Trust and transparency** | Date every number, footnote every scope limit, publish annual Statements of Activity openly, explain ECFA status plainly, and withhold unconfirmed figures until owners verify them. | Hiding consolidated accounting complexity, quoting undated numbers, or exaggerating overhead claims. |

## What "Family" Means (And Does Not Mean)

Across Global Fellowship's materials, **family** carries both spiritual and literal weight[^source-synthesis]:

- **Spiritually:** Belonging to one household in Christ (Ephesians 2:19) where workers and churches bear one another's burdens in prayer, candor, and encouragement across cultures.
- **Literally:** Guarding home life, marriage, children, teenagers, rest, and emotional endurance, while making **single workers** explicitly visible, included, and cared for without awkward family-only assumptions.
- **Boundary:** "Family" never means informality that excuses missing safeguarding standards, vague finances, or ignored boundaries. Healthy families keep clear commitments and safe routes to raise concerns outside a direct supervisor's line[^source-synthesis].
