# Global Fellowship Protected Foundations

These foundational statements are permanently protected. Neither the brand creation phase nor the future website phase may rewrite, optimize, reinterpret, rename, or replace them. Supporting headlines, explanations, narratives, and campaign copy must serve these foundations without altering or masquerading as revised formal statements.

## Official Mission Statement (Verbatim)

Global Fellowship's primary sources preserve two grammatical lead-ins with identical substantive wording:

- **Declarative primary form** (VVR Orientation Deck, p. 3; Core Values Master List)[^vvr-deck][^values-master]:
  > "We make disciples and plant new churches by empowering local leaders and sending cross-cultural missionaries to pioneer church-planting work among unreached people."
- **Infinitive public website form** (Home P001; Our Mission P004)[^website-archive]:
  > "To make disciples and plant new churches by empowering local leaders and sending cross-cultural missionaries to pioneer church-planting work among unreached people."

**Usage rule:** Quote the declarative form (`We make disciples...`) when Global Fellowship speaks directly in the first person, and the infinitive form (`To make disciples...`) only where a sentence structure specifically calls for an infinitive statement (for example, directly under a formal heading `Our Mission`). Never alter, compress, rearrange, or paraphrase a single word inside either quotation.

## Official Vision Statement (Verbatim)

Preserved identically across the Whitepaper (p. 3), Core Values Master List, and public website (Home P001 Mobile; Our Mission P004)[^whitepaper][^values-master][^website-archive]:

> "It is our vision to plant a Gospel witness within 30 minutes of everyone."

**Usage rule:** Always preserve this exact wording when presenting the formal vision statement. Always frame it honestly as an organizational **vision and prayerful longing**, never as a measured coverage claim or achieved geographic metric. Never attach clock graphics, timers, or coverage maps that imply universal 30-minute travel verification.

## The Three Pillars (Unchanged in Name, Meaning, and Number)

Global Fellowship's mission rests on exactly **three** pillars. They must never be reduced to two, expanded to four, renamed, or replaced[^vvr-deck][^values-master][^website-archive]:

1. **Church Planting:** Proclaiming Christ's death and resurrection, making disciples, baptizing believers, and gathering healthy, multiplying local churches.
2. **Local Leadership:** Empowering believers who already belong to the language, culture, and community to shepherd and multiply the church.
3. **Unreached People:** Communities with little or no access to the Gospel and no self-sustaining local church movement able to reach the wider group. _(Quantitative research context belongs strictly in footnotes or speaker Q&A: Joshua Project defines unreached people groups as 2% or fewer evangelicals and 5% or fewer Christian adherents; exact snapshot date tracked in `OVR-19`.)_

**Canonical Pillar Gloss Rule:** Reuse these exact one-line pillar glosses consistently across decks, guides, and public briefs so terminology never drifts between surfaces.

**Transparent source-variant note:** On the public homepage (P001), the three pillars appear as `Church Planting`, `Local Leadership`, and `Unreached People`. On the legacy pillars page (P006), the third pillar heading appeared as `Unreached Regions`, and internal synthesis materials (S1, S7) summarize the third pillar's scope as `Unreached people and places`[^website-archive][^vvr-deck]. Across the brand system, use **Church Planting**, **Local Leadership**, and **Unreached People** as the canonical pillar triad, and use `unreached places` or `unreached communities` only inside supporting prose without altering the formal triad.

## Doctrinal Foundation (Statement of Faith)

Global Fellowship's public website (`globalfellowship.org/statement-of-faith`, P008) publishes **eight articles** of historic evangelical faith, while internal orientation materials (VVR Orientation Deck, S1) reference a 10-point internal Statement of Faith affirmed by missionaries and staff (public vs. internal article count tracked for leadership reconciliation in `OVR-15`)[^vvr-deck][^website-archive].

**Public Statement of Faith: Normalized Reading Copy (`P008`, `globalfellowship.org/statement-of-faith`):** _(Normalized reading copy: wording matches `P008` word for word, with a single typographical double-period `cross..` at the end of Article 5 normalized to `cross.` for clean typography without altering meaning. Consult `P008` directly for exact source punctuation.)_

1. We believe that the writings of both Old and New Testaments were breathed out by God through the Holy Spirit, perfectly and without error, and are the final authority in all matters of faith and practice.
2. We believe in one true and living God the maker of heaven and earth, who exists eternally as three distinct persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.
3. We believe man was created in the image of God, to glorify God, and enjoy Him forever. But, because of Adam's sin, man lost his innocence and incurred the penalty of spiritual and physical death, so that all human beings are now born with a sinful nature, unable to please God, unable to satisfy God's just requirements, and are therefore separated from God, lost, and unable to save themselves.
4. We believe Jesus Christ is the only begotten Son of God, who was born of the virgin Mary, lived a life of perfect obedience, suffered under Pontius Pilate, was crucified, died, and was buried; and that on the third day He arose bodily from the tomb, ascended into heaven, and now sits at the right hand of the throne of God.
5. We believe salvation is in no way a work of man, but a gracious gift of God, received by faith, and made possible only by the substitutionary death of Jesus Christ on the cross.
6. We believe in one church, which is the body of Christ, and includes all those who have been born again by the Holy Spirit. We affirm that believers in Jesus Christ should be baptized, and should participate together in the Lord's Supper to remember His death until He comes.
7. We believe Jesus Christ is coming again in glory to receive His church to Himself, and to judge the world in righteousness.
8. We believe in the resurrection of the dead, the eternal punishment of the wicked, and the eternal blessedness of the redeemed.

Alongside clear historic Christian convictions, Global Fellowship expects workers to pursue unity across denominational differences and maintain humility on secondary, nonessential matters[^vvr-deck].
