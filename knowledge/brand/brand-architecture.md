# Global Fellowship Brand Architecture and Partner Adaptability

Global Fellowship operates as a **federated, polycentric fellowship** (`Global coordinates. Regional contextualizes. Local leads.`), not a top-down franchise[^source-synthesis]. Its brand architecture holds global coherence while giving autonomous local ministries full ownership of their own names and protecting workers in legally sensitive jurisdictions.

## Four-Level Partner Architecture

_(Naming discipline: Partner branding relationships use **Levels 1 through 4**; field security disclosure uses **Tiers 1 through 3** so staff never confuse branding autonomy with security disclosure.)_

| Level | Entities & Examples | Visual & Verbal Relationship | Naming & Mark Rule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Level 1: Master Brand** | **Global Fellowship** (Home Office, Global Engagement, international communications, annual reports, future website) | Preserved Cross-in-Oval mark + `Global Fellowship` vector-outlined `Plus Jakarta Sans` wordmark + Living Nearness v15.0 system (`Harbor Teal #0C525C` & `Kiln Clay #7E3119`, with `gf-lockup-horizontal-mono` Charcoal Ink mandatory on Candle Apricot surfaces and `gf-lockup-horizontal-black` on 1-color black copiers). | Always write **Global Fellowship** in full on first mention; use **GF** only in internal shorthand or compact tables, never replacing the full wordmark on public headers. |

### Worked Example: When Partner Level (1–4) and Security Tier (1–3) Diverge

Because **Partner Level** governs _whose organizational identity leads_ while **Security Tier** governs _what personal/location facts may be disclosed_, the two axes operate independently:

| Concrete Scenario | Partner Level (Who Owns It) | Security Tier (Disclosure Limit) | Exact Asset & Output | Approver for Each Axis |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Case A:** A cross-cultural household sent by Global Fellowship serves in a restricted city where public naming risks deportation. | **Level 1** (Master GF household) | **Tier 3** (Withheld identity & location) | Home-side card uses `GF-TPL-01` (cross-free tier-neutral front, GF mark on back only); quarterly letters use zero-logo `worker-prayer-letter-restricted-starter.docx`; updates go strictly to encrypted chats (`GF-TPL-14`). | **Level 1:** Home Office / Global Engagement. **Tier 3:** Field household + Regional Director + Security Reviewer (`GF-TPL-15`). |
| **Case B:** An autonomous regional church network in a semi-open province welcomes endorsement on church newsletters (`In fellowship with Global Fellowship`) while withholding elder surnames and exact towns. | **Level 3** (Local-First Endorsed) | **Tier 2** (Partial / alias & broad region) | Local ministry name leads at top with `gf-lockup-endorsement.svg` beneath on vetted Tier 2 newsletters; zero social media posting; travel/local handoffs use zero-logo `worker-prayer-letter-restricted-starter.docx`. | **Level 3:** Autonomous local ministry elders. **Tier 2:** Local elders + Regional Director (`GF-TPL-15`). |

### Level 3 to Level 4 Transition Protocol (Retiring Endorsed Materials)

When local NGO registration laws, tax rules, or community safety conditions tighten for a Level 3 endorsed local ministry, the local leader and Regional Director execute an immediate **Level 3 -> Level 4 step-down** without altering pastoral care or relational belonging:

1. Switch all future local printing immediately to `GF-TPL-16` (`local-only-zero-attribution-sheet.html`), `GF-TPL-19` (`arabic-first-local-brief.html`), `office/local-only-meeting-sheet-starter.docx`, `office/local-fellowship-brief-arabic-rtl-starter.docx`, and `office/worker-prayer-letter-restricted-starter.docx` (zero logo, zero GF name, zero brand font fingerprint).
2. Remove the `"In fellowship with Global Fellowship"` endorsement footer from local websites, social profiles, stationery, and digital templates, and clear cached public pages.
3. Update Home-side partner cards to `GF-TPL-01` (tier-neutral front, calm privacy care on back) and notify vetted sending churches calmly to retire older endorsed handouts while keeping private prayer active.

### Ministry Initiatives & Gatherings (Sub-Expressions of Level 1)

**The Bridge Project** (and **Bridge Kids**), **Exposure Trips**, **Faith on the Frontlines**, **Leadership Summit**, and **Training** are sub-expressions inside the Master Brand (never competing off-palette logos that fracture recognition). **The Bridge Project** carries a dedicated parent-anchored lockup (`gf-lockup-bridge-project.svg`: Cross-in-Oval mark + `The Bridge Project` + `Global Fellowship · Correspondence & Prayer`); other initiatives set titles in crisp `Plus Jakarta Sans` Bold paired with the Global Fellowship parent lockup and a concise descriptive subtitle.

## Sister Entities and Consolidated Financial Clarity (`MissionStream` & `BOLK`)

Global Fellowship's published annual Statements of Activity (`D042`, `D043`) consolidate **Global Fellowship (`GF`)** alongside affiliated ministries **MissionStream (`MS`)** and **`BOLK`** (external audit/review wording remains open pending Finance confirmation in `OVR-02`)[^source-synthesis][^verification-register].

- **Public brand separation rule:** Global Fellowship's brand system represents **Global Fellowship, Inc.** (501(c)(3), EIN `68-0214543`). Do **not** merge MissionStream or BOLK into Global Fellowship's narrative or imply they share identical public branding.
- **Stewardship transparency rule:** Wherever consolidated financial totals or annual statements are presented to donors, include a plain-English **Open Books** note explaining that published consolidated statements span Global Fellowship alongside affiliated entities (`MissionStream` and `BOLK`), pending Finance's final approved plain-language entity footnote (`OVR-03` in [Open Verification Register](../references/open-verification-register.md))[^verification-register].

## Partner Church Co-Branding Rules

When a sending or partner church promotes an Exposure Trip, a Faith on the Frontlines evening, or a Bridge Project small group:

- **The local congregation leads its own Sunday bulletin and stage slides.** Provide clean transparent PNG marks (`brand-kit/logos/png/`), pure-black half-letter inserts (`GF-TPL-17`), and editable Office starters (`brand-kit/office/`) so the church adopts Global Fellowship's voice without clashing with its own congregation identity.
- **Worked Joint-Event Heading (Regional Hub + Autonomous Local Church Co-Host):** When a Level 2 Regional Hub (such as Chiang Mai) and a Level 3 autonomous local church network co-host a training workshop or prayer evening, the **local congregation or church network leads the headline in its own language and script**, with the regional hub signing calmly below:
  - _Top primary title:_ `[Local Church Network Name] · Pastors & Church Planters Gathering`
  - _Subordinate co-host line below:_ `Hosted with Global Fellowship | Southeast Asia` (`gf-lockup-regional-hub.svg` or `gf-lockup-endorsement.svg`, never larger than 65% of the local network's mark height; omitted entirely if Level 4 zero-attribution applies).
- **Host-veto rule:** Whenever a church prepares slides or printed bulletins mentioning a specific worker or local partner, the worker's approved security tier governs what may appear on screen, especially if the service is recorded or livestreamed.

## Program Naming Note (`Faith on the Frontlines`)

`Faith on the Frontlines` is an active 2026 program name documented in Global Fellowship's coordinator job description[^source-synthesis]. Because `"Frontlines"` carries military overtones that conflict with Global Fellowship's non-militaristic security-safe vocabulary in restricted regions, this brand system retains the established name for North American home/church gatherings while flagging it for leadership discernment (`OVR-10` in [Open Verification Register](../references/open-verification-register.md)) rather than renaming an active ministry unilaterally[^verification-register].
