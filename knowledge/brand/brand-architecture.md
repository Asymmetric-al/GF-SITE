# Global Fellowship Brand Architecture and Partner Adaptability

Global Fellowship operates as a **federated, polycentric fellowship** (`Global coordinates. Regional contextualizes. Local leads.`), not a top-down franchise[^source-synthesis].

_Naming discipline:_ Partner branding ownership uses **Levels 1 through 4**; field security disclosure uses **Tiers 1 through 3**. Never conflate the two axes.

## 1. Four-Level Partner Architecture

| Level | Entities & Examples | Visual & Verbal Relationship | Naming & Mark Rule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Level 1: Master Brand** | **Global Fellowship** (Home Office, Global Engagement, international reports, website) | Preserved Cross-in-Oval mark + `Global Fellowship` wordmark + Living Nearness v15.0 system (`gf-lockup-horizontal-mono` Charcoal Ink on Candle Apricot; `gf-lockup-horizontal-black` on B&W copiers). | Write **Global Fellowship** in full on first mention; reserve **GF** for internal shorthand or compact tables. |
| **Level 2: Regional Hubs** | Southeast Asia (Chiang Mai hub), South/Central/East Asia, Europe, MENA, Americas teams | Master mark paired with regional pipe lockup (`gf-lockup-regional-hub.svg`) and local-script Noto typography. | Format: `Global Fellowship \| SOUTHEAST ASIA` (or approved local-script translation). Hubs may adapt tone, idioms, and vetted regional photography (`OVR-11`). |
| **Level 3: Autonomous Local Ministries (Endorsed)** | Indigenous church-planting ministries where public association is safe and welcome | **Local-First Endorsed Model** (`Integration Without Assimilation`). | Local ministry name, logo, colors, and photos lead at the top; Global Fellowship signs beneath using `gf-lockup-endorsement.svg` (`In fellowship with Global Fellowship`). |
| **Level 4: Zero-Attribution Option** | Local ministries where foreign-NGO laws, tax rules, or cross marks create legal or safety risk | **100% Local-Only Publishing** with identical relational belonging and pastoral care. | Publish strictly from standalone `GF-TPL-16`, `GF-TPL-19` (Arabic RTL), `local-only-meeting-sheet-starter.docx`, or `local-fellowship-brief-arabic-rtl-starter.docx` with zero GF mark, name, cross emblem, EIN, California address, brand fonts, or hidden metadata. |

### When Partner Level (1-4) and Security Tier (1-3) Diverge

| Scenario | Partner Level | Security Tier | Exact Asset & Approver |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cross-cultural household sent by GF in a restricted city | **Level 1** (Master GF) | **Tier 3** (Withheld) | Home-side card `GF-TPL-01` (cross-free front, mark on back); zero-logo `worker-prayer-letter-restricted-starter.docx`; encrypted `GF-TPL-14` twins. Approved by household + Regional Director (`GF-TPL-15`). |
| Autonomous local church network in a semi-open province welcoming endorsement without surnames/towns | **Level 3** (Endorsed) | **Tier 2** (Partial/Alias) | Local ministry leads with `gf-lockup-endorsement.svg` below on vetted newsletters; zero social posting; zero-logo restricted Word starter for travel/local handoffs. Approved by local elders + Regional Director. |

### Level 3 to Level 4 Transition Protocol

When local legal or safety risk tightens for an endorsed Level 3 ministry, execute an immediate step-down without changing pastoral care:

1. Switch all local printing to Level 4 masters (`GF-TPL-16`, `GF-TPL-19`, and Level 4 `.docx` starters) and zero-logo restricted letter starters.
2. Remove `"In fellowship with Global Fellowship"` from local sites, profiles, and templates, and clear cached pages.
3. Move home-side partner cards to `GF-TPL-01` and ask vetted churches calmly to retire older endorsed handouts.

### Ministry Sub-Expressions (Inside Level 1)

**The Bridge Project** (and **Bridge Kids**), **Exposure Trips**, **Faith on the Frontlines**, **Leadership Summit**, and **Training** sit inside the Master Brand rather than using competing logos. **The Bridge Project** has a dedicated parent-anchored lockup (`gf-lockup-bridge-project.svg`); other initiatives pair bold `Plus Jakarta Sans` titles with the parent lockup.

## 2. Sister Entities (`MissionStream` & `BOLK`)

Published annual Statements of Activity (`D042`, `D043`) consolidate **Global Fellowship (`GF`)** alongside affiliated ministries **MissionStream (`MS`)** and **`BOLK`** (`OVR-02`, `OVR-03`)[^source-synthesis][^verification-register]:

- **Brand separation:** This brand system governs **Global Fellowship, Inc.** (501(c)(3), `EIN 68-0214543`). Do not merge MissionStream or BOLK into Global Fellowship's public narrative.
- **Public financial copy boundary (Stakeholder Directive 2026-11-25):** When consolidated totals appear publicly, note that consolidated statements cover every ministry account administered by our Home Office and show Global Fellowship, Inc.'s own contribution totals, without naming MissionStream, BOLK, or internal QuickBooks affiliate labels in public copy (`OVR-03`). Internal Finance accounting remains documented in [2025 Proof and Stewardship Ledger](../messaging/proof-ledger-2025.md).

## 3. Partner Church Co-Branding & Program Naming

- **Local congregation leads Sunday media:** Supply transparent PNG marks (`brand-kit/logos/png/`), pure-black half-letter inserts (`GF-TPL-17`), and editable Office starters so sending churches keep their own sanctuary identity.
- **Joint-event hierarchy (Regional Hub + Autonomous Church):** The local congregation/network leads the headline in its own language; the regional hub signs beneath (`Hosted with Global Fellowship | [Region]`, mark height `<= 65%` of local emblem; omitted entirely under Level 4).
- **Host-veto & livestream rule:** The worker's cleared security tier always governs church slides, bulletins, and livestreams.
- **Program naming note (`Faith on the Frontlines`):** Retained for documented North American gatherings while logged for leadership review (`OVR-10`) because `"Frontlines"` carries military overtones inconsistent with restricted-region vocabulary[^verification-register].
