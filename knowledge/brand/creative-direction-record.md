# Creative Direction Decision Record

**Status:** Proposed Brand Candidate (`v15.0`, Living Nearness) awaiting explicit human approval. _(Full per-round council scorecards and chronological version changelog live in `docs/brand-council/council-review-record.md` and `knowledge/log.md` so daily brand retrieval stays concise.)_

## 1. Strategic Territory Decision

- **Chosen:** **Brought Near, Led Locally** (anchored in Don and Kathy Oates's Liberia service under a local pastor before founding Global Fellowship in Auburn, California in 1989, its 2012 cross-cultural sending expansion, and Ephesians 2:13 to 22 / 4:16)[^strategy].
- **Alternatives Considered:**
  1. _The Guest Chair_ (asking "Who leads when a missionary arrives?"): Preserved directly as our flagship headline (`"When a cross-cultural missionary arrives, the local leader still leads"`), while avoiding a chair metaphor that breaks in floor-seating cultures.
  2. _The Near Witness / Shared Threshold_: Integrated into the _Brought Near_ theological spine so Jesus Christ remains unmistakably the actor.
- **Tradeoff Accepted:** _Brought Near_ demands disciplined Christian clarity so nearness always points to Jesus Christ's death and resurrection and a gathered local church rather than vague social warmth. Enforced via Agency Grammar and required Christian substance at every altitude.

## 2. Visual Mark & Color Freedom Decision (Incorporating Explicit Stakeholder Directives)

- **Chosen:** **Preserved Global Fellowship Cross-in-Oval Mark (`140 x 101`) Redesigned in Harbor Teal, Kiln Clay & Warm Paper:** Following explicit stakeholder directives supplying Global Fellowship's official logo (2026-11-21) and confirming full color freedom (2026-11-22: zero obligation to keep legacy blue/yellow/gold), we retain the exact Cross-in-Oval silhouette vectorized cleanly onto a `140 x 101` grid while retiring the legacy blue-and-yellow palette (which created corporate retail/civic interference, muddy ochre shifts on paper, and national-flag hazards across Eastern Europe and Asia). We pair the mark with **Harbor Teal (`#0C525C`, `8.27:1` AAA)** + **Kiln Clay (`#7E3119`, `8.40:1` AAA, 2 Cor 4:7 earthenware warmth)** on Warm Paper (`#FAF7F2`), **Warm Paper (`#FAF7F2`)** + **Candle Apricot (`#F2B488`, `8.68:1` AAA)** on unified **Harbor Night (`#0B272E`)** dark grounds, **Charcoal Ink (`#16191C`, `9.80:1` AAA)** monochrome on Candle Apricot hospitality bands, and pure `#000000` for church office copiers, locked beside a vector-outlined **Plus Jakarta Sans Bold** wordmark[^visual].
- **Optical Scaling Discipline:** Unified 2-color `512px` circular avatars fill `68.75%` of tile width across light and dark social UIs; integer-pixel-snapped `16x16` optical favicons keep the inner negative-space cross sharp on both light and dark browser tabs.
- **Tradeoff Accepted & Mitigated:** The Cross-in-Oval mark gives immediate recognition and unmistakable Christian identity on public materials while requiring firm safety boundaries in sensitive nations. Mitigated strictly via our **self-contained Level 4 Zero-Attribution standalone master (`GF-TPL-16`)** (with inlined SIL OFL `Noto Sans` & `Noto Serif` WOFF2s), **zero-logo Restricted Word starter (`worker-prayer-letter-restricted-starter.docx`)**, **standalone Level 4 editable Word starter (`local-only-meeting-sheet-starter.docx`)**, unbranded travel cards (`GF-TPL-12`), standalone confidential Member Care Journal separation (`GF-TPL-18`), back-only mark placement on restricted prayer cards (`GF-TPL-01`), and cross-free **`architectural-frame-dark.svg`** headers on restricted fronts.
