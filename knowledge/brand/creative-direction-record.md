# Creative Direction Decision Record

**Purpose & Authority:** Historical rationale explaining _why_ the current working brand candidate (**Living Nearness v15.0**, `status: draft`, awaiting `OVR-15`) was chosen. For current operational rules, read [Brand on a Page](brand-on-a-page.md), [Strategy & Positioning](strategy-and-positioning.md), and [Visual Identity](visual-identity.md). Per-round council evaluation records live outside the OKF in `docs/brand-council/`.

## 1. Strategic Territory Chosen & Alternatives Rejected

- **Chosen (`Brought Near, Led Locally`):** Anchored in Don and Kathy Oates's pre-1989 Liberia service under a local pastor, the 2012 cross-cultural sending expansion, and Ephesians 2:13 to 22 / 4:16[^strategy].
- **Alternatives evaluated:**
  - _The Guest Chair_ (`"Who leads when a missionary arrives?"`): Kept as the flagship headline while dropping the literal chair metaphor because it breaks in floor-seating cultures.
  - _The Near Witness / Shared Threshold_: Folded into _Brought Near_ so Christ remains unmistakably the actor.
- **Tradeoff accepted:** _Brought Near_ requires strict Agency Grammar so warmth never crowds out Jesus Christ and local church planting[^foundations].

## 2. Visual Mark, Palette Freedom & Street Photography

- **Preserved mark silhouette, retired legacy palette:** Per explicit stakeholder directives supplying Global Fellowship's official logo (2026-11-21) and confirming full color freedom (2026-11-22), the `140:101` Cross-in-Oval mark silhouette was vectorized cleanly while retiring legacy blue-and-yellow (which caused retail/civic interference, muddy paper shifts, and national-flag hazards). **Harbor Teal (`#0C525C`) + Kiln Clay (`#7E3119`, 2 Cor 4:7 earthenware warmth) + Warm Paper (`#FAF7F2`)** paired with **Harbor Night (`#0B272E`) + Candle Apricot (`#F2B488`)** and **Source Serif 4 / Plus Jakarta Sans / SIL OFL Noto** replaced the legacy three-sans stack[^visual].
- **Mark safety tradeoff mitigated:** Keeping the Cross-in-Oval preserves unmistakable public recognition while requiring strict sensitive-context boundaries: back-only placement on Tier 3 cards (`GF-TPL-01`), zero-logo restricted Word starters, unbranded travel cards (`GF-TPL-12`), standalone confidential Member Care (`GF-TPL-18`), and zero-attribution Level 4 masters (`GF-TPL-16`, `GF-TPL-19`).
- **Street photography over generic illustrations:** Per explicit stakeholder direction (2026-11-22), 6 cohesive EXIF-stripped, zero-ShareAlike (`CC0 1.0` / `CC BY 2.0`) street photographs (`brand-kit/photography/`) replaced schematic vector illustrations on Tier 1 public surfaces, leaving non-figurative plates strictly as photo-free fallbacks.

## 3. Ethical Adaptation of Consulted Frameworks

Four nonprofit communication frameworks informed this system (consulted via official public excerpts and methodology overviews, never uncritically imported):

- **StoryBrand 2.0 (Donald Miller):** Adopted decision-journey clarity (tension, 3-step path, parallel direct/transitional invitations) while rejecting donor-as-hero saviorism; God acts first and local leaders remain active peers.
- **The Brand IDEA (Laidler-Kylander & Stenzel):** Applied **Brand Integrity** (matching lean ~$251.6k operations), **Brand Democracy** (empowering regional/local teams without Figma), and **Brand Affinity** (zero disparaging of peer ministries).
- **Brandraising (Sarah Durham) & Made to Stick (Heath & Heath):** Anchored experiential touchpoints strictly to organizational foundations, favoring concrete local scenes, unexpected truthfulness (zero-ask Bridge Project), and dated credibility.

Native regional review of the palette, Arabic wording (`الشيوخ`), and non-English proofs (`OVR-11`), ESV quotation permissions (`OVR-09`), physical 300dpi copier/CMYK press proofs (`OVR-18`), and leadership sign-off (`OVR-15`) remain open until confirmed by their owners.
