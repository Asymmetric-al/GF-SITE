# Global Fellowship Brand on a Page

## 1. Permanent Locked Foundations (Never Edit)

- **Mission Statement (declarative primary form):** `"We make disciples and plant new churches by empowering local leaders and sending cross-cultural missionaries to pioneer church-planting work among unreached people."`[^foundations]
- **Vision Statement:** `"It is our vision to plant a Gospel witness within 30 minutes of everyone."` _(Always frame as a prayerful longing, never a coverage metric.)_[^foundations]
- **Three Pillars (always all 3 together, unchanged):** **Church Planting**, **Local Leadership**, **Unreached People**.[^foundations]
- **Doctrinal text:** Verbatim 8-article public Statement of Faith reading copy lives in [Protected Foundations](foundations.md) (internal 10-point alignment tracked in `OVR-15B`).

## 2. Core Strategy: Brought Near, Led Locally

Global Fellowship is an evangelical church-planting fellowship uniting indigenous church planters and cross-cultural missionaries in Jesus Christ[^strategy]:

- **Founding order (1989 / 2012):** Before founding Global Fellowship in Auburn, California in 1989 (and expanding into North American cross-cultural sending in 2012), Don and Kathy Oates served under a local pastor in Liberia. One rule endures everywhere: **when a cross-cultural missionary arrives, the local leader still leads**.
- **Operating order:** `"Global coordinates. Regional contextualizes. Local leads."`
- **Local stop authority:** Local leaders edit or veto any story, photograph, translation, or visiting team request without penalty.
- **Theological anchor:** Ephesians 2:13 to 22 and 4:16 (ESV): Christ brings those once far off near into one household without erasing local identity.

## 3. Verbal Identity: Witness, Open Books, and Note

Write across **three native registers** governed by **Agency Grammar**[^verbal]:

- **Witness** (stories, prayer cards, invitations, Bridge Project, worker letters, member care): Specific, warm without sentimentality, candid about joy and hardship, naming Jesus Christ directly.
- **Open Books** (annual reports, financial statements, candidate briefs): Calm, dated, and plain about scope and decision rights.
- **Note** (under-60-word encrypted mobile cards, EN/TH/AR plain-text twins, 9:16 vertical video): Crisp, low-bandwidth, plain language.
- **Five daily rules:** (1) God acts first in spiritual fruit, never a giver's money. (2) Local leaders are active grammatical subjects. (3) Show concrete policy instead of self-praising adjectives. (4) Date every figure with its source scope in the same sentence. (5) Omit em dashes, military verbs, and agency clichés.

## 4. Visual Identity: Living Nearness v15.0

- **Mark, Palette & Typography:** Preserves the exact `140:101` Cross-in-Oval silhouette paired with the vector-outlined **Rector Medium** `"Global Fellowship"` wordmark across website logos (`OVR-12` license review), alongside **Harbor Teal**, **Harbor Night**, **Kiln Clay**, **Candle Apricot**, **Warm Paper**, and **Charcoal Ink** (proposed pending regional review `OVR-11`, with an **Ink-Neutral Fallback**), set in SIL OFL 1.1 **Source Serif 4**, **Plus Jakarta Sans** (`Nunito` on the public web build), and **Noto** global scripts. Exact lockup rules, tokens, and contrast ratios live in [Visual Identity](visual-identity.md).[^visual]
- **Photography & Photo-Free Parity:** Tier 1 public surfaces use consented local photography (`OVR-06`) or vetted non-ShareAlike documentary street photography (`brand-kit/photography/`, `GF-PHO-01..06`) rather than generic illustrations. Tier 2/3 restricted cards (`GF-TPL-01`), separately cleared Tier 1 anonymous collective livestream prayer (`GF-TPL-10` Slide 5, never Tier 2/3 personal updates), encrypted chat twins (`GF-TPL-14`), and Level 4 local handouts remain dignified, photo-free typographic pieces.[^visual]

## 5. Field Security & Truthfulness

- **Stricter tier governs:** Public (`GF-TPL-02`) and restricted (`GF-TPL-01`) prayer cards share one tier-neutral, cross-free front skeleton with zero silhouettes, blurred faces, or regional colors. Keep confidential Member Care (`GF-TPL-18`) separate from public stationery (`GF-TPL-12`). Use the branded Word starter for **Tier 1 only**, the zero-logo Word starter for **Tier 2/3**, and Level 4 starters (`GF-TPL-16`, `GF-TPL-19`) for zero-attribution ministries. Never post Tier 3 details on unencrypted email, social media, or livestreams.[^security]
- **Real or none & open gates:** Never fabricate quotes, stock faces, or AI portraits; keep every open item in [Open Verification Register](../references/open-verification-register.md) (`OVR-01` to `OVR-24`) honest until its owner confirms it.
