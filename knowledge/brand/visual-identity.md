## 1. The Mark: Preserved Cross-in-Oval Silhouette & Lockup System

### Preserved Geometry & Meaning

Traced mathematically from Global Fellowship's official logo master onto a `140 x 101` (`1.386:1`) precision vector grid, the emblem unifies three enduring realities in one compact form:

- **Enclosing oval/globe:** Two sweeping crescent arcs surround the center without closing out the world.
- **Interlocking G and F forms:** Crisp geometric 45-degree chamfered interior cuts shape the `G` and `F` initials without ornament.
- **Central negative-space cross:** The two opposing forms meet around an unmistakable open cross at the heart of the mark, keeping Jesus Christ central while distinct communities hold together.

## 5. Art Direction: Three-Path Photography Sourcing Rule & Warm Photo-Optional Parity

### Three-Path Photography Sourcing Rule (Resolving Street Photography + Field Consent)

The five real documentary photographs in Global Fellowship's supplied Brand Kit PDF (a pastor teaching in a modest room, local men baptizing believers in rivers and humble blue tubs, and a village church gathered under a bamboo shelter) establish Global Fellowship's genuine visual instinct[^source-synthesis]. Following explicit 2026-11-22 stakeholder direction (_"we want to use street photography rather than generic illustrations"_), every Global Fellowship surface selects from **three strictly separated photography paths** (never uncurated stock faces, never AI-generated people, and never generic vector illustrations on public storytelling surfaces):

1. **Path 1 (Locally Commissioned Consented GF Photography, Primary Long-Term Standard, `OVR-06`):** Hire local photographers from the host community first with paid credit lines; shoot eye-level at `1–2m` in natural daylight with local elders leading the frame; strip all EXIF/GPS/COM metadata; file signed standalone Revocable Consent (`GF-TPL-15`) strictly outside Git before publishing real portraits or ministry scenes (including before reusing archived Brand Kit PDF photos publicly).
2. **Path 2 (Vetted Non-Identifying, Zero-ShareAlike Documentary Street Photography, `brand-kit/photography/`, `GF-PHO-01..06`):** Where Tier 1 public brand storytelling needs real visual presence before Path 1 local commissions are cleared (`GF-GDE-01` Cover & Page 5 complete 6-frame vault sheet, `GF-TPL-05` Annual Report p. 2, `GF-TPL-10` & `.pptx` Slide 1, `GF-TPL-11` OpenGraph & `#card-photo-story`, `GF-SPEC-01/02`, and Tier 1 `GF-VID-01` Beat 2), draw strictly from the **6 cohesive, sRGB-normalized (`0xE2`), zero-EXIF/IPTC/COM, zero-ShareAlike (`4 CC0 1.0` + `2 CC BY 2.0`) documentary street photographs** in `brand-kit/photography/` (`gf-street-tea-table.jpg`, `gf-street-shared-bread.jpg`, `gf-street-vendor-hands.jpg`, `gf-street-neighborhood-dusk.jpg`, `gf-street-urban-balconies.jpg`, `gf-street-terracotta-alley.jpg`), audited for SHA-256, zero metadata, unified warm-neutral daylight house grade, and open non-copyleft Creative Commons license URLs in `brand-kit/photography/PHOTO-LICENSES.md` (with explicit contextual captions confirming the street scene does NOT depict a specific Global Fellowship worker or protected congregation). Reject ShareAlike copyleft licenses, neon signage, license plates, religious amulets, traffic cones, or tourist crowds (see the Editorial Rejection Log in `PHOTO-LICENSES.md`).
3. **Path 3 (Photo-Free Typographic & Architectural Parity, Default for Tier 2/3 & Level 4):** On restricted prayer cards (`GF-TPL-01`), Tier 1 anonymous collective livestream prayer (`GF-TPL-10` Slide 5; zero Tier 2/3 personal details, each broadcast requires service-specific clearance), encrypted mobile prayer notes & twins (`GF-TPL-14`: `exports/mobile-prayer-note-twin.txt`, `-thai-twin.txt`, `-arabic-twin.txt`), zero-`<img>` emails (`GF-TPL-08`/`09`), Level 4 handouts (`GF-TPL-16`/`19`), and 1-color church bulletins (`GF-TPL-17`/`20`), `Source Serif 4` typography and warm cross-free Harbor Night architectural framing carry full editorial dignity without any image.
