# Global Fellowship Brand Strategy and Positioning

This strategy governs discretionary brand choices across Global Fellowship. It supports and amplifies [Protected Foundations](foundations.md) and never alters or replaces the verbatim Mission Statement, Vision Statement, or Three Pillars[^foundations].

## The Strategic Insight: Brought Near, Led Locally

Contemporary Christian missions messaging often emphasizes **distance**: crossing oceans, entering distant frontiers, or sending resources across borders. Across public missions pages, two recurring emphases frequently appear[^missions-context]:

1. **Cross-Cultural Sending Emphasis:** Public messaging centered primarily on mobilizing outsiders to cross borders and initiate pioneer church-planting teams.
2. **Indigenous Funding Emphasis:** Public messaging centered primarily on channeling financial support to indigenous ministries abroad.

Without caricaturing any peer organization's internal field practices, Global Fellowship's own documented history and operating model unite both halves inside one shared family[^source-synthesis]:

- **Origin that proves the posture:** Before founding Global Fellowship in Auburn, California in 1989, Don and Kathy Oates served in Liberia **under a local church leader**. When Global Fellowship expanded into sending North American cross-cultural missionaries in 2012, it kept that exact order: **when a cross-cultural missionary arrives, the local leader still leads the work.**
- **Nearness built into the Vision:** Global Fellowship's locked Vision Statement counts minutes rather than continents (`"It is our vision to plant a Gospel witness within 30 minutes of everyone."`). Every unreached place is somebody's home, and the believers closest in language, history, and relationship are already near.
- **One worldwide family (`Global coordinates. Regional contextualizes. Local leads.`):** Near-culture (indigenous) church planters and cross-cultural missionaries belong to the same fellowship, cared for by regional teams, sharing mutual accountability while local ministries keep their own identity and contextual methods (`integration without assimilation`).

### Theological Spine (Ephesians 2:13 to 22 and 4:16)

In Ephesians 2:13 (ESV), Paul writes of believers who once were far off being **"brought near by the blood of Christ"** and built together into one household of God, **"joined and held together by every joint"** (Ephesians 4:16, ESV). _(Note: ESV is used provisionally pending leadership Bible translation confirmation in [Open Verification Register](../references/open-verification-register.md).)_

Three strategic truths follow directly from that divine passive:

1. **God is the actor.** Neither Global Fellowship nor any financial giver brings anyone near to God; Christ does. Donor-saviorism is dismantled at the theological root rather than merely edited out of sentences[^frameworks].
2. **Every supporter was once far off.** Churches in California, Asia, Europe, and Latin America meet on level ground before the cross, eliminating paternalism and turning partnership into reciprocal fellowship.
3. **Every joint stays distinct while holding the whole.** Local ministries do not lose their tongue, name, or culture when joining Global Fellowship.

## Positioning Statement (Internal Strategic Compass)

_(Internal strategy articulation only; never publish in place of the locked Mission Statement.)_

> For Christians, local churches, cross-cultural missionary candidates, and indigenous church planters who believe unreached communities deserve enduring, locally led churches rather than short-lived outside projects, **Global Fellowship** is a worldwide Christian church-planting fellowship uniting near-culture and cross-cultural workers under local leadership. Unlike traditional Western-centered sending agencies or arm's-length donor funding conduits, Global Fellowship practices one shared family where local ministries keep their identity, cross-cultural missionaries serve alongside and under local leaders, worker health is guarded above metrics, and global churches join through reciprocal prayer, friendship, and transparent stewardship.

## Five Concrete Reasons to Believe (Grounded in Primary Evidence)

1. **Demonstrated founding order (1989 and 2012):** Born from serving under a local leader in Liberia, then expanding into North American cross-cultural sending in that same servant posture 23 years later[^source-synthesis].
2. **Real operational polycentrism:** Most workers come from the regions where they serve, supported by regional leadership (including the Chiang Mai Southeast Asia hub opened in 2024) and South-to-South cross-cultural sending already active among public worker profiles[^source-synthesis].
3. **Reciprocity without a price tag:** The Bridge Project pairs households and small groups with local church planters for two-way correspondence and prayer with an explicit no-money policy; Exposure Trips put local hosts in charge of purpose and timing[^source-synthesis].
4. **Health Above Results as policy, not slogan:** Regionally led member care addresses spiritual, emotional, physical, financial, and family health, explicitly inclusive of single workers as well as couples and children, with safeguarding and privacy routes outside a direct leader's authority[^source-synthesis].
5. **Lean, candid stewardship:** Home Office staff raise personal support alongside field workers; annual Statements of Activity are published openly; non-membership in ECFA is explained plainly rather than hidden[^source-synthesis].

## Strategic Decision Rules ("Choose X Over Y")

A brand strategy is only useful when it resolves real Tuesday-afternoon decisions. Apply these fifteen trade-off rules across all writing, design, and planning[^frameworks]:

| When deciding between... | Choose this | Over this | Why it matters for GF |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Scale of story** | One named neighborhood, table, or congregation (when safe) | Abstract totals about "the nations" | Grounds church planting in human reality rather than charity scale. |
| **Voice on the page** | A local church planter's own cleared words | Headquarters summarizing on their behalf | Demonstrates local agency instead of merely describing it. |
| **Cross-cultural posture** | "Serving alongside and under local leadership" | "Going to reach and lead them" | Preserves GF's actual operating order since 1989. |
| **Proof presentation** | One dated, defined figure with its source year | An undated cumulative or blended headline | Protects pastoral and donor trust against reconciliation errors. |
| **Missing photograph** | Ordered fork: (1) on Tier 1 public pages/decks/social, choose a vetted EXIF-stripped documentary street photo (`brand-kit/photography/`) when a real neighborhood detail strengthens the story; (2) go straight to optical serif typography or cross-free architectural framing (`patterns/`) on Tier 2/3 restricted print, Level 4 local handouts, copiers, or when no curated frame fits the story | Stock portrait, AI face, silhouette, or blurred face | Protects dignity, keeps public work visually concrete, and ensures restricted workers never look second-class. |
| **Invitation posture** | Clear step with honest terms and no guilt | Countdown urgency or guaranteed-outcome math | Reflects "warmth, sound judgment, and persistence without pressure." |
| **Hard months** | Honest account of grief, waiting, or rest (even 1 sentence) | Forced victory narrative every month | Makes _Health Above Results_ credible to workers and supporters alike. |
| **Peer ministries** | Gratitude for the wider body of Christ | Comparing GF's overhead against "10-25% agencies" | Honors Brand Affinity and factual honesty. |
| **Regional cultural fit** | Local working language, idioms, and ink-and-paper fallback where needed | Imposing English idioms or sensitive color pairings uniformly | Keeps _integration without assimilation_ true in every region (`OVR-11`). |
| **Co-branding hierarchy** | Local ministry name & emblem leading first (Level 3) or standalone Level 4 zero-attribution | Putting Global Fellowship's logo above an autonomous local congregation | Protects local ownership and legal safety in host nations. |
| **Language & mobile parity** | Native-script first authoring (including Arabic RTL `GF-TPL-19`) paired with exact plain-text twins | English-only PDFs or image-only mobile updates | Ensures low-bandwidth regional teammates and supporters read with equal dignity. |
| **Safety vs. publicity** | Stepping down disclosure immediately when risk rises | Keeping a compelling story or video live for a campaign | Demonstrates that worker and congregation safety outrank marketing reach. |
| **Caveat clutter vs. clarity** | Maximum one concise inline qualifier plus one consolidated bottom proof-note | Repeating stacked parenthetical disclaimers inside every paragraph or card | Preserves honesty and contemporary editorial confidence without turning public copy into legal boilerplate. |
| **No-Send Option** | Send nothing when no cleared, truthful, and useful update remains | Manufacturing filler copy or rushing unreviewed field details to hit a send date | Protects whole-life health, truthfulness, and local stop authority above editorial calendars. |
| **Photography Sourcing & Channel Fork** | Consented local photography (`OVR-06`), vetted EXIF-stripped street photography (`brand-kit/photography/`) with honest contextual credit, or photo-free typographic parity | Generic vector illustrations on public pages, uncurated stock portraits, AI human faces, or non-consented worker photos | Honors real neighborhood life visually while protecting local dignity and field security across every channel. |

- **Decorative geometry hygiene:** Keep Living Aperture curves strictly inside open margins (`>= 16px` from live text and rules) rather than crossing headlines, footers, or card copy.

### Tie-Break Precedence & Edge-Case Governance

When two valid principles collide on a live deadline, resolve in this strict order:

1. **Life Safety & Field Security** (stricter Identity/Location axis always governs).
2. **Doctrinal Integrity & Locked Foundations** (Christ central; Mission, Vision, and Three Pillars unchanged).
3. **Local Stop Authority & Whole-Life Health** (local elders and affected households hold veto over stories, photos, and visits).
4. **Factual Honesty & Open-OVR Discipline** (never publish unverified figures or claim an Open OVR is confirmed).
5. **Communication Craft & Schedule** (never let a print or campaign deadline override points 1 through 4).

- **No-Local-Editor-Available Deadline Rule:** If a local elder or affected household cannot review and sign off on a story or translation before a publication deadline, **hold the story** and publish either a collective Scripture prayer focus or an already-cleared archive piece within its 24-month window. Silence or delay always outranks unapproved representation.
- **Sending-Church vs. Regional-Care Authority Conflict:** When a sending church asks for updates, travel, or public visibility that conflicts with a Regional Director's or local elders' safety or _Health Above Results_ assessment, **regional care and local stop authority govern disclosure and visits**, while Global Engagement speaks candidly with sending-church elders so trust stays intact.

### Worked Conflict-Resolution Example

**Scenario:** A generous partner church preparing its annual missions Sunday asks Global Fellowship for a short 45-second video clip and city map showing a worker in a Tier 3 restricted region so the pastor can project it on the sanctuary livestream.

**How the decision rules resolve it (keeping public broadcast and private channels strictly separate):**

1. **Field Security ceiling overrides promotional energy:** Because Tier 3 prohibits identifiable video, personal names, and geographic clues on any public broadcast, Global Engagement declines the identifiable video and map calmly without shame or drama.
2. **Option A (Public Livestream & Sanctuary Broadcast: Separating Channel Eligibility From Instance Clearance):** Only **photo-free collective formats** containing zero personal or regional identifiers are **channel-eligible** for a Tier 3-adjacent sanctuary livestream: Slide 5 of `GF-TPL-10` (calm Harbor Night typography with `"God knows them by name"`), the livestream-safe pulpit script (`GF-TPL-06`), and the pure-black half-letter bulletin insert (`GF-TPL-17`). Do **NOT** route the public contextual-photo video (`GF-VID-01`, `living-nearness-prayer-vertical.mp4`, which carries the Global Fellowship mark and a Tier 1 public street photograph in Beat 2) as a Tier 3 restricted or photo-free piece; `GF-VID-01` is strictly **Tier 1 Public Social / Lobby Display only**. Each specific broadcast instance still requires formal leadership clearance before release (`status: draft` specimens are never live-cleared). _(Individual Tier 3 household updates NEVER move into public video channels.)_
3. **Option B (Vetted Offline Small Groups & Encrypted Prayer Channels Only):** For personal household prayer off-camera, offer the photo-free printed Tier 3 prayer card (`GF-TPL-01`, cross-free front, mark on back, do-not-post notice) or the authorized zero-photo encrypted-chat **Note** (`GF-TPL-14` Retina PNG + EN/TH/AR plain-text twins `exports/mobile-prayer-note-twin.txt`, `-thai-twin.txt`, and `-arabic-twin.txt`) strictly inside vetted private groups, never projected on a livestream.
4. **Open-OVR Suppression Rule (Truthfulness Over Launch Speed):** Whenever a live URL, portal route, or financial schedule remains `Open` in [Open Verification Register](../references/open-verification-register.md) (for example, `OVR-21` donor portal routing or `OVR-01` admin-fee scope), **never assert that the item is confirmed** on any public sheet or print footer; route readers to the verified human email desk (`finance@globalfellowship.org` or `info@globalfellowship.org`) until the responsible owner closes the OVR gate in writing.
