# Global Fellowship Brand Strategy and Positioning

This strategy governs discretionary brand choices across Global Fellowship. It serves [Protected Foundations](foundations.md) and never alters or replaces the verbatim Mission Statement, Vision Statement, or Three Pillars[^foundations].

## 1. Strategic Insight: Brought Near, Led Locally

Public Christian missions messaging often splits into two patterns: mobilizing cross-cultural outsiders to cross borders, or funding indigenous ministries at arm's length[^missions-context]. Global Fellowship's documented history unites both halves inside one shared family[^source-synthesis]:

- **Origin that proves the posture:** Before founding Global Fellowship in Auburn, California in 1989, Don and Kathy Oates served in Liberia **under a local church leader**. When Global Fellowship began sending North American cross-cultural missionaries in 2012, that order stayed unchanged: **when a cross-cultural missionary arrives, the local leader still leads.**
- **Nearness in the Vision:** Global Fellowship's locked Vision Statement counts minutes rather than continents (`"It is our vision to plant a Gospel witness within 30 minutes of everyone."`). Every unreached place is somebody's home, and believers closest in language and relationship are already near.
- **One worldwide family (`Global coordinates. Regional contextualizes. Local leads.`):** Indigenous church planters and cross-cultural missionaries belong to one fellowship, cared for regionally while local ministries keep their own identity and methods (`integration without assimilation`).

### Theological Spine (Ephesians 2:13 to 22 and 4:16)

In Ephesians 2:13 (ESV, provisional pending `OVR-09`), believers once far off are **"brought near by the blood of Christ"** into one household of God, **"joined and held together by every joint"** (Ephesians 4:16, ESV):

1. **God acts first.** Christ brings people near to God, never Global Fellowship or a financial gift[^creative-record].
2. **Every supporter was once far off.** Churches everywhere meet on level ground before the cross, turning partnership into reciprocal fellowship.
3. **Every joint stays distinct.** Local ministries keep their own language, name, and cultural wisdom while belonging to the whole.

## 2. Internal Positioning Statement

_(Internal strategic compass only; never publish in place of the locked Mission Statement.)_

> For Christians, local churches, cross-cultural missionary candidates, and indigenous church planters who believe unreached communities deserve enduring, locally led churches rather than short-lived outside projects, **Global Fellowship** is a worldwide Christian church-planting fellowship uniting near-culture and cross-cultural workers under local leadership. Unlike Western-centered sending agencies or arm's-length funding conduits, Global Fellowship operates as one shared family where local ministries keep their identity, cross-cultural missionaries serve alongside and under local leaders, worker health is guarded above metrics, and global churches join through reciprocal prayer, friendship, and transparent stewardship.

## 3. Five Reasons to Believe (Source-Backed)

1. **Demonstrated founding order (1989 and 2012):** Formed after serving under a local leader in Liberia and expanded into cross-cultural sending in that same servant posture 23 years later[^source-synthesis].
2. **Real operational polycentrism:** Most workers come from the regions where they serve, backed by regional hubs (including Chiang Mai, opened 2024) and active South-to-South cross-cultural sending[^source-synthesis].
3. **Reciprocity without a price tag:** The Bridge Project pairs households with local church planters for two-way correspondence and prayer with an explicit no-money policy; Exposure Trips give local hosts unconditional veto over visits[^source-synthesis].
4. **Health Above Results in practice:** Regionally led member care addresses spiritual, emotional, physical, financial, and family health for singles, couples, and children, with confidential safeguarding routes outside a direct supervisor's line[^source-synthesis].
5. **Lean, candid stewardship:** Home Office staff raise personal support alongside field workers; annual financial statements are shared openly; ECFA non-accreditation is explained plainly rather than hidden[^source-synthesis].

## 4. Operational Decision Rules ("Choose X Over Y")

Apply these core trade-off rules across writing, design, and planning[^creative-record]:

| Decision | Choose This | Over This |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Scale of story** | One concrete neighborhood, table, or congregation (when safe) | Abstract totals about "the nations" |
| **Voice on the page** | A local church planter's own cleared words | Headquarters summarizing on their behalf |
| **Cross-cultural posture** | "Serving alongside and under local leadership" | "Going to reach and lead them" |
| **Proof presentation** | One dated, defined figure with its source year | Undated cumulative or blended headlines |
| **Missing photograph** | Consented local photo (`OVR-06`), vetted street photo (`brand-kit/photography/`) on Tier 1 public surfaces, or photo-free typographic parity | Generic illustrations, stock faces, AI faces, silhouettes, or blurred faces |
| **Invitation posture** | Clear step with honest terms and no guilt | Countdown urgency or guaranteed-outcome math |
| **Hard months** | Honest account of grief, waiting, or rest (even 1 sentence) | Forced monthly victory narratives |
| **No-Send Option** | Send nothing when no cleared, truthful, useful update exists | Filler copy or rushed unreviewed field details |
| **Peer ministries** | Gratitude for the wider body of Christ | Comparing GF overhead against "10-25% agencies" |
| **Regional cultural fit** | Working language, local idioms, and Ink-Neutral fallback (`OVR-11`) | Uniform English idioms or culturally sensitive colors |
| **Co-branding hierarchy** | Local ministry leading (Level 3) or Level 4 zero-attribution | Putting GF's logo above an autonomous local congregation |
| **Caveat discipline** | Maximum one concise inline qualifier plus one consolidated proof note | Stacked legal disclaimers inside every paragraph |
| **Safety vs. publicity** | Stepping down disclosure immediately when risk rises | Keeping a compelling story or video live for a campaign |

## 5. Tie-Break Precedence & Edge-Case Governance

When valid priorities collide, resolve in this strict order:

1. **Life Safety & Field Security** (stricter Identity/Location axis always governs).
2. **Doctrinal Integrity & Locked Foundations** (Christ central; Mission, Vision, and Three Pillars unchanged).
3. **Local Stop Authority & Whole-Life Health** (local elders and affected households hold veto over stories, photos, and visits).
4. **Factual Honesty & Open-OVR Discipline** (never publish unverified figures or claim an Open OVR is confirmed; route to `finance@globalfellowship.org` or `info@globalfellowship.org` while web portals remain pending in `OVR-21`).
5. **Communication Craft & Schedule** (deadlines never override points 1 through 4).

- **No-local-editor-available rule:** If a local elder or household cannot review a story or translation before deadline, **hold the story** and publish a collective Scripture prayer focus or an already-cleared archive piece within its 24-month window.
- **Sending-church vs. regional-care conflict:** When a church requests updates, travel, or public visibility that conflicts with a Regional Director's or local elders' safety or _Health Above Results_ judgment, **regional care and local stop authority govern**, and Global Engagement communicates candidly with sending elders.

### Worked Conflict Example: Sanctuary Livestream Request for a Restricted Worker

A partner church asks for a 45-second video clip and city map of a Tier 3 worker for its livestreamed missions Sunday.

1. **Decline identifiable media calmly:** Tier 3 bars identifiable video, names, and geographic clues from any public broadcast.
2. **Offer public-livestream-safe collective formats only:** Slide 5 of `GF-TPL-10` (`"God knows them by name"`), the livestream-safe pulpit script (`GF-TPL-06`), and the pure-black bulletin insert (`GF-TPL-17`), each cleared per service. Do **NOT** route the public contextual-photo video (`GF-VID-01`, `living-nearness-prayer-vertical.mp4`) as a Tier 3 or photo-free piece; `GF-VID-01` is strictly **Tier 1 Public Social / Lobby Display only**.
3. **Offer vetted offline prayer options:** For off-camera small groups, use the photo-free Tier 3 prayer card (`GF-TPL-01`) or encrypted-chat plain-text **Note** (`GF-TPL-14` EN/TH/AR twins), never projected on a livestream.
