> **Active Website Build (`website/build-hillclimb`, Round 29 Opportunities, Navigation, Photography & Financial Summary Refinement of Global Fellowship's Marketing Brand Kit + 7-Catalog Harmonized Craft + Full `globalfellowship.org` Content Parity + OKF Copy Compliance).**
>
> - **Approval boundary:** Global Fellowship's OKF knowledge bundle (`knowledge/`, `styles/GF/`, `52` concepts) and print/Office kit (`brand-kit/`) remain working candidates awaiting human leadership approval (`OVR-15`).
> - **Contemporary Brand Kit palette:** Luminous Alabaster Ivory (`#FCFAF6`) canvas, Gallery White (`#FFFFFF`) elevated cards, Warm Cream (`#F3EDE3`) chapter bands, delicate warm hairlines (`#E5DED2`), Deep Navy (`#122A3B`) ink and dark sanctuaries, Petrol Blue (`#08354D`) primary actions, Muted Gold (`#B28B61`) flat rules, Dark Bronze (`#614D29`) WCAG AA+ labels, Warm Deep Bronze (`#47361D`) Member Care plates, and unified `#0A1822` Deep Navy OS dark mode.
> - **Wordmark & unified typography:** All `"Global Fellowship"` website logos pair the `140:101` Cross-in-Oval mark with a vector-outlined **Rector Medium** wordmark (`src/lib/brand/rector-wordmark.ts`; author attribution tucked away at `/credits` alongside the ESV Bible translation notice rather than repeated in every page footer; commercial `Production Type` logo license confirmation tracked in `OVR-12`). `Source Serif 4` (`400..600`, weight-instanced for mobile speed) leads all display headlines, section headings (`.gf-display-section`), `/financials` KPIs, monumental numerals (`1989`, `47`), and sacred/human prose; `Nunito` (`400..700`, `27 KB` Latin WOFF2) handles navigation, kickers, card titles, body copy, and tabular data.

This document is the canonical design reference for `GF-SITE` (Global Fellowship public website frontend). Read it before work that materially affects visual design, layout, interaction, navigation, iconography, imagery, motion, or public content presentation.

## How to Read Evidence in This Document

Every statement in this document is categorized into one of four evidence levels so future agents do not mistake provisional code or vendor defaults for approved design choices:

- **Explicitly established:** Supported by an identifiable user instruction or accepted architecture decision.
- **Observed in the implementation:** Currently present in repository code, stylesheets, or assets without assuming final leadership approval (`OVR-15`).
- **Existing proposal, not approved:** Available option, completed brand candidate, or prior suggestion that must **not** be treated as formally leadership-approved until `OVR-15` closes.
- **Undecided or unknown:** Open creative or product question requiring user clarification or launch sign-off.

## 7. Design Quality and Verification Process

Design and UI work is judged by verifiable user outcomes:

- **Clarity and hierarchy:** Visitors immediately identify page purpose, reading order, headings, and primary actions without visual clutter or decorative chrome.
- **Coherent interaction:** Interactive widgets use a single behavior owner (`@ui-sdk/react`), support pointer, touch (`>= 44px` tap targets), keyboard navigation, visible focus states, and clean focus return.
- **Responsive stability:** Layouts work cleanly from narrow mobile (`320px`, `390px`) through desktop viewports with zero unintended horizontal overflow and zero CLS.
- **Accessibility:** Semantic HTML landmarks, single `<h1>`, meaningful accessible names, WCAG 2.1/2.2 A/AA contrast across light, dark, and standalone error routes, automated `@axe-core/playwright` scans, plus manual keyboard and print verification.
- **Purposeful motion:** Motion clarifies state changes, remains interruptible, avoids layout-thrashing properties, and honors `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.
- **Accurate, readable content:** Real source-backed copy, plain language without em dashes, checked against Vale `styles/GF/` rules and automated `foundations.test.ts` invariants.
- **Performance:** Production builds stay lean, verified via `bun run analyze` and isolated 3-run mobile and desktop Lighthouse CI runs (`bun run perf:lhci`) with all runs reported candidly.
