## 1. Passing Standard & Six Binary Release Gates

To pass and stop for human approval, the frozen candidate revision must satisfy **ALL FOUR** conditions simultaneously:

1. **Unrounded Council Mean >= 9.90 / 10.00** across all 228 independent scores (`6 x 38`) in the same complete round under Rubric v2.0.
2. **All Six Binary Release Gates (G1 through G6) PASS** independently of the mean (no average can excuse a gate breach):
   - **G1 Protected Foundations:** Byte-exact preservation of Global Fellowship's verbatim Mission Statement (both declarative and infinitive source forms), verbatim Vision Statement (`"It is our vision to plant a Gospel witness within 30 minutes of everyone."`), and exact Three Pillars (`Church Planting`, `Local Leadership`, `Unreached People`), never reducing the pillars to two and never presenting unauthorized paraphrases as formal statements (`bun run verify:brand`).
   - **G2 Truthfulness & Verification Integrity:** Zero fabricated testimonials, quotes, photos, financial outcomes, or fake `human:` OKF verification records; visible illustrative/specimen and pre-publication hold status surviving into standalone PDF exports; every statistic dated and sourced; unreconciled items (`Admin Fees - 8%` scope, `43` vs `47` countries, `1,254` vs `230` churches planted, `BOLK`, `OVR-15` public vs internal Statement of Faith, `OVR-16` consolidated vs report figures) withheld from unqualified headlines and logged with owners in `knowledge/references/open-verification-register.md`.
   - **G3 Field Security & Zero Mosaic Leakage:** Zero aggregated lists of restricted worker identifiers in this public repository; zero EXIF/PDF/filename/alt-text identifying leaks; zero region-coded colors or patterns on restricted materials; fully synthetic illustrative identity on the Tier 3 specimen card.
   - **G4 Mechanical Hygiene:** `bun run okf:validate` (`--strict`: 0 errors, 0 warnings), `bun run check:foundation` pass, `bun run check:links` (0 errors), `bun run check:prose` (including `styles/GF/` Vale rules across docs AND extracted template text matching every documented rule: 0 errors), SVGO clean, zero em dashes in public copy, zero token-sync drift across `tokens.json`, `brand-tokens.css`, `contrast-report.json`, and `knowledge/`.
   - **G5 Verified Accessibility:** Every permitted text/background and meaningful non-text token pair across **both light and dark semantic scopes** passes **WCAG 2.2 AA** programmatically at full unrounded precision (`>= 4.5:1` normal text, `>= 3.0:1` large text/UI; primary body text targeting `>= 7.0:1` AAA), plus zero serious/critical `axe-core` violations across all HTML templates, accessible PDF structure where supported, and verified grayscale copier readability.
   - **G6 Asset Provenance & Licensing:** Every declared and embedded typeface variant (including serif and sans script families embedded in PDFs) and every asset carries explicit verified licensing (SIL OFL 1.1 typefaces, original works created for Global Fellowship, or verified non-ShareAlike `CC0 1.0` / `CC BY 2.0` contextual documentary street photography logged in `brand-kit/photography/PHOTO-LICENSES.md` with full attribution URLs and non-congregation disclaimers) and a verified entry in `brand-kit/manifest.json` with matching SHA-256 checksums.
3. **Per-Criterion Floor & Min Transparency:** Every individual area's 6-model mean (`1` through `38`) must be **`>= 9.60`**, with min, median, and per-area means reported transparently alongside the overall unrounded mean. Any single score `< 9.0` triggers mandatory root-cause investigation before advancing.
4. **Honest Evidence Ceiling (Correct Withholding Rule):** Real human stakeholder approval, Finance confirmation, renewed photo/story consent, native regional-leader review, physical laser copier tests, and legal trademark clearance sit outside what six AI models can produce and must **never** be fabricated. A score of `9.9-10.0` explicitly means _"publication-ready for human approval within this AI brand-creation phase, verified directly on saved artifacts, rendered exports, and automated checks, with every human-only dependency honestly withheld from premature claims and logged with an owner in `open-verification-register.md`."_ Reward correct withholding; penalize premature assertion.

## 2. Scoring Scale, Contemporary Benchmark Requirement & Anti-Inflation Rules

- **0.0-4.9 (Broken / Gate Failure):** Omission, contradiction, altered protected foundation, fabricated claim, unsafe disclosure, or unusable asset.
- **5.0-6.9 (Generic / Paper-Only Draft / Dated & Safe):** Interchangeable, clichéd, stale, old-fashioned, incomplete, or documented only on paper without rendered proof. _(Hard cap: any visual, contemporary-taste, accessibility, or production area `#22-#28, #30, #32, #36, #38` without direct rendered PNG/PDF inspection is capped at `6.9`.)_
- **7.0-7.9 (Competent / Serviceable but Safe):** Accurate and tidy, yet still reads like a conventional institutional update or predictable template with noticeable creative timidity or dated tone.
- **8.0-8.9 (Strong):** Cohesive, truthful, and genuinely usable across core touchpoints, with identifiable defects or opportunities for bolder, fresher contemporary conviction.
- **9.0-9.4 (Excellent):** Distinctive, publication-grade craft across text and visuals with clear, localized improvements still available.
- **9.5-9.8 (Exceptional):** Benchmark-level contemporary execution verified directly on files and rendered artifacts; any deduction names a concrete, fixable defect.
- **9.9-10.0 (Ready for Approval):** Verified masterwork standing comfortably beside excellent contemporary branding while remaining deeply appropriate to Global Fellowship; zero material defects, zero generic filler, zero unverified claims, and directly usable without explanation.

**Binding Reviewer Discipline:**

1. Reviewers inspect the exact same frozen revision blindly (before seeing one another's scores) and never edit repository files.
2. **Pre-Scoring Contemporary Benchmark Requirement (Areas 36, 37, 38):** Before scoring areas 36 through 38, each reviewer must inspect strong current examples of branding and communication, explicitly including excellent work **beyond Christian missions** (such as contemporary cultural institutions, editorial publications, global nonprofits, civic movements, hospitality, or modern digital brands) so the benchmark is never bounded by missions-sector conventions. Name the concrete outside benchmarks you compared against.
3. **Distinct Use of Areas 36, 37, and 38:**
   - **Area 36** evaluates the freshness and originality of the overall creative direction.
   - **Area 37** evaluates present-day relevance to the real people and contexts Global Fellowship serves today.
   - **Area 38** evaluates contemporary taste, feel, and creative confidence in the actual execution.
   - Do not give bland, tidy, or merely unobjectionable work high marks on Areas 36 through 38. Distinguish purposeful restraint from creative timidity.
4. Every score **`>= 9.5`** MUST cite the exact file path(s) and/or rendered PNG/PDF artifact(s) and check evidence actually inspected.
5. Every score **`< 9.9`** MUST name a specific, concrete, fixable defect tied to actual work (for Areas 36 through 38, name the exact choices that feel dated, generic, disconnected, or unnecessarily safe, explain their effect, and propose meaningful improvements; cosmetic tweaks alone never resolve a deeper creative weakness).
6. Every **`10.0`** MUST state the hardest check the reviewer ran that the work passed.
