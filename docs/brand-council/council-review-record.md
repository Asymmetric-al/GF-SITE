## Transition to Rubric v2.0 (`N = 38`, `/228`): Contemporary Benchmark Study & Creative Challenge

**Date:** 2026-11-21  
**Trigger:** Stakeholder feedback identified a substantive creative weakness across Candidate Version 2: the work felt **too stale, old-fashioned, and creatively safe**, requiring a truly contemporary identity and compelling present-day expression that remain deeply appropriate to Global Fellowship without diluting historic Christian convictions or altering locked foundations.

**Rubric Expansion:** Preserved all 35 areas intact and appended three distinct equal-weight areas (`N = 38`, `6 x 38 = 228` scores):

- **36. Modern & Fresh** (freshness and originality of overall creative direction)
- **37. Present-Day Audience Relevance** (meaningful resonance with the real people and contexts Global Fellowship serves today)
- **38. Contemporary Taste, Feel & Creative Confidence** (sophistication, presence, and intentional confidence in actual execution)

**Lead's Pre-Scoring Non-Missions Contemporary Benchmark Inspection (2024-2026):** Before launching Round 3 reassessment, the lead inspected strong contemporary branding and communication work well beyond Christian missions:

- **Guggenheim Global Identity System (Pentagram / Harry Pearce, 2024-2025):** Unified international constellation putting New York, Bilbao, Venice, and Abu Dhabi on equal structural footing with open-source multi-script (Latin + Arabic) typography.
- **Poetry Foundation & _Poetry_ Magazine (Pentagram / Andrea Trabucco-Campos, 2026):** Broke out of austere institutional nostalgia into confident, luminous color and made actual human words and line excerpts the primary graphic architecture.
- **ProPublica Redesign (Gretel, 2026):** Honored serious investigative credibility while discarding a pre-mobile institutional look in favor of bolder, cleaner typography, structural clarity, prominent multilingual translations, and multi-format packaging across mobile, social, newsletters, and reports.
- **Sun Day (COLLINS, 2025):** Participatory open-arc mark geometry inviting collective completion rather than top-down institutional broadcasting.
- **GoFundMe & Stack Overflow (Koto, 2026) and Biotic & Faro (Mucho, 2026):** Forward-looking human-community positioning anchored in what automation cannot replicate, paired with expressive modular typography and crisp contemporary graphic systems.
- **charity: water, Wellcome, and International Rescue Committee:** Modern global nonprofits replacing guilt tropes and dusty annual-report templates with transparent proof, confident visual presence, and empowered local agency.
